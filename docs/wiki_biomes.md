## Spirit World
- **Description:** An alternate dimension added by Witchery, mirroring the Overworld's topography.
- **Features:** Wispy Cotton generates naturally; Disturbed Cotton and Mellifluous Hunger are nightmare rewards rather than terrain features. Flowing Spirit does not generate as natural pools. Nether Warts can be obtained using Mutandis on plants here. Circle Magic does not work, and Endermen do not exist.
- **Mobs:** Nightmare (perpetually in nighttime), Demons (in demonic nightmares).
- **Notes:** Players leave most gear behind but retain baubles if Baubles is installed. Brews of Flowing Spirit can only be created here. Entering can be done via Apple of Sleeping, Brew of Sleeping, or Spirit Portal. Exiting occurs upon death, using an Icy Needle, or if the player's body is slain in the Overworld.

## Spirit Portal
- **Description:** A multiblock structure from Witchery for traveling between the Overworld and Spirit World.
- **Features:** Created with a ring of snow blocks forming a 2x2 vertical hole. Ignited by placing a Flowing Spirit source block on the bottom layer using a Brew of Flowing Spirit or Spirit Bucket.
- **Mobs:** None specific to the portal itself.
- **Notes:** Only usable after performing the Rite of Manifestation. Allows short-duration travel as a ghost.

## Torment
- **Description:** An alternate dimension added by Witchery containing multiple randomly generated mazes.
- **Features:** Six deterministic 31 x 31 perfect DFS mazes stacked at the source heights. Invisible Force forms unbreakable walls and ceilings; indestructible Torment Stone with the source Mycelium-top appearance forms floors, foundations, and portal frames. Each level has three Refilling Chests and one Torment Portal chamber.
- **Mobs:** The dimension has no ordinary natural spawn list. The second phase of the Lord of Torment encounter is moved into one shared random maze level.
- **Notes:** Summon the Lord outside Torment with the Torment Scroll obtained as a Flame Imp's fourth secret. At half health he carries nearby or tracked fighters into Torment. An interior portal returns to the first safe entry anchor 95% of the time and sends the traveller to another random level 5% of the time; defeating the linked Lord returns encounter participants, including a deferred safe return after reconnect.
