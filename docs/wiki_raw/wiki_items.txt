Placement

The Altar multi-block is complete when a red cloth recovers the whole structure.

It continuously scans its environment in a 14 block radius (cube 
shaped) and searches life to draw power from. The last altar block 
placed is the center of this range.

The most important factor is variety.

Upgrading

The 
Altar
 can be upgraded by placing specific items on its surface.

Upgrades are additive (Infinity egg and pentacle recharge bonuses
 are multiplicative), Player head (x3) and filled chalice (x2) is: 1 
(base altar) + 3 + 2 = x6 power multiplier. Only one of each type of 
item (skull/head, chalice, etc.) will have an effect.

Power Sources

The altar can draw power from a number of block types within a 14 block radius (placing an 
Arthana
 on the altar does 
NOT

 increase the range it will draw power from).
Each instance of the blocks below within the radius add to the altar's 
maximum power. However, there is a limit to the number of blocks of each
 type that can contribute to the altar's power. For example, each 
melon

 near the altar adds +4 to its maximum power, but an altar cannot 
benefit from more than 20 melons (for +80 power total); adding more 
melons after 80 will not increase the altar's maximum power. Therefore, 
the altar must be surrounded with a large variety of the blocks listed 
in the table below in order to store enough power to perform more 
demanding rituals.

Note that some small sets of blocks are considered to be 
equivalent to the altar, and the limit on their number applies to all 
combinations of those equivalent blocks. For example, 
dirt
 and 
podzol

 are considered equivalent, so an altar cannot benefit from more than 80
 total blocks of both, whether it's 40 dirt + 40 podzol, 60 dirt + 20 
podzol, 80 dirt + 0 podzol, etc.

Placement Tips

The growth stage of crops does not affect how much power they 
provide the altar; they provide just as much when they are freshly 
planted as they do when they are ready to harvest.

If you are using saplings or mushrooms to power your altar, make 
sure they cannot grow into trees or giant mushrooms, as those are 
composed of completely different blocks.

Don't bury grass, mycelium, or farmland under other blocks, or they will revert to dirt.

Make sure farmland is placed within 4 blocks of water so that it 
does not dry out and revert to dirt (but you should probably plant 
something on it anyway for even more power).

If you are using a mod that disables vanilla crops, these may still be placeable by a Dispenser.

Make sure leaf blocks are connected (either directly or through other leaves) to wood blocks to keep them from decaying.

Usage

For a kettle to work properly, a fire needs to be set under the 
kettle, the kettle needs to be filled with water from a bucket, then 
requiring the ingredients of the brew being brewed to be thrown into it,
 and finally it needs a glass bottle to be used on the kettle to bottle 
the completed brew.

The fire needs to be constantly burning while preparing a brew, 
otherwise it would not work and need to be redone. Swirl particles 
coming from the kettle shows that it is running fine, while white swirl 
particles means the brew is complete. Black smoke coming from the kettle
 with the brew turning brown signals that something has gone wrong and 
the brew has been ruined. When a brew is brown and ruined, the kettle 
must either be emptied with a glass bottle or simply refilled with more 
water.

Sometimes a brew also requires magical energy from a nearby 
Altar

 before it can finish brewing. If a brew gets bottled too early and 
there is not enough energy available for it, the brew will ruin. Black 
swirl particles means that there is not enough power and it needs more. A
 kettle needs to be about 15 blocks from an Altar in order to obtain 
power from it, and only one Altar can supply power per brew.

Use the 
Witchcraft: Brews & Infusions
 book to find all recipes for the kettle.

# Ruby Slippers
============================================================
Ruby Slippers

Name

Ruby Slippers

Source Mod

Witchery

ID Name

witchery:rubyslippers

Type

Item

Stackable

No

Durability

66

Armor

1 (
)

Tooltip

There's no place like home.
Teleport using a waystone (1 min cooldown)
Teleport to bed (30 min cooldown)

Ruby Slippers
 are worn in the foot slot and have the same durability and protection as 
Leather Boots
. They cannot be dyed but can be enchanted.

Infused players
 wearing these slippers can chant in the chat window:

/chant there's no place like home

and will be teleported back to the spawn point of that dimension they
 are in or their bed if it is in the same dimension. This works once 
every thirty minutes and costs forty percent of the total infused power 
but if done across different dimensions it costs sixty percent of the 
total infused power.

Or this can be done with a dropped 
Bound Waystone

 and will teleport the caster to the waystone location, consuming the 
waystone. Doing this is an easier way to cast the Rite of Transposition 
by bypassing the Rite entirely but does cost twenty percent of the 
player's total infused power and forty percent if teleporting to another
 dimensional.

Crafting damaged slippers with four Impregnated Leather will repair the slippers. Alternatively use an 
anvil
 and normal 
leather
.

Trivia

This item originates from "The Wizard of Oz". Near the beginning of 
the story, the protagonist Dorothy obtained a pair of ruby slippers when
 her house crushed a witch. At the end, she found that she was able to 
click her heels together and chant "There's no place like home," and 
wake up in her bed.
