Belladonna is an ingredient used in many recipes. It is harvested from a fully grown Belladonna plant that must be grown from Belladonna seeds.

To grow, Belladonna Seeds must be planted in tilled soil. It has three growth stages. Left clicking removes the entire plant, and drops Belladonna and Belladonna Seeds. The plant can then be replanted using the seed.

Blood Poppy

Witchery
 mod. It is a thorny flower that snags those who traverse over it. The blood can then be gathered with a 
Taglock Kit
 on the poppy.

Be careful! Only a 
Boline
 can safely harvest blood poppies without damaging them!

Leech Chest

Poppies
. To do so, place a Leech Chest above a 
Water

Mutating Sprig
 on the Leech Chest.

Usage

Blood Poppy has no known uses in crafting. It is used for gathering the blood of trespassers in a 
Taglock Kit
 or just whoever the witch convinces to walk through them.

Name

Demon Heart

Source Mod

Witchery

ID Name

witchery:ingredient:23

Type

Item

Stackable

Yes (64)

The 
Demon Heart
 is very evil and very powerful. One can acquire such an item only from demons themselves either through trading with a demon or killing a demon. As with most special Witchery drops, killing a demon with an 
Arthana
 would give better chances of it dropping.

Usage

Demon Hearts can be used for imbuing oneself with the 
Infernal Infusion
, or for many others rituals.

They can be placed as decoration and will provide power for nearby 
Altars
 (max 2), demon heart will also increase chance of nightmare(35% each). 

Eating a demon heart will grant demon powers temporarily to the eater, giving Speed III, Health boost, Stengh III, Regen II, Fire resistance III for 2 minutes, it also give debuffs, Hunger II, nausea and set player on fire.

it can also be fed to a 
Treefyd
 to increase its heath to 75 hearts and its damage to 8 points. 

Demon Heart can be used to create the following items:
 
Brew of Paralysis
 
Demonic Blood
 
Devils Tongue Charm
 
Refined Evil
 
Soul Sand
 
Statue of The Goddess
