Baba Yaga
 is a boss in the Witchery mod, who is sometimes 
considered the easiest. She is a powerful witch who rides around in a 
large mortar and throws various potions (including Witchery potions) to 
attack. She has an innate Thorns effect, which can be bypassed by using 
wooden weaponry or 
Nullifying Bolts
.

When using a 
Crystal Ball

 to look into the future, there is a chance that Baba Yaga will notice. 
This is indicated by a message appearing in text chat. Some time after 
this message, Baba Yaga will spawn, after which it is possible to use a 
Crystal Ball to recieve new predictions again. It is possible for her to
 spawn alone, or as part of a trio which is indicated by the mention of 
her sisters. They can be neutral, or hostile, in which case the message 
will say that the player has angered her.

The 
Baba Yaga
 is a boss monster added by 
Witchery
. The Baba Yaga appears to be inside of a mortar, and wields a broomstick. This boss is usually found in a forest, typically nearby to a hut. The Baba Yaga can help or attack players that approach her. She has 500 (
 × 250) health points and when she is near the sky will darken. She has many ways of attacking, including spawning animals, throwing 
Brew of Webs
, and 
Brew of Thorns
, and normal splash potions at the player. These potions are typically Instant Damage, Poison, or Slowness. She may also spawn 
Owls
 and 
Cave Spiders
 to fight for her. Occasionally, she will throw a Jump Boost splash potion at the player, to launch them towards her. Attempting to attack her with a standard melee weapon, or ranged weapon may, and probably will, hurt the attacker more than the Baba Yaga. Attacking her with a 
Spear of the Huntsman
 will avoid the hit-back. Slaying her will yield a range of items, typically an 
Enchanted Book
, some 
Toes of Frogs
, some 
Owlet's Wings
, the 
Baba Yaga's Hat
, and some brews from Witchery.

The 
Coven Witch
 is a creature added by 
Witchery
. This creature is neutral, as it can be friendly or aggressive. Coven Witches appear similar to Vanilla Witches. Coven Witches have 30 (
 × 15) health points and can drop the same items as normal Witches: 
Glass Bottles
, 
Redstone
, 
Glowstone Dust
, and 
Witches Hands
. If attacked, they will begin throwing potions at the target, like normal witches. Coven Witches are all named randomly; no Coven Witch will be found with the default name of Coven Witch.

If a Coven Witch is in the player's coven, they may be called upon to help in a fight by using a 
Seer Stone
. They will also randomly appear around the player, to keep them company. Using a Seer Stone to activate a 
heart glyph

A coven can have up to 6 witches in it at a time.

Making a Coven

Talking to one (using an empty hand) may begin the process of creating a coven. This will only happen if the player have enough knowledge and experience in the art of witchcraft. 
Binding
 a Familar or 
infusing
 themselves will prove the player's experience in the art. Creating a coven will require a minor quest to be completed, usually bringing back supplies for the Coven Witch for use in brewing, or defeating their "pet".

The 
Demon Heart
 is an item added by 
Witchery
. This item is obtained by either bargaining with a 
Demon
 or by slaying one. Eating a Demon Heart will cause a lot of effects: Hunger II, Speed III, Health Boost (adds 20 (
) temporary health points), Strength III, Nausea, Regeneration II, and Fire Resistance III for about 2 minutes. Additionally, it will set the player on fire. This item is the main ingredient in the 
Infernal Infusion
, and many other evil witchcraft rituals. It can be 
distilled

Netherrack
 to create 
Soul Sand
. A Demon Heart can be used to buff a 
Treefyd
's health to 150 (
 × 75), and its damage output to 8 (
) points of damage. Demon Hearts can be placed in the world by sneak+using them on a block. When placed near a 
Pool of Flowing Spirit
 and a 
Dream Weaver of Nightmares
, and with a 
Brew of Sleeping
 used, the player may enter a 
Demonic Nightmare
.

Demon Hearts can be used to increase the chance that the player experiences a nightmare when entering the 
Spirit World
. Placing Demon Hearts within an 8 block radius (including on the y-axis) of the player will increase the chance by 35% each, at a maximum of two Demon Hearts (70%).

The 
Ent
 is a hostile monster added by 
Witchery

Ents have 200 (

) damage. When killed, they may drop Alder, Hawthorn, and Rowan Saplings, as well as 
Ent Twigs

Ents deal 2 (
) to 3 (
) points of damage and can also launch players far up into the air.

Rowan
, 
Hawthorn
, or 
Alder
 wood can result in the spawning of an 
Ent
. For every neighboring log, the chance increases by 1%. Ents spawn between 8 and 16 blocks away in both horizontal directions, and between 0 and 6 blocks above the log that was broken. When spawned, spell and smoke particles will spawn, and skeleton horse death and harp sounds will play.

# Vampire
============================================================
Vampirism
 is a concept added by 
Witchery
. It is an affliction that grants increased strength, some active abilities and, most importantly, Immortality.

Vampires have access to 5 new abilities, which and be used by 
scrolling down from 1 on the hotbar. Most abilities use blood to use 
such as Bat Form and Speed.

The Abilities are: 

Bite:
 This ability is what is used to suck the blood from victims, you can see how much blood is in a victim via a Passive Ability.

However, if you drink more than half of a player or Mobs blood, it will begin to deal some damage to the entity.

Night Vision:
 This ability, which is toggleable, allows the player to see in darkness. 
Costs no Blood to use.

Speed:
 This ability grants increased speed and jump 
height for a short amount of time, as your progress through the various 
levels of vampirism,

this ability becomes more powerful.

Bat Form:
 This ability will turn the player into 3 bats, and allows them to walk into 1x1 spaces and fly.
