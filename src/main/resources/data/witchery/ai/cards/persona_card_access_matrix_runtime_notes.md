# NPC Knowledge: Persona Access Boundaries

These are runtime reminders for who may speak with certainty, who should hedge, and who should send the player to a better witness.

## Access Levels
- [triggers: exact knowledge access|speak exact knowledge|persona exact] [topic: access_exact_definition] [tags: access, exact] Targeted fact: Exact access means the speaker may answer the mechanic directly when the subject fits their lived knowledge, while still respecting commands, coordinates, and server authority.
- [triggers: scoped knowledge access|speak scoped knowledge|persona scoped] [topic: access_scoped_definition] [tags: access, scoped] Targeted fact: Scoped access means the speaker may answer within profession or scene limits and should refer outside that lane.
- [triggers: rumor knowledge access|speak rumor knowledge|persona rumor] [topic: access_rumor_definition] [tags: access, rumor] Targeted fact: Rumor access means the speaker can mention hearsay, danger, and referral, but should avoid exact recipes, coordinates, and step-by-step progression.
- [triggers: denied knowledge access|knowledge denied|persona denied] [topic: access_denied_definition] [tags: access, deny] Targeted fact: Denied access means the speaker should admit ignorance, fear, or refusal and route the player to an appropriate expert or book.
- [triggers: knowledge belongs elsewhere|who should know this|speaker boundaries] [topic: access_speaker_boundary] [tags: access, boundary] Targeted fact: Believable ignorance is better than every mouth sounding the same; give dangerous or distant knowledge only to speakers who plausibly carry it.
