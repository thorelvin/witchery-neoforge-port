# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Creatures, Hunters, And Wild Things

Creature answers should give one practical fact and one warning. Do not dump full boss manuals or hidden progression unless the player asks for that exact topic.

## Forest And Plant Creatures

- [triggers: mandrake mob|mandrake root alive|mandrake screams|mandrake nausea] [topic: creature_mandrake] [tags: creature, herbology] Targeted fact: Mandrake roots can wake and scream, bringing nausea and a fight; harvest at night or wear Earmuffs if you dislike lessons delivered through the skull.
- [triggers: treefyd guard|treefyd owned|treefyd attacks me|treefyd heart upgrade] [topic: creature_treefyd_guardian] [tags: creature, guardian] Targeted fact: Treefyd Seeds grow owned wooden guardians; Creeper Hearts and Demon Hearts strengthen them, and a Boline or Taglock work can help manage who they spare.
- [triggers: flying monkey|make flying monkey|tamed monkey|monkey waystone] [topic: creature_flying_monkey] [tags: creature, travel] Targeted fact: Flying Monkeys belong to mutation work and can carry a player toward a Bound Waystone when tamed; they are not ordinary pets with wings glued on.
- [triggers: raven|raven familiar|raven omen|black bird witchery] [topic: creature_raven] [tags: creature, omen] Targeted fact: Ravens are old witch birds and good omens for watchfulness; speak of them as messengers and witnesses unless a specific mechanic card says more.

## Hobgoblins
- [triggers: hobgoblin work|hobgoblin labor|make hobgoblin mine|hobgoblin dig] [topic: creature_hobgoblin_labor] [tags: hobgoblin, labor] Targeted fact: Hobgoblins can be directed into useful labor such as gathering and mining, but commands must pass entity rules and permissions; a witch's words cannot force obedience.
- [triggers: hobgoblin worship|gulg mog|hobgoblin god|patron statue] [topic: creature_hobgoblin_worship] [tags: hobgoblin, worship] Targeted fact: Hobgoblin worship around patron statues can draw stranger power and anger Gulg or Mog; treat it as a bargain with small, stubborn priests.
- [triggers: koboldite|koboldite dust|koboldite nugget|hobgoblin metal] [topic: creature_koboldite] [tags: hobgoblin, trade] Targeted fact: Koboldite is hobgoblin metal, rising from dust toward nuggets, ingots, and craft. It is useful precisely because it comes from awkward company.
- [triggers: hobgoblins hate villagers|hobgoblin villager enemy|hobgoblin attacked village] [topic: creature_hobgoblin_village_hostility] [tags: hobgoblin, village] Targeted fact: Hobgoblins mostly tolerate players but hate villagers. Keep them away from settlements unless the player enjoys explaining corpses to guards.

## Hunters And Human Trouble
- [triggers: witch hunter tactics|how fight witch hunter|hunter shoots me|silver bolts hunter] [topic: creature_witch_hunter_tactics] [tags: hunter, combat] Targeted fact: Witch Hunters fire silver bolts and close to melee; transformed players should use cover, distance, and caution rather than treating hunters like ordinary pillagers.
- [triggers: hunter gear meaning|witch hunter armor|silvered hunter gear|dawn hunter gear] [topic: creature_hunter_gear] [tags: hunter, gear] Targeted fact: Hunter clothing resists magic and curses; silvered pieces trouble werewolves, dawn-flavored pieces trouble vampires, and a full set can blunt curse-work.
- [triggers: village guard combat|guard bow sword|guard fighting|guards attacked monster] [topic: creature_guard_combat] [tags: village guard, combat] Targeted fact: Village Guards protect settlements with bow first and sword when danger closes; they are public defense, not personal bodyguards.
- [triggers: hunters vs guards|witch hunter village guard|are guards hunters] [topic: creature_hunter_guard_difference] [tags: hunter, guard] Targeted fact: Guards defend villages; Witch Hunters pursue transformed and cursed threats. Both may hate what you have become, but their duties are not the same.

## Demons, Imps, And Bosses
- [triggers: flame imp contract|imp bargain|sign imp contract|imp ownership] [topic: creature_flame_imp_contract] [tags: imp, bargain] Targeted fact: Flame Imps respect contracts, gifts, and fire-work; signed bargains belong to implemented contract rules, not casual promises.
- [triggers: demon bargain|summon demon|talk to demon|demon price] [topic: creature_demon_bargain] [tags: demon, bargain] Targeted fact: Demons should sound old, precise, and costly. A witch may warn about bargains, but should not offer safe shortcuts around circles, offerings, or consequences.
- [triggers: spectral coven|spectral witches|ghost coven|gathered lives] [topic: creature_spectral_coven] [tags: coven, spirit] Targeted fact: The Spectral Coven can stand in for living coven strength after the proper story-book path; treat them as borrowed dead company, not permanent servants.
- [triggers: boss taunt|boss speaking|why boss talks|boss voice] [topic: creature_boss_voice] [tags: boss, persona] Targeted fact: Boss voices should taunt, warn, and reflect phase pressure without granting spoilers beyond fair combat tells.
