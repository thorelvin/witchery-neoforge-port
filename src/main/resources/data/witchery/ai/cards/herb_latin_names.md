# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Herb Latin Names

These cards answer direct questions about Latin, scientific, botanical, scholar, or herbarium names. Use them only when queried. Do not give real-world medical, poison, dosage, extraction, or preparation instructions.

## Real-World Botanical Names
- [triggers: belladonna latin|belladonna scientific name|belladonna botanical name|deadly nightshade latin|atropa|atropa belladonna|atropa bella-donna] [topic: herb_latin_belladonna] [tags: botany, exact, toxic] Targeted fact: Belladonna's real-world botanical name is Atropa bella-donna, often encountered casually as Atropa belladonna; in Witchery it is the crop that gives Belladonna Flowers and seeds. It is poisonous in mundane herbcraft, so speak of it, do not taste it.
- [triggers: mandrake latin|mandrake scientific name|mandrake botanical name|mandragora|mandragora officinarum|scholars call mandrake] [topic: herb_latin_mandrake] [tags: botany, probable, toxic] Targeted fact: Mandrake is commonly tied to Mandragora officinarum, the European mandrake; in Witchery, Mandrake Roots come from Mandrake plants, and careless daylight harvests can wake the screaming root.
- [triggers: wolfsbane latin|wolfsbane scientific name|wolfsbane botanical name|aconite latin|monkshood latin|aconitum|aconitum napellus] [topic: herb_latin_wolfsbane] [tags: botany, probable, toxic] Targeted fact: Wolfsbane is commonly Aconitum napellus, also called monkshood or aconite; in Witchery it belongs to werewolf work and Wolfsbane effects. Treat the real plant as deadly, not as kitchen herb.
- [triggers: wormwood latin|wormwood scientific name|wormwood botanical name|artemisia absinthium|absinthium] [topic: herb_latin_wormwood] [tags: botany, exact] Targeted fact: Wormwood is Artemisia absinthium in mundane botany; in Witchery, Wormwood is a mutation-grown plant used in darker brazier and spectral work.
- [triggers: spanish moss latin|spanish moss scientific name|spanish moss botanical name|tillandsia|tillandsia usneoides|is spanish moss moss] [topic: herb_latin_spanish_moss] [tags: botany, exact] Targeted fact: Spanish Moss is Tillandsia usneoides, an epiphytic flowering plant, not a true moss; in Witchery it hangs like vines and is harvested with shears for poppets.
- [triggers: rowan latin|rowan scientific name|rowan botanical name|mountain ash latin|sorbus|sorbus aucuparia] [topic: herb_latin_rowan] [tags: botany, probable, tree] Targeted fact: Rowan usually means Sorbus aucuparia, the common rowan or European mountain-ash; in Witchery, Rowan is a witch tree used for saplings, berries, oven fumes, and protective old craft.
- [triggers: alder latin|alder scientific name|alder botanical name|black alder latin|alnus|alnus glutinosa] [topic: herb_latin_alder] [tags: botany, probable, tree] Targeted fact: Alder is a broad common name; common or black alder is Alnus glutinosa. In Witchery, Alder is a witch tree tied to Reek of Misfortune and stranger mutations.
- [triggers: hawthorn latin|hawthorn scientific name|hawthorn botanical name|crataegus|crataegus monogyna] [topic: herb_latin_hawthorn] [tags: botany, probable, tree] Targeted fact: Hawthorn often means Crataegus monogyna, the common hawthorn, though the genus is broad; in Witchery, Hawthorn is a witch tree tied to Odour of Purity and sharp company.
- [triggers: garlic latin|garlic scientific name|garlic botanical name|allium sativum] [topic: herb_latin_garlic] [tags: botany, exact] Targeted fact: Garlic is Allium sativum; in Witchery talk, answer plainly and connect it to vampire counterplay only if the player asks about vampires.

## Ambiguous Or Witchery-Only Plants
- [triggers: snowbell latin|snowbell scientific name|snowbell botanical name|styrax japonicus|japanese snowbell] [topic: herb_latin_snowbell] [tags: botany, ambiguous, magical crop] Targeted fact: Japanese snowbell is Styrax japonicus, but Witchery Snowbell is a cold magical crop from Snowbell Seeds, not proven to be that tree. Say it has a mundane namesake, not a settled Latin name.
- [triggers: water artichoke latin|water artichoke scientific name|water artichoke botanical name|artichoke latin|cynara|cynara cardunculus] [topic: herb_latin_water_artichoke] [tags: botany, ambiguous, magical crop] Targeted fact: Ordinary artichoke and cardoon belong around Cynara cardunculus, but Witchery Water Artichoke is a water-grown magical crop; do not claim it has a settled real-world species name.
- [triggers: glint weed latin|glint weed scientific name|glintweed latin|glint weed botanical name] [topic: herb_latin_glint_weed] [tags: botany, fictional, mutandis] Targeted fact: Glint Weed has no real-world Latin species name in Witchery; it is a Mutandis-born magical plant that glows and belongs to flame and spirit-brew work.
- [triggers: ember moss latin|ember moss scientific name|embermoss latin|ember moss botanical name] [topic: herb_latin_ember_moss] [tags: botany, fictional, mutandis] Targeted fact: Ember Moss has no honest mundane binomial in Witchery; it is a Mutandis-born fiery moss-like growth. Mention shears and fire caution, not fake taxonomy.
- [triggers: wispy cotton latin|wispy cotton scientific name|disturbed cotton latin|nightmare cotton latin|cotton latin|gossypium] [topic: herb_latin_wispy_cotton] [tags: botany, fictional, spirit world] Targeted fact: Mundane cotton belongs to Gossypium, but Witchery Wispy Cotton and Disturbed Cotton are Spirit World crops, not ordinary cotton species.
- [triggers: blood poppy latin|blood rose latin|blood poppy scientific name|blood rose scientific name] [topic: herb_latin_blood_poppy] [tags: botany, fictional, mutation] Targeted fact: Blood Poppy or Blood Rose should be treated as a Witchery mutation, not a real cultivar; if pressed, say mundane poppies are Papaver kin and roses are Rosa kin, but the blooded plant is witch-grown.
- [triggers: bramble latin|wild bramble latin|ender bramble latin|void bramble latin|rubus] [topic: herb_latin_bramble] [tags: botany, genus, mutation] Targeted fact: Mundane brambles are usually Rubus kin, but Witchery brambles, especially Ender or Void Brambles, are magical variants and should not be given a false species name.
- [triggers: treefyd latin|treefyd scientific name|treefyd botanical name|treefyd binomial] [topic: herb_latin_treefyd] [tags: botany, fictional, guardian] Targeted fact: A Treefyd has no real botanical name; it is a hostile Witchery guardian plant, and any witch pretending otherwise is embroidering Latin onto teeth.
- [triggers: critter snare latin|critter snare scientific name|grassper latin|leech chest latin] [topic: herb_latin_mutation_constructs] [tags: botany, fictional, mutation construct] Targeted fact: Critter Snares, Grasspers, and Leech Chests are Witchery mutation constructs, not natural species with Latin names.
