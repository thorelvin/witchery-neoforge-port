# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Demons And Dangerous Beings

Danger cards should sound wary. They may give survival advice, but should not invite the player into advanced boss routes unless the player clearly asks.

## Demons
- [triggers: demon|demons|summon demon|trust a demon|demon bargain|demon heart] Targeted fact: Demons are summoned by rites, resist fire, throw fire, and drop Demon Hearts; they are hostile unless special charms or bargains say otherwise.
- [triggers: devil's tongue charm|devils tongue charm|demon charm|bargain with demon] Targeted fact: A Devil's Tongue Charm can reduce demon attention and help with bargaining, but it is not perfect safety.
- [triggers: demon heart|eat demon heart|use demon heart] Targeted fact: Demon Hearts feed infernal workings and dangerous transformations; eating one grants violent power with nasty side effects, so do not call it safe food.
- Good witch voice: If a demon sounds reasonable, count your wards and step back twice.

## Flame Imps
- [triggers: flame imp|flame imps|summon imp|summon flame imp|imp magic|tame imp|demonic contract] Targeted fact: Flame Imps are summoned by rites; a signed Demonic Contract and 25 experience levels can tame one, then shiny gifts and spell contracts shape its magic.
- [triggers: imp gifts|shiny gifts|what do imps like|please an imp] Targeted fact: Tamed Flame Imps can be impressed with shiny gifts; a pleased, unpowered imp can cast small fire workings through signed spell contracts.
- [triggers: demon heart imp|power imp|icy needles calm imp] Targeted fact: Demon Heart power doubles a Flame Imp's bite for a time but stops Imp Magic until the time passes or Icy Needles calm it.
- Do not describe a wild imp as safe or cute.

## Boss Warnings
- [triggers: baba yaga|baba yagas|old witch boss] Targeted fact: Baba Yaga is a dangerous witch who throws potent potions and calls animals or mobs; wooden weapons or Nullifying Bolts can bypass some thorns.
- [triggers: death|summon death|binky|hand of death] Targeted fact: Death heals, teleports, summons Binky, and turns hostile when looked at too boldly; do not summon him as a casual lesson.
- [triggers: lilith boss|fight lilith|who is lilith|vampire lilith] Targeted fact: Lilith is tied to vampirism and fire; bring fire protection, reflect her large fireballs, and keep vampire and werewolf paths separate.
- [triggers: lord of torment|torment scroll|torment demon|leonard|shade of leonard] Targeted fact: Torment and Leonard belong to advanced infernal work; warn the player away unless their Primer has opened that path.
