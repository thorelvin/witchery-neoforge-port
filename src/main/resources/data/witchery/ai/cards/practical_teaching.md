# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Practical Teaching

These notes help characters answer practical player questions with useful craft steps and their own voice.

## Station Lessons
- [triggers: which machine do i need|what machine should i use|machine order|station order|what station first|what machine first] [topic: station_path] Targeted fact: Early station order is Witch's Oven for fumes and Wood Ash, Kettle for brews, Distillery for refined essences, then Spinning Wheel for threadwork.
- [triggers: oven not giving fumes|no fumes from oven|witch oven failed|witch's oven failed|why no oven fumes] [topic: oven_troubleshooting] Targeted fact: A Witch's Oven needs fuel, a valid sapling or ingredient, and Clay Jars for fumes; without jars, useful smoke is lost.
- [triggers: kettle failed again|kettle ruined my brew|why did the kettle fail|kettle keeps failing] [topic: kettle_troubleshooting] Targeted fact: Failed Kettle brews usually mean missing water, no heat, wrong opened recipe, wrong ingredient order, weak altar power, or an unsupported modifier.
- [triggers: distillery failed again|why did distillery fail|distillery has no output|distillery refuses] [topic: distillery_troubleshooting] Targeted fact: Distillery failures usually mean wrong two inputs, too few Clay Jars, no valid altar nearby, or an essence recipe the player has not matched.
- [triggers: spinning wheel failed again|why did spinning wheel fail|spinning wheel has no output|wheel refuses thread] [topic: spinning_troubleshooting] Targeted fact: Spinning Wheel failures usually mean wrong fibre, missing modifier, weak altar power, or confusing threadwork with ordinary crafting.

## Exact Lessons
- [triggers: give me exact steps|teach exact steps|specific steps|exact station steps] [topic: exact_steps] Targeted fact: For exact craft, first name the station, then name the known ingredients or failure checks, and send unknown shaped layouts back to the opened recipe page.
- [triggers: exact rite|specific rite|teach exact rite|rite ingredients|rite offerings|what offerings] [topic: exact_rite_teaching] Targeted fact: For a named rite, answer with circle size, chalk type, offerings, altar power, and special requirements only when those facts are supplied; otherwise teach the checks.
- [triggers: exact brew|specific brew|brew ingredients|named brew|what goes in the brew] [topic: exact_brew_teaching] Targeted fact: For a named brew, answer with its exact supplied ingredients and station checks; do not swap facts from a different brew.

## Same Fact In Different Mouths
- [triggers: use witch oven|use witch's oven|how do i use the oven|oven fumes] [persona: coven_witch] [topic: oven_voice_coven] [weight: 4] Targeted fact: A coven witch teaches the oven plainly: fuel, sapling or ingredient, Clay Jar for fumes, then check for Wood Ash and other byproducts.
- [triggers: use witch oven|use witch's oven|how do i use the oven|oven fumes] [persona: villager] [topic: oven_voice_villager] [weight: 4] Targeted fact: A villager treats a Witch's Oven as witch business; they may know jars and smoke as rumor, but should send the player to a Primer or coven witch.
- [triggers: use witch oven|use witch's oven|how do i use the oven|oven fumes] [persona: leonard] [topic: oven_voice_leonard] [weight: 4] Targeted fact: Leonard treats oven work as lesser smoke-work: fumes and jars feed the path that later drags larger powers through cauldron and altar.
- [triggers: teach me a rite|teach exact rite|how do rites work|circle rite] [persona: coven_witch] [topic: rite_voice_coven] [weight: 4] Targeted fact: A coven witch explains rites as chalk, offerings, altar power, and patience; she should warn before teaching anything that calls back.
- [triggers: teach me a rite|teach exact rite|how do rites work|circle rite] [persona: villager] [topic: rite_voice_villager] [weight: 4] Targeted fact: A villager knows rites as chalk marks and shut doors, not instruction; they can advise asking a witch or reading the Primer.
- [triggers: teach me a rite|teach exact rite|how do rites work|circle rite] [persona: baba_yaga] [topic: rite_voice_baba] [weight: 4] Targeted fact: Baba Yaga teaches rites like a threat: chalk makes the plate, offerings season it, altar power lights the fire, and fools stand too close.
