# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Settlements, Hunters, And Hobgoblins

Settlement cards should make the world feel watchful. Give practical warning first, then a witchy edge.

## Witch Hunters
- [triggers: why are witch hunters after me|hunters after me|witch hunters after me|witch hunter enemy|why are witch hunters after transformed folk|witch hunters after transformed folk|hunters after transformed folk|transformed folk] Targeted fact: Witch Hunters pursue vampires and werewolves as a profession; they shoot silver bolts, then close to melee if you let them.
- [triggers: witch hunter gear|hunter clothes|hunter clothing|hunter armor|hunter armour] Targeted fact: Hunter clothing helps against magic, curses, vampires, and werewolves; silvered pieces punish werewolves, and dawn pieces trouble vampires.
- [triggers: silver damage|silver hurts werewolves|silver hurts vampires|silver against werewolves|silver against vampires|what does silver do to werewolves|silver do to werewolves|silver and werewolves|silver werewolves] Targeted fact: Silver weapons and Silver Bolts add hard bonus damage to vampires and werewolves; Wolfmen suffer worse than most.
- [triggers: silvered hunter armor|silvered hunter armour|silvered armor|silvered armour] Targeted fact: Silvered hunter armor reduces werewolf harm and bites back for 1 damage when a werewolf strikes the wearer; cruel cloth, that.
- [triggers: hunter curse protection|witch hunter curse protection|full hunter set curses|curse hunter gear] Targeted fact: A full Hunter set can block curses; do not trust it as kindly magic, but respect the stitching.

## Villages And Guards
- [triggers: village guard|village guards|guards shouting|guards angry|why guards angry|guard shouted at me|village guard shouted|a village guard shouted at me|what did i do guard] Targeted fact: Village guards attack monsters, vampires, and werewolves; they shout what they see, then swap bow for sword if trouble gets close.
- [triggers: guards hate vampires|guards hate werewolves|village guard vampire|village guard werewolf] Targeted fact: A transformed player looks like trouble to village guards; silver, stakes, and distance are the language guards understand.
- [triggers: apothecary villager|village apothecary|apothecary trade|potion trade villager|are apothecaries coven witches|apothecaries coven witches|apothecary coven witch] Targeted fact: Apothecaries are village potion trade and village building craft, not coven witches; buy supplies, but do not expect circle secrets.
- [triggers: village walls|walled village|village wall|witchery walls] Targeted fact: Walled villages are settlement defense; a wall means folk expect trouble, not that they welcome witchcraft.

## Hobgoblins And Koboldite
- [triggers: hobgoblin trade|hobgoblin trades|trade with hobgoblin|koboldite trade] Targeted fact: Hobgoblin trade unlocks in five steps: coal into dust, dust and gold into nuggets, then upward toward useful Koboldite.
- [triggers: hobgoblin hut|where find hobgoblin|find hobgoblins|hobgoblin woodland] Targeted fact: Hobgoblins live in woodland huts, trade in Koboldite, mostly tolerate players, and hate villagers.
- [triggers: hobgoblin patron|statue of hobgoblin patron|patron statue|gulg mog] Targeted fact: The Hobgoblin Patron statue draws worship power from nearby hobgoblins; use may anger Gulg or Mog and make the hobgoblins hostile.
- [triggers: koboldite pickaxe recipe|make koboldite pickaxe|craft koboldite pickaxe] Targeted fact: Koboldite Pickaxe is crafted from Koboldite Ingots and Sticks; hobgoblin metal makes stranger tools than iron.
- [triggers: twisting band recipe|make twisting band|craft twisting band] Targeted fact: Twisting Band is crafted from Koboldite Ingots and an Attuned Stone Charged; that is hobgoblin-adjacent craft, not trinket work.
