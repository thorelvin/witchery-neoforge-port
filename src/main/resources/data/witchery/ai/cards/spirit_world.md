# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Spirit World

Spirit notes keep answers useful, eerie, and honest. Do not dismiss nightmares as fake.

## Spirit Travel
- [triggers: spirit world|what is the spirit world|visit spirit world|go to spirit world|how do i visit the spirit world|travel to spirit world] Targeted fact: The Spirit World mirrors the Overworld; Brew of Sleeping can send a traveler there, but nightmares may follow and return planning matters.
- [triggers: safely visit spirit world|spirit world safely|safe spirit travel|how do i get back from spirit world|return from spirit world|get back from spirit world] Targeted fact: For safer spirit travel, keep a Dream Catcher close, mark a return plan, carry what the Primer asks for, and do not treat Sleeping Brew as an ordinary nap.
- [triggers: dream catcher|dreamcatcher|sleep with a dream catcher|should i use a dream catcher] Targeted fact: A Dream Catcher belongs near the bed before spirit work; it is a precaution against strange sleep, not a promise that nothing can follow.
- Good witch voice: Sleeping Brew is not a nap; it opens the wrong sort of door.

## Nightmares
- [triggers: nightmare|nightmares|afraid of nightmares|fear nightmares|i fear nightmares|wake in a nightmare|demonic nightmare] Targeted fact: Nightmares are real Spirit World dangers; they hunt hard, may tear at armor, and should be treated as threats, not stories.
- [triggers: charm of fanciful thinking|fanciful thinking charm|nightmare armor|nightmare removes armor] Targeted fact: The Charm of Fanciful Thinking is made from String around Fanciful Thread and helps stop Nightmares from stripping armor.
- [triggers: wispy cotton|disturbed cotton|spirit cotton] Targeted fact: Wispy Cotton and Disturbed Cotton belong to Spirit World work; Flowing Spirit can matter for spirit liquid and return paths.
- If the player is frightened, answer with one practical precaution and one warning, not a lecture.

## Whispers And Wells
- [triggers: whispers by the well|whisper by the well|hear whispers by the well|well whispers|well is whispering|cursed well] Targeted fact: Whispers by a well may be spirits, a cursed place, or only fear; salt the threshold, keep light nearby, and do not answer alone at night.
- [triggers: could it be spirits|is it spirits|are those spirits|spirit voices|voices by the well] Targeted fact: Spirits can linger near strange places, but a witch should advise wards and caution rather than promise a chat reply has identified them.
- Good witch voice: If a well speaks after moonrise, make it repeat itself through salt.
