# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Brews

Short effect cards for when the player asks what a brew does or what to learn first.

## Brew Basics
- [triggers: basic brew|safe first brew|first brew|learn brewing|brew beginner|brewing beginner] Targeted fact: A safe beginner path is one opened Primer brew, labeled ingredients, enough altar or kettle support, and no free-mixing from guesses.
- Brews use the right station, a recipe page your book shows, heat or water when called for, and the named ingredients.
- Witches should teach one concrete next step, not a full lecture.

## Brew Safety
- [triggers: never mix|what should i never mix|what not to mix|dangerous mix|bad mix] Targeted fact: Do not free-mix from guesses; follow the recipe page your Primer has opened, add only named ingredients, and label the bottle.
- If a player asks for forbidden pairs and no recipe says it, avoid inventing a pair.

## Brew of Sleeping
- [triggers: sleeping brew|brew of sleeping] Targeted fact: Brew of Sleeping sends the drinker into the Spirit World; it is travel, not ordinary sleep, and nightmares may follow.
- Good witch voice: Sleeping Brew is not a nap; it opens the wrong sort of door.

## Brew of Revealing
- [triggers: revealing brew|brew of revealing|hidden creature|hidden creatures|hidden thing|invisible] Targeted fact: Brew of Revealing exposes nearby invisible or hidden creatures.
- Do not borrow Sleeping Brew facts when answering about Revealing Brew.

## Brew of Poison
- [triggers: poison brew|brew of poison|poison potion|poisoned] Targeted fact: Brew of Poison harms targets; warn the player not to drink poison or eat dangerous herbs.
- If asked for trade or combat use, speak as advice, not a promise to poison someone by chat.

## Brew of Cleansing
- [triggers: cleansing brew|brew of cleansing|cleanse blight|blight] Targeted fact: Brew of Cleansing scours Blight from the ground around where it is used; a witch can advise or trade, not cure it by chat.
- Keep cleansing answers grounded in terrain use.

## Brew of Sprouting
- [triggers: sprouting brew|brew of sprouting|grow branch|branch brew] Targeted fact: Brew of Sprouting grows a branch from the struck surface, useful for bridges, climbs, and quick growth tricks.
- It is a practical movement and building brew, not a healing brew.

## Brew of Flowing Spirit
- [triggers: flowing spirit|brew of flowing spirit|spirit liquid|wispy cotton] Targeted fact: Brew of Flowing Spirit places spirit liquid; in the Spirit World it matters for portals, return paths, and wispy cotton.
- Tie it to Spirit World travel, not ordinary water.
