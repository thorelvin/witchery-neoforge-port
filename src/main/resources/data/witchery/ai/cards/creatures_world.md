# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Creatures And The Wider World

Creature cards should answer ordinary curiosity without dumping boss manuals. Keep one useful fact and one warning.

## Forest Creatures
- [triggers: hobgoblin|hobgoblins|koboldite|koboldite dust|gulg|mog] Targeted fact: Hobgoblins are forest creatures, neutral to players but hostile to villagers; they can be led to gather items or dig for Koboldite Dust, and their god-worship belongs to stranger craft.
- [triggers: ent|ents|tree spirit|angry tree|witch tree attacks|rowan tree attacks|alder tree attacks|hawthorn tree attacks] Targeted fact: Ents may rise when Rowan, Alder, or Hawthorn trees are felled carelessly; axes and Huntsman weapons bite them better than panic does.
- [triggers: mandrake screams|mandrake scream|harvest mandrake|mandrake at night|earmuffs mandrake] Targeted fact: Mandrake roots can wake and scream when harvested, causing nausea; night harvesting lowers the chance, and Earmuffs help.
- Good witch voice: The forest forgives slowly and keeps witnesses.

## Made Guardians
- [triggers: treefyd|treefyd seed|treefyd seeds|plant guardian|wooden guardian] Targeted fact: Treefyd Seeds make owned wooden guardians when planted on grass; Creeper Hearts and Demon Hearts strengthen them, and a Boline can manage wandering.
- [triggers: treefyd taglock|treefyd ignore|teach treefyd|treefyd whitelist] Targeted fact: A filled Taglock Kit can teach an owned Treefyd to ignore a named player or one exact creature; use it carefully so the guardian knows who not to bite.
- [triggers: flying monkey|flying monkeys|monkey carry|waystone monkey] Targeted fact: Flying Monkeys are made from wolf-and-owl mutation work; tamed ones can carry a player toward a Bound Waystone.

## Witch Hunters And Villages
- [triggers: witch hunter|witch hunters|hunters after me|hunter armor] Targeted fact: Witch Hunters track vampires and werewolves, shoot silver bolts, and close to melee; do not answer them with bravado when distance will do.
- [triggers: villagers hate witches|villagers fear witches|village angry|village afraid|hobgoblin villagers] Targeted fact: Villagers fear curses, vampires, werewolves, demons, and brewing mishaps; hobgoblins are hostile to villagers, and curses near villages bring attention.
- [triggers: apothecary|village apothecary|witchery villager|villager trade potions] Targeted fact: Apothecary villagers belong to practical potion trade and village infrastructure; treat them as mundane help, not coven witches.
