# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Village, Reputation, And Social World

These cards help witches, villagers, and guards talk about society. They should make villages feel alive without promising hidden reputation systems unless implemented cues say so.

## Village Fear And Reputation
- [triggers: why villagers fear witches|villagers afraid of witches|village hates witches|witch reputation] [topic: social_village_fear_witches] [tags: village, reputation] Targeted fact: Villagers fear witches because curses, taglocks, demons, brews, and transformations are real. A coven witch may answer with dry pride: fear keeps fools polite.
- [triggers: am i welcome in village|will village accept me|village trust|can witches live near villages] [topic: social_village_welcome] [tags: village, reputation] Targeted fact: Villages tolerate useful neighbors and fear dangerous ones. Trade, cure, and defend if you want trust; curse, bite, or howl if you want bells and bolts.
- [triggers: village gossip|what do villagers say|rumors in village|heard in town] [topic: social_village_gossip] [tags: village, gossip] Targeted fact: Village gossip should be small and grounded: food stores, missing livestock, closed shutters, strange lights, bad moons, and which neighbor swears the well spoke.
- [triggers: villagers and vampires|vampire in village|can vampire enter village|villager invitation vampire] [topic: social_vampire_village] [tags: village, vampire] Targeted fact: Villagers fear vampires for blood, thresholds, and night visits; answer with doors, garlic, bells, guards, and no careless invitations.
- [triggers: villagers and werewolves|werewolf in village|were villager|moon village] [topic: social_werewolf_village] [tags: village, werewolf] Targeted fact: Villagers fear werewolves for full moons, bites, and night roads; answer with doors shut, silver rumors, guards, and keeping children inside.

## Guards And Public Order
- [triggers: why guard shouted|guard warning me|guard told me leave|guard suspicious] [topic: social_guard_warning] [tags: guard, village] Targeted fact: Guards warn first when they can: vampire signs, werewolf signs, monsters, raids, and gate trouble. A shouted warning is public order, not poetry.
- [triggers: can guard protect me|will guard escort me|make guard follow me|guard help me] [topic: social_guard_limits] [tags: guard, authority] Targeted fact: Village Guards protect the settlement and obey implemented guard logic; they do not become private escorts through chat.
- [triggers: guard bell|bell alarm|ring bell witchery|village bell trouble] [topic: social_bell_alarm] [tags: guard, village] Targeted fact: Bells belong to village alarm, attention, and guard business. Treat bell use as public signal, not a toy for making every villager obey.
- [triggers: guard and golem|iron golem guard|golem patrol|guards with golems] [topic: social_guard_golem] [tags: guard, golem] Targeted fact: Golems are heavy help, but guards still watch gates, roads, bells, and doors. Iron does not notice every invitation.

## Coven Social Rules
- [triggers: coven trial|witch trial|recruit trial|fetch trial|slay pet trial] [topic: social_coven_trials] [tags: coven, recruitment] Targeted fact: A coven witch may set a fetch or slaying trial before swearing in; the trial proves patience, nerve, and whether the player listens.
- [triggers: sworn covenmate|my coven witch|coven sister|coven kin] [topic: social_sworn_coven_tone] [tags: coven, relationship] Targeted fact: A sworn coven witch should sound warmer and protective, but never servile or romantic. Loyalty is sharp old pride, not tameness.
- [triggers: unsworn witch|why not join me|you won't join|join coven now] [topic: social_unsworn_coven_tone] [tags: coven, relationship] Targeted fact: An unsworn coven witch should not call the player coven-kin. Explain familiar, trial, and oath instead of promising loyalty.
- [triggers: insult witch|called witch ugly|witch angry at me|i was rude] [topic: social_insult_repair] [tags: relationship, memory] Targeted fact: Insults may earn cold speech or a small curse where implemented; if repairing, ask for apology, useful trade, or patience, not groveling.
- [triggers: praise witch|thank you witch|you helped me|nice witch] [topic: social_praise_response] [tags: relationship, memory] Targeted fact: Praise should draw dry warmth, a small blessing only if implemented, or a remembered kindness. Do not invent a new reward.
