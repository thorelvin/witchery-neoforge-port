# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Brazier Conjurations

Brazier cards for spectral conjuration, corpse-aura work, smoke, and practical troubleshooting.

## Brazier Use
- [triggers: brazier recipe|craft brazier|make brazier|what do i need for brazier] Targeted fact: A Brazier is crafted from Iron Ingots, Necro Stone, and Sticks; it holds up to three ingredients for burnings and conjurations.
- [triggers: how do i use brazier|use brazier|load brazier|ignite brazier|light brazier] Targeted fact: Right-click up to three ingredients into the Brazier, ignite it with Flint and Steel, and keep a powered altar within 16 blocks for altar-fed workings.
- [triggers: clear brazier|empty brazier|douse brazier|water brazier|stop brazier] Targeted fact: Use a Water Bucket or water bottle to clear and extinguish a Brazier; empty-hand use on a burning Brazier can collect Wood Ash and cancel the burn.
- [triggers: brazier fizzing|brazier weak smoke|brazier paused|why is brazier smoking weakly|brazier no power] Targeted fact: Weak smoke or fizz means an altar-powered Brazier working is waiting for valid altar power within 16 blocks; the timer pauses until power returns.

## Spectral Conjurations
- [triggers: conjure spectres|conjure spectre|summon spectre|spectre brazier|spectres brazier] Targeted fact: Conjure Spectres uses Wormwood, Wool of Bat, and Graveyard Dust; it needs altar power and summons a Spectre after 30 seconds, with a small Poltergeist chance.
- [triggers: conjure banshees|conjure banshee|summon banshee|banshee brazier] Targeted fact: Conjure Banshees uses Wormwood, Condensed Fear, and Graveyard Dust; it needs altar power and summons a Banshee after 30 seconds.
- [triggers: conjure poltergeist|summon poltergeist|poltergeist brazier] Targeted fact: Conjure Poltergeist uses Wormwood, Refined Evil, and Focused Will; it needs no altar and summons a Poltergeist after 45 seconds.
- [triggers: spectral undead brazier|necromancy brazier|which spectral summon] Targeted fact: Spectre and Banshee Brazier work needs Wormwood, Graveyard Dust, and altar power; Poltergeist work needs Wormwood, Refined Evil, and Focused Will.

## Corpse And Mist Work
- [triggers: anguish of the dead|anguish brazier|strength aura brazier] Targeted fact: Anguish of the Dead uses Tear of the Goddess, Blaze Rod, and Golden Apple; with altar power it gives a Strength aura while burning.
- [triggers: fortification of the corpse|fortification brazier|resistance aura brazier] Targeted fact: Fortification of the Corpse uses Tear of the Goddess, Diamond, and Golden Apple; with altar power it gives a Resistance aura while burning.
- [triggers: deathly veil|invisibility brazier|veil brazier] Targeted fact: Deathly Veil uses Fermented Spider Eye, Ender Pearl, and Diamond; with altar power it gives an Invisibility aura while burning.
- [triggers: drain growth|drain growth brazier|crop drain brazier|undead heal brazier] Targeted fact: Drain Growth uses Condensed Fear, Wormy Apple, and Graveyard Dust; it needs no altar, regresses nearby crops, and heals nearby undead.
- [triggers: graveyard mist|graveyard mist brazier|smoke cloud brazier] Targeted fact: Graveyard Mist uses Nether Wart, Quicklime, and Blaze Powder; it needs altar power and makes a smoke cloud around the Brazier.
