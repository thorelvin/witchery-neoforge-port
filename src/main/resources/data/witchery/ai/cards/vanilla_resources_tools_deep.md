# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Resources, Tools, And Mining Deep Pack

These cards broaden ordinary survival knowledge for witches, guards, hobgoblins, smiths, and vanilla-teacher contexts.

## Tool Progression
- [triggers: tool progression|what tools first|minecraft tools order] [topic: vanilla_tool_progression_v3] [tags: vanilla, tools, progression] Targeted fact: A practical tool path is wood, stone, iron, diamond, then netherite if desired. Upgrade when the resource cost saves time or life.
- [triggers: pickaxe tiers|what pickaxe mines what|mining tiers] [topic: vanilla_pickaxe_tiers] [tags: vanilla, mining, tools] Targeted fact: Pickaxe tier matters. Stone handles early ores, iron handles diamonds and redstone, diamond handles obsidian, and netherite is stronger diamond work.
- [triggers: axe versus sword|best weapon axe sword|combat weapon choice] [topic: vanilla_axe_sword_choice] [tags: vanilla, combat, tools] Targeted fact: Swords are steady against groups, axes hit hard and help with shields depending on rules. Choose for the fight, not for pride.
- [triggers: hoe uses|why make hoe|hoe sculk leaves] [topic: vanilla_hoe_uses] [tags: vanilla, tools, farming] Targeted fact: Hoes are for farming and can quickly break leaves, hay, mossy plant blocks, and sculk-like materials depending on block rules. Not every useful tool looks heroic.
- [triggers: shovel paths|make path|dig gravel sand] [topic: vanilla_shovel_uses] [tags: vanilla, tools, building] Targeted fact: Shovels speed dirt, sand, gravel, clay, snow, and path work. Keep one around unless you enjoy punching geography.
- [triggers: shears uses|wool leaves vines|make shears] [topic: vanilla_shears_uses] [tags: vanilla, tools, gathering] Targeted fact: Shears collect wool, leaves, vines, cobwebs, and other delicate blocks. They save both sheep and time.
- [triggers: bucket uses|water bucket clutch|lava bucket] [topic: vanilla_bucket_uses] [tags: vanilla, tools, survival] Targeted fact: Buckets carry water, lava, milk, fish, powder snow, and panic solutions. A water bucket can stop falls, lava, fires, and many poor decisions.
- [triggers: milk bucket|remove effects|drink milk] [topic: vanilla_milk_bucket] [tags: vanilla, items, effects] Targeted fact: Milk removes many status effects, good and bad. Drink it for poison, mining fatigue, and curses of ordinary potion logic, but remember it also removes helpful effects.

## Mining And Ore Search
- [triggers: mining safely|safe mining|dont die mining] [topic: vanilla_safe_mining] [tags: vanilla, mining, safety] Targeted fact: Mine with torches, food, blocks, water, spare tools, and marked routes. Never dig straight down unless you have accepted slapstick as a religion.
- [triggers: branch mining|strip mining|mine efficiently] [topic: vanilla_branch_mining_v3] [tags: vanilla, mining, method] Targeted fact: Branch mining uses a main tunnel with side branches to expose many blocks safely. Mark exits and lava pockets before turning greed into a maze.
- [triggers: cave mining|explore caves|large cave safety] [topic: vanilla_cave_mining] [tags: vanilla, mining, cave] Targeted fact: Cave mining exposes ores quickly but brings mobs, drops, and darkness. Light as you go and leave escape paths.
- [triggers: coal source|find coal|need torches] [topic: vanilla_find_coal_v3] [tags: vanilla, resource, coal] Targeted fact: Coal appears commonly in stone and mountain areas and fuels early smelting and torches. Charcoal from logs is the backup when caves are stingy.
- [triggers: charcoal|make charcoal|no coal torches] [topic: vanilla_charcoal_v3] [tags: vanilla, resource, fuel] Targeted fact: Smelt logs into charcoal when coal is scarce. It works for torches and fuel, and trees complain less than miners.
- [triggers: iron source|find iron|iron mining] [topic: vanilla_find_iron_v3] [tags: vanilla, resource, iron] Targeted fact: Iron appears underground and in mountains; it supports buckets, shields, armor, tools, hoppers, rails, and a civilization that hurts less.
- [triggers: copper uses|find copper|what is copper for] [topic: vanilla_copper_uses] [tags: vanilla, resource, copper] Targeted fact: Copper is useful for building, lightning rods, spyglasses, brushes, bulbs, and trial-chamber style blocks. It oxidizes unless managed.
- [triggers: redstone source|find redstone|redstone mining] [topic: vanilla_find_redstone] [tags: vanilla, resource, redstone] Targeted fact: Redstone is found deep underground and powers circuits, compasses, clocks, rails, pistons, and contraptions. Mine it with iron tier or better.
- [triggers: lapis lazuli|find lapis|lapis enchanting] [topic: vanilla_lapis_uses] [tags: vanilla, resource, enchanting] Targeted fact: Lapis lazuli is used for enchanting and blue dye. Keep some near the enchanting table or the table will sit there judging you.
- [triggers: gold uses|find gold|gold armor nether] [topic: vanilla_gold_uses] [tags: vanilla, resource, gold] Targeted fact: Gold is soft but useful for powered rails, golden apples, clocks, piglin manners, bartering, and Nether preparation.
- [triggers: diamond source|find diamonds|diamond mining] [topic: vanilla_find_diamonds] [tags: vanilla, resource, diamond] Targeted fact: Diamonds are deep-world treasure for strong tools, armor, enchanting tables, and jukeboxes. Bring iron-or-better picks and lava caution.
- [triggers: emerald source|find emeralds|emerald trading] [topic: vanilla_emerald_uses] [tags: vanilla, resource, emerald] Targeted fact: Emeralds are the villager trade coin and can be mined rarely in mountain biomes. Trading is usually steadier than digging for them.
- [triggers: amethyst uses|what is amethyst for|amethyst shards] [topic: vanilla_amethyst_uses] [tags: vanilla, resource, amethyst] Targeted fact: Amethyst shards support spyglasses, tinted glass, calibrated sculk sensors, and decoration. Let budding amethyst keep growing crystals.
- [triggers: clay source|find clay|bricks terracotta] [topic: vanilla_clay_uses] [tags: vanilla, resource, clay] Targeted fact: Clay comes from watery places and lush caves and becomes bricks or terracotta paths. Bring a shovel unless mud is your chosen enemy.
- [triggers: sand gravel flint|get flint|flint and steel] [topic: vanilla_flint_gravel] [tags: vanilla, resource, flint] Targeted fact: Flint drops from gravel and is needed for arrows and flint and steel. Fortune helps; patience helps cheaper.
- [triggers: obsidian mining|diamond pick obsidian|crying obsidian difference] [topic: vanilla_obsidian_deep] [tags: vanilla, resource, obsidian] Targeted fact: Obsidian needs diamond tier or better to mine and forms when water meets lava source blocks. Crying obsidian is not valid portal-frame obsidian.
- [triggers: deepslate|cobbled deepslate|deep mining slow] [topic: vanilla_deepslate_v3] [tags: vanilla, mining, deepslate] Targeted fact: Deepslate is slower to mine and marks deeper layers. Bring better picks, patience, and a plan for lava.
- [triggers: tuff calcite|decorative stone|stone types] [topic: vanilla_stone_variety] [tags: vanilla, building, geology] Targeted fact: Tuff, calcite, basalt, and stone variants are useful for building palettes and geological clues. A witch notices what a tunnel is made of.
- [triggers: silk touch ores|fortune ores|which enchant mining] [topic: vanilla_silk_fortune_ores] [tags: vanilla, mining, enchantment] Targeted fact: Fortune increases many ore drops; Silk Touch preserves blocks. Use Fortune for yield and Silk Touch when the block itself matters.
- [triggers: repair tools|anvil repair|mending tools] [topic: vanilla_tool_repair] [tags: vanilla, tools, repair] Targeted fact: Repair tools with an anvil, combine tools, or use Mending if enchanted. Grindstones remove enchantments but can recover experience.
- [triggers: keep inventory|items despawn|death loot timer] [topic: vanilla_death_items_general] [tags: vanilla, death, items] Targeted fact: Dropped items despawn after time while their chunk is loaded unless server rules change it. Return prepared, not naked and angry.
