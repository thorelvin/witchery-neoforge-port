# Witchery AI Cards: Vanilla Knowledge Bridge

These cards help NPCs connect ordinary Minecraft questions to Witchery flavor without confusing modded mechanics with vanilla mechanics.

## Comparing Systems
- [triggers: vanilla witch vs coven witch|minecraft witch vs witchery witch|witch mob or coven witch] [topic: bridge_vanilla_witch_coven] [tags: vanilla, witchery, mobs] Targeted fact: A vanilla witch is a hostile potion-throwing mob; a Witchery Coven Witch is a merchant-practitioner who can trade, teach, test, and possibly join a coven through game rules.
- [triggers: vanilla brewing vs witchery brews|potion or brew|kettle vs brewing stand|witchery potion difference] [topic: bridge_brewing_vs_brews] [tags: vanilla, witchery, brewing] Targeted fact: Vanilla brewing uses a brewing stand, blaze powder, bottles, and potion ingredients; Witchery brews use Kettles, custom ingredients, power, and stranger delivery forms.
- [triggers: vanilla village vs witchery village|apothecary or villager|guard or iron golem|witchery settlement] [topic: bridge_village_systems] [tags: vanilla, witchery, village] Targeted fact: Vanilla villages provide villagers, job blocks, golems, and raids; Witchery adds apothecaries, guards, walls, witch huts, bookshops, and social fear around curses and transformations.
- [triggers: vanilla map vs player compass|map compass waystone|witchery scrying vs map|find player compass] [topic: bridge_navigation_tools] [tags: vanilla, witchery, navigation] Targeted fact: Vanilla maps and compasses help with terrain and spawn; Witchery adds Player Compasses, Waystones, Crystal Balls, taglocks, and scrying for more personal or magical locating.
- [triggers: nether and infernal|nether witchery|warped coven nether|infernal magic vanilla] [topic: bridge_nether_infernal] [tags: vanilla, witchery, nether] Targeted fact: The Nether is vanilla's fire dimension; Witchery layers infernal rites, demons, flame imps, warped covens, and bargains on top of that danger.
- [triggers: end and otherwhere|end vs otherwhere|ender magic witchery|otherwhere vanilla] [topic: bridge_end_otherwhere] [tags: vanilla, witchery, end] Targeted fact: The End is vanilla's dragon-and-islands dimension; Otherwhere is Witchery's sideways movement and transposition flavor, tied to witch powers rather than End progression alone.
- [triggers: elytra or flying monkey|fly with monkey or elytra|witchery flight vs vanilla flight] [topic: bridge_elytra_flying_monkey] [tags: vanilla, witchery, travel] Targeted fact: Elytra are vanilla late-End wings; Witchery's Flying Monkeys are mutation-born carriers tied to Bound Waystones. Both move bodies, but only one is a pair of stolen sky-cloth.
- [triggers: mansion and witch hut|woodland mansion witchery hut|illagers or witches|mansion vs vanishing hut] [topic: bridge_mansion_hut] [tags: vanilla, witchery, structures] Targeted fact: Woodland mansions are vanilla illager strongholds; Witchery huts, vanishing huts, and coven places belong to witchcraft.
