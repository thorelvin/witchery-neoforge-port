# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Villagers And Trading

These cards help NPCs answer ordinary village economy questions. Be careful with exact trade lists because servers and versions can change them.

## Villager Basics
- [triggers: how villagers work|villager jobs|profession blocks|job site blocks] [topic: vanilla_villager_jobs] [tags: vanilla, villager, trading] Targeted fact: Adult villagers take professions from nearby job-site blocks, then trade items for emeralds and profession goods. Babies, nitwits, and unemployed villagers do not trade.
- [triggers: villager levels|level up villager|novice apprentice journeyman|master villager] [topic: vanilla_villager_levels] [tags: vanilla, villager, trading] Targeted fact: Villagers level through trading: novice, apprentice, journeyman, expert, master. Higher levels unlock more offers, but not every offer is worth your emeralds.
- [triggers: villager restock|trades locked|villager won't restock|job block restock] [topic: vanilla_villager_restock] [tags: vanilla, villager, trading] Targeted fact: Villagers restock by working at their job-site block during work time. If they cannot reach it, their shelves stay stubborn.
- [triggers: lock villager trade|reroll trades|break lectern trades|villager trade changed] [topic: vanilla_trade_reroll_boundary] [tags: vanilla, villager, trading] Targeted fact: A villager's trades can change before trading locks them; after a trade is made, that profession and offer set are fixed unless special rules change it.
- [triggers: emeralds fast|get emeralds|trade for emeralds|best early trades] [topic: vanilla_emeralds] [tags: vanilla, villager, trading] Targeted fact: Early emeralds often come from farmers, fletchers, shepherds, and other villagers buying crops, sticks, wool, clay, paper, or common goods. Exact best trades depend on the world.

## Useful Professions
- [triggers: cartographer|cartographer villager|cartography table|maps from villager] [topic: vanilla_cartographer] [tags: vanilla, villager, maps] Targeted fact: Cartographers use cartography tables and can sell explorer maps, making them the honest road toward mansions, monuments, and newer mapped structures where supported.
- [triggers: librarian|librarian villager|lectern villager|enchanted book trades] [topic: vanilla_librarian] [tags: vanilla, villager, enchanting] Targeted fact: Librarians use lecterns and can sell enchanted books in ordinary trading rules; exact books and availability may depend on version, experimental settings, or server config.
- [triggers: mending villager|mending book trade|get mending|librarian mending] [topic: vanilla_mending_villager] [tags: vanilla, villager, mending] Targeted fact: Mending can come from enchanted-book sources such as librarians or loot, depending on rules. If the server changes villager trades, trust local behavior over old gossip.
- [triggers: cleric villager|brewing stand villager|ender pearls trade|rotten flesh trade] [topic: vanilla_cleric] [tags: vanilla, villager, brewing] Targeted fact: Clerics use brewing stands and trade in strange goods such as rotten flesh, redstone, lapis, glowstone, bottles, and sometimes End-related supplies depending on version.
- [triggers: farmer villager|composter villager|food trades|crop trades] [topic: vanilla_farmer_villager] [tags: vanilla, villager, farming] Targeted fact: Farmers use composters and are excellent early trade partners: crops into emeralds, emeralds into food, and village gossip into supper.
- [triggers: toolsmith weaponsmith armorer|diamond gear villagers|smith villagers|smithing trades] [topic: vanilla_smith_villagers] [tags: vanilla, villager, gear] Targeted fact: Toolsmiths, weaponsmiths, and armorers can sell useful gear at higher levels. Their job blocks are smithing table, grindstone, and blast furnace.
- [triggers: fletcher villager|stick trade|arrow villager|fletching table] [topic: vanilla_fletcher] [tags: vanilla, villager, arrows] Targeted fact: Fletchers use fletching tables and often help early emerald income through stick or ranged-item trades. Forests become money if chopped politely.
- [triggers: mason villager|stone mason|clay trade|terracotta quartz] [topic: vanilla_mason] [tags: vanilla, villager, building] Targeted fact: Masons use stonecutters and trade stone, clay, bricks, terracotta, quartz, and building goods. Builders should make friends with dusty hands.

## Village Care
- [triggers: protect villagers|keep villagers safe|zombie villagers|village defense] [topic: vanilla_protect_villagers] [tags: vanilla, villager, defense] Targeted fact: Protect villagers with light, walls, beds indoors, door control, golems, and safe job access. A village economy dies one open night at a time.
- [triggers: cure zombie villager|zombie villager cure|weakness golden apple|villager cure] [topic: vanilla_cure_zombie_villager] [tags: vanilla, villager, cure] Targeted fact: Zombie villagers can be cured with Weakness and a golden apple, then time and safety. Trap first, cure second, celebrate after teeth are no longer involved.
- [triggers: iron golem village|golem protects village|make iron golem|village golem] [topic: vanilla_iron_golem_village] [tags: vanilla, villager, defense] Targeted fact: Iron golems protect villages and can also be built with iron blocks and a carved pumpkin. Do not strike villagers or golems unless you want public disagreement.
- [triggers: raid village|pillager raid|bad omen|ominous bottle raid] [topic: vanilla_raids] [tags: vanilla, villager, raid] Targeted fact: Raids bring waves of illagers to a village. Prepare walls, bells, bows, milk or current omen counterplay, and protect villagers before chasing glory.
