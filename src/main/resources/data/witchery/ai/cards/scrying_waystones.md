# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Scrying And Waystones

These cards cover practical sight, binding, and travel focus items. Keep them careful: the witch can advise, not teleport anyone with mere talk.

## Waystones
- [triggers: waystone recipe|make waystone|craft waystone|blank waystone|how do i make a waystone] [topic: waystone_recipe] Targeted fact: A blank Waystone is crafted from Flint and a Bone Needle; it is the stone before place or target binding.
- [triggers: what is a waystone|how do waystones work|use waystone|waystones] Targeted fact: Waystones are binding foci for places or targets; circle rites can turn blank stones into bound or blooded stones for travel and scrying work.
- [triggers: what is a bound waystone|what are bound waystones|bound waystone|bind waystone|location waystone|copy waystone|waystone to place] Targeted fact: Bound Waystones hold a location for travel, scrying, and rite relocation; exact binding steps should come from the opened circle lesson.
- [triggers: what is a blooded waystone|what are blooded waystones|blooded waystone|blood waystone|waystone to player|waystone to creature|entity waystone] Targeted fact: Blooded Waystones bind to a living player or creature through taglock-style ritual work; treat them as personal magic, not ordinary luggage.

## Scrying
- [triggers: crystal ball|scrying|scry|how do i scry|see through someone|see through eyes|prediction] Targeted fact: A Crystal Ball near a powered altar gives predictions; filled Taglock Kits and complete bound Waystones can focus scrying, and invalid or cross-dimension foci are refused before power is spent.
- [triggers: seer stone|call coven|read coven|check coven|summon coven] Targeted fact: A Seer Stone can read coven state or call coven aid; it is for witchcraft status and coven help, not gossip on demand.
- [triggers: player compass|bound compass|compass to player|find a player] Targeted fact: A Player Compass can bind to a player directly or through a filled player Taglock Kit, then report distance or direction when the target is reachable.
- Good witch voice: Seeing is expensive; lies are cheap.
