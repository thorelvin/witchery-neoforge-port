# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Structures, Huts, And Strange Places

These cards make worldgen feel discoverable without turning NPCs into coordinate printers. Prefer clues, counterplay, and danger tone.

## Witchery Villages
- [triggers: witchery village buildings|what buildings in village|apothecary bookshop tower keep|village watch tower] [topic: structures_village_roster] [tags: village, structures] Targeted fact: Witchery villages may include apothecaries, bookshops, witch huts, watch towers, keeps, gatehouses, guards, and walls. A wall means the village expects trouble.
- [triggers: village wall meaning|why village has walls|walled village safe|gatehouse] [topic: structures_village_walls] [tags: village, defense] Targeted fact: Village walls are defense and warning, not welcome. They keep some trouble out and some fear in; vampires, werewolves, and raids still require watchfulness.
- [triggers: apothecary building|apothecary cauldron|apothecary jobsite|is apothecary a witch] [topic: structures_apothecary] [tags: village, apothecary] Targeted fact: An Apothecary is a village potion worker with a cauldron anchor and trade stock; useful, but not a coven witch and not a source of circle secrets.
- [triggers: bookshop witchery|village bookshop|witchery books in village|find primer books] [topic: structures_bookshop] [tags: village, books] Targeted fact: A Witchery bookshop should feel like mundane shelves brushing old craft; send players to books and the Primer rather than inventing a hidden librarian quest.
- [triggers: appoint guard|make village guard|bell guard|sneak use bell] [topic: structures_guard_appointment] [tags: village, guard] Targeted fact: A player can appoint an adult villager as a Village Guard by sneak-using a Bell where the implemented rules allow it; guard life is duty, not glamour.

## Vanishing And Witch Huts
- [triggers: vanishing hut signs|find vanishing hut|wandering hut clue|ash debris hut] [topic: structures_vanishing_hut_clues] [tags: hut, structure clue] Targeted fact: A Vanishing Witch Hut leaves clues: ash, debris, old threshold shapes, and a place that feels recently emptied. It may rebuild nearby or slip Spirit-side.
- [triggers: stop hut moving|anchor vanishing hut|witches salt doorway|salt threshold] [topic: structures_vanishing_hut_anchor] [tags: hut, counterplay] Targeted fact: Lay Witches' Salt across the doorway threshold before looking away to hold a Vanishing Witch Hut; the old remedy works because doors remember crossing.
- [triggers: witch hut resident|hut witch vanished|did witch move with hut|same witch in hut] [topic: structures_hut_resident] [tags: hut, coven witch] Targeted fact: A Vanishing Witch Hut carries its resident witch with it; she belongs to the hearth and should not be treated as a random respawn.
- [triggers: normal witch hut|swamp witch hut|forest witch hut|witch hut safe] [topic: structures_witch_hut_manners] [tags: hut, safety] Targeted fact: A witch hut is shelter, shop, warning, and territory. Ask politely, keep hands out of chests, and assume the threshold has opinions.

## Hobgoblin And Nether Places
- [triggers: hobgoblin hut|find hobgoblin hut|woodland hut|forest hobgoblin] [topic: structures_hobgoblin_hut] [tags: hobgoblin, structure] Targeted fact: Hobgoblin Huts belong to woodland edges and forest work; expect a resident hobgoblin, rough craft, and no village manners.
- [triggers: warped coven|nether coven|warped forest coven|infernal coven structure] [topic: structures_warped_coven] [tags: nether, coven] Targeted fact: A Warped Coven may appear in Nether warped forests with a coven witch and infernal loot; Nether witchcraft has no patience for village comfort.
- [triggers: strange well|haunted well|well structure|cursed well] [topic: structures_strange_well] [tags: well, omen] Targeted fact: Strange wells invite rumor, spirits, and bad decisions. Bring light, salt, and another pair of eyes before testing whether the whisper has teeth.
