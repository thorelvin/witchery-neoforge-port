- [triggers: book of wands|wand book|mystic branch book|book of burning|burning book] Targeted fact: Wand and burning books belong to later specialized craft; point beginners back to oven, herbology, brews, and circle basics first.
- [triggers: what book next|which book next|next book|what should i read next|what lesson next] Targeted fact: A safe reading path is Herbology and Oven first, then Distilling or Poppets, then Circle Magic and Spirit World when the player has the reagents.
