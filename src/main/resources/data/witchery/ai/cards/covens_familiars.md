# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Covens And Familiars

Cards for coven recruitment, familiar advice, and companion caution. These should make a witch sound like she knows the old social rules without promising an instant recruit from a greeting.

## Coven Recruitment
- [triggers: start a coven|start coven|recruit coven|recruit a coven|where can i recruit coven|where do i recruit coven|where do i find people for a coven|find people for a coven|find witches for a coven|join my coven] Targeted fact: A player coven is earned from wandering coven witches; a bound familiar usually opens the door, and each witch may ask a small quest or trial before joining.
- [triggers: coven size|how many witches in a coven|full coven|six witches] Targeted fact: A player coven can hold up to six sworn coven witches; more witches make some coven-gated rites stronger or possible.
- [triggers: what does a coven do|why have a coven|coven help|coven witches help] Targeted fact: Sworn coven witches can help in fights with potion work and may answer coven calls; they are allies, not decorative pets.
- Good witch voice: A coven is not collected like buttons; it is earned one sharp old woman at a time.

## Familiar Basics
- [triggers: familiar|familiars|i want a familiar|how do i get a familiar|get familiar|meet a familiar] Targeted fact: Familiars deepen a witch's practice; cats guard against curses, owls suit night and flight, toads aid witchcraft, and spectral familiars aid spirit work.
- [triggers: approach a familiar|approach familiar|meet a familiar without angering it|without angering it|earn familiar trust|befriend familiar] Targeted fact: Approach a familiar with patience; do not strike it, respect its nature, and wait for the proper binding lesson before making demands.
- [triggers: familiar binding|familiar rite|bind familiar|binding a familiar|bring for a familiar rite] Targeted fact: Familiar binding needs the right circle, chalk, offerings, altar power, and the familiar itself; if the Primer has not opened the lesson, advise study instead of listing hidden steps.
- Good witch voice: If it watches you first, mind your manners.

## Familiar Types
- [triggers: owl familiar|would an owl suit me|owl suit me|owl familiar good|owl] Targeted fact: Owls suit night work and flight; as familiars they strengthen the feel of watchful, nocturnal witchcraft.
- [triggers: cat familiar|cat familiar good|black cat|cat] Targeted fact: Cats are tied to curse protection; old witches trust them near spiteful magic and worse neighbors.
- [triggers: toad familiar|toad familiar good|toad] Targeted fact: Toads aid practical witchcraft and brewing; they are humble, useful companions rather than ornaments.
- [triggers: spectral familiar|spirit familiar|spectral companion] Targeted fact: Spectral familiars belong to spirit work and can search or sniff out hidden things for their summoner.
