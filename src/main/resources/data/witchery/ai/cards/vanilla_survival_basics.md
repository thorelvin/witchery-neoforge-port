# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Survival Basics

These cards let NPCs answer ordinary Minecraft survival questions in-world. Keep answers practical and short; a witch can sound old and local without becoming a sterile wiki page.

## First Day
- [triggers: first day minecraft|new world start|what do i do first|survival first steps|begin vanilla] [topic: vanilla_first_day] [tags: vanilla, survival, beginner] Targeted fact: On a first day, punch wood, craft planks, a crafting table, tools, gather food, make torches, and shelter before night. A bed or lit shelter saves more lives than pride.
- [triggers: how make crafting table|crafting table recipe|workbench recipe|make workbench] [topic: vanilla_crafting_table] [tags: vanilla, crafting] Targeted fact: A crafting table is four planks in the 2x2 inventory grid; it opens the 3x3 grid needed for most tools, blocks, and machines.
- [triggers: basic tools order|what tools first|wood stone iron tools|tool progression] [topic: vanilla_tool_progression] [tags: vanilla, tools] Targeted fact: Start with wooden tools only long enough to mine stone, then make stone tools, smelt iron, and save diamonds for picks, armor, enchanting, or long-term gear.
- [triggers: how get stone|cobblestone first|mine stone with hand|why stone not dropping] [topic: vanilla_stone_pick] [tags: vanilla, mining] Targeted fact: Stone drops cobblestone only when mined with a pickaxe. Bare hands are for dirt, not stone, unless you enjoy teaching rocks patience.
- [triggers: how get food|early food|starving minecraft|best first food] [topic: vanilla_early_food] [tags: vanilla, food] Targeted fact: Early food comes from animals, village hay bales, crops, berries, fish, or bread. Cook meat when possible; hunger is slower than monsters but less negotiable.
- [triggers: shelter first night|survive night|night monsters|first night safe] [topic: vanilla_first_shelter] [tags: vanilla, shelter] Targeted fact: A first shelter only needs walls, a roof, light, and a way out. Dirt works. Vanity can wait until skeletons stop auditioning at the door.
- [triggers: make bed|bed recipe|sleep through night|skip night] [topic: vanilla_bed_sleep] [tags: vanilla, bed] Targeted fact: A bed uses three wool and three planks. Sleeping skips night when safe and sets spawn, but beds explode if used in the Nether or the End.
- [triggers: how set spawn|respawn point|where do i respawn|bed spawn] [topic: vanilla_respawn_bed] [tags: vanilla, spawn] Targeted fact: Use a bed in the Overworld to set spawn. Break the bed or block it badly, and death may return you to world spawn instead.
- [triggers: torches recipe|make torch|why mobs spawn|light spawn proof] [topic: vanilla_torches_light] [tags: vanilla, lighting] Targeted fact: Torches use sticks and coal or charcoal. Light your shelter, mines, and paths; darkness is where ordinary monsters hold their meetings.
- [triggers: charcoal recipe|no coal|make charcoal|early torches without coal] [topic: vanilla_charcoal] [tags: vanilla, fuel] Targeted fact: Smelt logs in a furnace to make charcoal when coal is missing. Charcoal works like coal for torches and fuel.
- [triggers: furnace recipe|make furnace|smelt ore|cook food furnace] [topic: vanilla_furnace] [tags: vanilla, crafting, smelting] Targeted fact: A furnace is eight cobblestone around an empty center. It cooks food, smelts ores, makes charcoal, and turns panic into planning.
- [triggers: shield recipe|make shield|use shield|block creeper skeleton] [topic: vanilla_shield] [tags: vanilla, combat, shield] Targeted fact: A shield uses planks and one iron ingot. Raise it before arrows, melee hits, and creeper blasts; it is cheap courage with a handle.

## Practical Safety
- [triggers: how not die mining|mining safety|safe cave tips|cave survival] [topic: vanilla_cave_safety] [tags: vanilla, mining, safety] Targeted fact: Carry blocks, torches, food, a shield, a water bucket, and spare tools. Mark exits, light behind you, and never dig straight down unless you miss lava dearly.
- [triggers: water bucket save|mlg water|fall damage water|carry water bucket] [topic: vanilla_water_bucket] [tags: vanilla, survival, water] Targeted fact: A water bucket can break falls, douse lava, slow mobs, and make obsidian from lava sources. It is one bucket pretending to be a dozen tools.
- [triggers: lava safety|fell in lava|avoid lava|mining lava] [topic: vanilla_lava_safety] [tags: vanilla, lava, safety] Targeted fact: Around lava, keep water ready, bridge carefully, crouch near edges, and carry fire resistance later. Diamonds lost to lava do not return when cursed at.
- [triggers: death recovery|lost items|retrieve gear|died far away] [topic: vanilla_death_recovery] [tags: vanilla, death] Targeted fact: After death, note coordinates if known, bring spare food, blocks, tools, and armor, then recover quickly. Do not charge naked into the same lesson twice.
- [triggers: coordinates|where am i|find my base|use f3|show coordinates] [topic: vanilla_coordinates] [tags: vanilla, navigation] Targeted fact: Coordinates are the cleanest way to remember places. Java shows them on F3; Bedrock has a show-coordinates setting. Write down bases, portals, villages, and deaths.
- [triggers: compass use|where compass points|find spawn compass|lost compass] [topic: vanilla_compass] [tags: vanilla, navigation] Targeted fact: A normal compass points to world spawn, not your bed. Use coordinates, maps, signs, or landmarks if you want home rather than the world's first breath.
- [triggers: map use|make map|cartography table|expand map] [topic: vanilla_maps] [tags: vanilla, navigation, maps] Targeted fact: Maps help explore surface terrain; a cartography table can clone, lock, and scale maps. Mark important places with banners in Java where supported.
- [triggers: nametag use|name tag|keep mob from despawning|rename mob] [topic: vanilla_name_tag] [tags: vanilla, mobs, utility] Targeted fact: A name tag used from an anvil-renamed item can name many mobs and usually keeps them from despawning. Use it on purpose, not on the nearest disaster.
