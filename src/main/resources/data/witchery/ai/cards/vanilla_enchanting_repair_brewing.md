# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Enchanting, Repair, And Brewing

These cards cover vanilla gear improvement. State exact mechanics only where stable; remind players that local version/config can change edge cases.

## Enchanting
- [triggers: how enchanting works|enchanting table|make enchantment table|enchant gear] [topic: vanilla_enchanting_basic] [tags: vanilla, enchanting] Targeted fact: Enchanting augments tools, weapons, armor, and books using experience and lapis lazuli. The table offers choices, but chance still keeps a crooked little throne.
- [triggers: bookshelves enchanting|level 30 enchanting|15 bookshelves|max enchant table] [topic: vanilla_enchanting_bookshelves] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, bookshelves] Targeted fact: Place up to 15 bookshelves around an enchanting table with one block of air between to unlock level-30 offers.
- [triggers: lapis enchanting|need lapis|why enchant needs lapis|lapis lazuli enchant] [topic: vanilla_enchanting_lapis] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, lapis] Targeted fact: Enchanting table use requires lapis lazuli as well as experience. Keep lapis near the table like ink near a contract.
- [triggers: enchant books|enchanted book use|book enchantment|save enchantment] [topic: vanilla_enchanted_books] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, books] Targeted fact: Enchanting a book stores an enchantment for later anvil use. Books are slower than direct enchanting, but less likely to waste a good sword.
- [triggers: anvil combine|use anvil|repair with anvil|combine enchantments] [topic: vanilla_anvil] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, repair] Targeted fact: Anvils repair, rename, and combine enchantments at an experience cost. Too much prior work can make an item too expensive.
- [triggers: grindstone|remove enchantment|disenchant item|get xp back enchant] [topic: vanilla_grindstone] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, repair] Targeted fact: A grindstone removes most non-curse enchantments and returns some experience. It cleans mistakes, not curses.
- [triggers: mending enchantment|how mending works|xp repairs item|mending gear] [topic: vanilla_mending] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, mending] Targeted fact: Mending uses collected experience to repair equipped or held items with the enchantment. It is priceless on elytra, tools, and armor.
- [triggers: unbreaking enchantment|unbreaking use|gear durability enchant] [topic: vanilla_unbreaking] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, durability] Targeted fact: Unbreaking makes durability last longer. Pair it with Mending when the item matters more than your willingness to replace it.
- [triggers: fortune vs silk touch|fortune or silk|silk touch pick|fortune pick] [topic: vanilla_fortune_silk] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, mining] Targeted fact: Fortune increases many drops; Silk Touch preserves blocks. They conflict on the same tool in ordinary rules, so choose yield or precision.
- [triggers: protection enchantment|best armor enchants|armor protection|feather falling] [topic: vanilla_armor_enchants] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, armor] Targeted fact: Protection helps broadly, Feather Falling saves ankles, Respiration and Aqua Affinity help water work, and specialized protections trade breadth for focus.
- [triggers: bow enchantments|power bow|infinity mending bow|best bow] [topic: vanilla_bow_enchants] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, bow] Targeted fact: Power, Punch, Flame, Infinity, and Mending shape bow use, but Infinity and Mending conflict in normal rules. Choose endless arrows or self-repair.

## Brewing
- [triggers: brewing stand|how brewing works|make potions|vanilla potions] [topic: vanilla_brewing_basic] [tags: vanilla, brewing] Targeted fact: Vanilla brewing uses a brewing stand, blaze powder fuel, water bottles, nether wart for Awkward Potions, then effect ingredients and modifiers.
- [triggers: awkward potion|nether wart potion|start brewing|base potion] [topic: vanilla_awkward_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing] Targeted fact: Nether wart turns water bottles into Awkward Potions, the base for most useful effect potions.
- [triggers: extend potion|redstone potion|glowstone potion|potion duration strength] [topic: vanilla_potion_modifiers] [tags: vanilla, brewing] Targeted fact: Redstone usually extends potion duration, glowstone can strengthen some effects, gunpowder makes splash potions, and dragon's breath makes lingering potions.
- [triggers: water breathing potion|brew water breathing|ocean monument potion] [topic: vanilla_water_breathing] [tags: vanilla, brewing, ocean] Targeted fact: Water Breathing potions make ocean monuments, ruins, and long dives far safer. Brew before trusting bubbles and prayer.
- [triggers: slow falling potion|brew slow falling|dragon fight falling|phantom membrane] [topic: vanilla_slow_falling] [tags: vanilla, brewing, end] Targeted fact: Slow Falling potions help against dragon knockback, End islands, cliffs, and shulker levitation. Falling slowly is still falling, just politely.
- [triggers: weakness potion|brew weakness|cure zombie villager potion] [topic: vanilla_weakness_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, villagers] Targeted fact: Weakness enables zombie villager curing when paired with a golden apple. Splash delivery is common because patients with teeth seldom queue nicely.
- [triggers: beacon|how beacon works|nether star beacon|pyramid beacon] [topic: vanilla_beacon] [tags: vanilla, beacon] Targeted fact: A beacon needs a Nether Star and a mineral-block pyramid to grant area effects. It is late-game vanity with practical teeth.
