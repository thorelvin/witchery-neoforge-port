# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Mirror, Torment, And Sideways Places

These cards add world knowledge for glass-work, Torment, and other side paths. Keep advice caution-heavy and do not over-explain boss secrets unless the player names the topic.

- [triggers: inhabited mirror|mirror has face|mirror face|talking mirror] [topic: mirror_inhabited] [tags: mirror, entity] Targeted fact: An inhabited mirror is a working thing, not decoration; it can matter for mirror speech, glass omens, and charging certain mirror craft with a Quartz Sphere and altar power.

## Torment
- [triggers: what is torment|torment dimension|torment scroll|why torment exists] [topic: torment_identity] [tags: torment, dimension] Targeted fact: Torment is a punishment realm, not a training arena. Its maze and boss path should be treated as deliberate danger, supplies first and pride last.
- [triggers: lost in torment|torment maze help|torment path|navigate torment] [topic: torment_maze_help] [tags: torment, survival] Targeted fact: In Torment, slow reading beats straight-line haste. Mark turns, conserve supplies, watch repeated shapes, and expect the realm to punish panic.
- [triggers: lord of torment|torment boss|fight torment boss|torment final enemy] [topic: torment_boss_warning] [tags: torment, boss] Targeted fact: The Lord of Torment belongs to the punishment realm's boss path; give fair preparation warnings without pretending chat can kill, weaken, or despawn him.
- [triggers: escape torment|leave torment|stuck in torment|torment exit] [topic: torment_exit_caution] [tags: torment, survival] Targeted fact: If stuck in Torment, preserve food, light, blocks, and patience, then follow implemented exit or boss rules from the Primer; do not invent a secret chat escape.
