# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Status Effects And Useful Items

These cards cover common utility items and effects players ask about while adventuring.

## Utility Items
- [triggers: ender chest|make ender chest|ender chest use|private chest] [topic: vanilla_ender_chest] [tags: vanilla, storage, ender] Targeted fact: Ender chests share a private inventory per player across all ender chests. Silk Touch is needed to pick one up whole.
- [triggers: shulker box use|portable storage|make shulker box|shulker shell chest] [topic: vanilla_shulker_box_use] [tags: vanilla, storage, end] Targeted fact: Shulker boxes keep contents when broken, making them late-game portable storage. They are the reason many players forgive End cities for levitation.
- [triggers: bundle use|bundle item|store small stacks|inventory bundle] [topic: vanilla_bundle] [tags: vanilla, storage] Targeted fact: Bundles hold mixed small stacks to ease inventory clutter where enabled. They are pockets for people who pick up every flower and call it strategy.
- [triggers: spyglass|make spyglass|zoom item|amethyst copper] [topic: vanilla_spyglass] [tags: vanilla, utility, amethyst] Targeted fact: A spyglass uses copper and amethyst to zoom distant views. It finds towers, mobs, ships, and mistakes before your feet do.
- [triggers: brush archaeology|make brush|suspicious sand|suspicious gravel] [topic: vanilla_brush] [tags: vanilla, archaeology] Targeted fact: A brush reveals loot from suspicious sand or gravel at archaeology sites. Brush gently; broken suspicion drops nothing but disappointment.
- [triggers: goat horn|find goat horn|what goat horn does|horn sound] [topic: vanilla_goat_horn] [tags: vanilla, utility, sound] Targeted fact: Goat horns are sound items from goats and loot sources. They are not power by themselves, but they do announce drama nicely.
- [triggers: totem of undying|how to get totem|totem use|evoker drop] [topic: vanilla_totem] [tags: vanilla, combat, mansion] Targeted fact: Totems of Undying can save a player from fatal damage when held and are dropped by evokers, including those in mansions and raids.
- [triggers: saddle|find saddle|craft saddle|ride horse] [topic: vanilla_saddle] [tags: vanilla, travel, loot] Targeted fact: Saddles are found through loot, fishing, or trades depending on rules; they are not normally crafted in long-standing vanilla play. Check the local version before swearing.
- [triggers: lead recipe|make lead|leash animals|move mobs] [topic: vanilla_lead] [tags: vanilla, utility, animals] Targeted fact: Leads move many passive mobs and tie them to fences. They are string and slime pretending to be authority.
- [triggers: shears use|make shears|harvest leaves wool|shear sheep] [topic: vanilla_shears] [tags: vanilla, utility] Targeted fact: Shears collect wool without killing sheep and harvest leaves, vines, cobwebs, and other delicate things. Blades are not always for murder.

## Effects And Counters
- [triggers: poison effect|poisoned minecraft|cave spider poison|witch poison] [topic: vanilla_poison_effect] [tags: vanilla, effects] Targeted fact: Poison drains health but usually will not kill by itself in normal difficulty rules; it leaves you weak enough for everything else to finish the argument.
- [triggers: wither effect|wither damage|black hearts|wither skeleton effect] [topic: vanilla_wither_effect] [tags: vanilla, effects] Targeted fact: Wither damages over time and can kill. Treat wither skeletons, Wither roses, and the Wither boss with more caution than ordinary poison.
- [triggers: bad omen|ominous effect|raid omen|ominous bottle] [topic: vanilla_ominous_effects] [tags: vanilla, effects, raids] Targeted fact: Ominous effects and bottles tie into raids or trials in newer rules. Check the current server behavior before walking into a village like a loaded curse.
- [triggers: mining fatigue|elder guardian effect|can't mine monument|milk fatigue] [topic: vanilla_mining_fatigue] [tags: vanilla, effects, monument] Targeted fact: Elder guardians inflict Mining Fatigue in ocean monuments, making blocks painfully slow to break. Milk clears it, but the guardian may reapply it.
- [triggers: levitation effect|shulker levitation|floating up|counter levitation] [topic: vanilla_levitation] [tags: vanilla, effects, end] Targeted fact: Shulker levitation lifts players into fall danger. Ceilings, pearls, Slow Falling, Feather Falling, and quick kills keep the sky from claiming rent.
- [triggers: fire resistance effect|lava immunity|fire immunity potion|nether safety effect] [topic: vanilla_fire_resistance_effect] [tags: vanilla, effects, nether] Targeted fact: Fire Resistance prevents fire and lava damage while active. It does not prevent drowning, falling, arrogance, or poor navigation.
- [triggers: night vision effect|see in dark potion|cave vision|underwater vision] [topic: vanilla_night_vision] [tags: vanilla, effects] Targeted fact: Night Vision brightens dark places and pairs well with caves, oceans, ancient cities, and suspicious building inspections.
- [triggers: invisibility effect|invisible mobs|invisibility potion|does invisibility work] [topic: vanilla_invisibility] [tags: vanilla, effects] Targeted fact: Invisibility hides the player's body, but armor, held items, particles, sounds, and mob rules still matter. It is subtlety, not absence.
