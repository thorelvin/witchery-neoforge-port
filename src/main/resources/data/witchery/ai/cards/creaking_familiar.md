# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Creaking Familiar

These notes cover the stranger familiar path tied to a wild Creaking.

## Creaking Familiar
- [triggers: creaking familiar|rite of the creakening|bind creaking|wild creaking|creaking heart] [topic: creaking_familiar] Targeted fact: The Rite of the Creakening binds a wild Creaking as a familiar with two callings: a restrained following companion or a rooted guardian.
- [triggers: should i bind creaking|creaking safe|creaking watched|creaking resin] [topic: creaking_familiar_caution] Targeted fact: Only a rooted, unwatched Creaking slowly sheds Resin Clumps; it will not shed another while one waits nearby, and following Creakings produce none.
- [triggers: creaking familiar command|creaking sit|creaking follow|dismiss creaking|root creaking] [topic: creaking_familiar_commands] Targeted fact: Use a bound Creaking to switch between following and a guard rooted at its present place; sneak-use an already rooted guardian to dismiss it. The Rite of Calling will not uproot a chosen watch.
- [triggers: creaking guard|creaking warning|creaking intruder|creaking repel] [topic: creaking_familiar_guard] Targeted fact: A rooted Creaking warns its owner of non-allied intruders without constant repetition and turns aside only a few nearby monsters at a patient cadence.
