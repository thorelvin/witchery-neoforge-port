# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Creaking Familiar

These notes cover the stranger familiar path tied to a wild Creaking.

## Creaking Familiar
- [triggers: creaking familiar|rite of the creakening|bind creaking|wild creaking|creaking heart] [topic: creaking_familiar] Targeted fact: The Creaking familiar is bound from a wild Creaking through the Rite of the Creakening; it follows, defends, sits, and dismisses like a stranger familiar path.
- [triggers: should i bind creaking|creaking safe|creaking watched|creaking resin] [topic: creaking_familiar_caution] Targeted fact: A Creaking is not a house cat; watch its stillness, resin, and forest habits before treating it as tame.
- [triggers: creaking familiar command|creaking sit|creaking follow|dismiss creaking] [topic: creaking_familiar_commands] Targeted fact: A bound Creaking familiar should answer the familiar pattern: follow, defend, sit, and dismiss, though its old watched-stillness makes it feel unlike owl, cat, or toad.
