# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Farms, Animals, And Village Life Deep Pack

These cards support grounded everyday knowledge for villagers, witches, guards, and practical teachers.

## Crop Farming
- [triggers: start farm|basic crop farm|grow crops] [topic: vanilla_basic_crop_farm] [tags: vanilla, farming, crops] Targeted fact: Till dirt near water, plant seeds or crops, keep enough light, protect from trampling, and harvest mature plants for food and more seed.
- [triggers: farmland water range|hydrate farmland|how far water crops] [topic: vanilla_farmland_water] [tags: vanilla, farming, water] Targeted fact: One water source hydrates farmland up to four blocks away horizontally. A neat nine-by-nine field with water in the center is the old practical pattern.
- [triggers: bone meal crops|grow crops faster|use bone meal] [topic: vanilla_bone_meal_crops] [tags: vanilla, farming, bone meal] Targeted fact: Bone meal speeds many plants, grows saplings, and feeds impatient farms. Skeletons are terrible neighbors but useful fertilizer.
- [triggers: compost seeds|make bone meal compost|composter] [topic: vanilla_composter] [tags: vanilla, farming, compost] Targeted fact: Compost spare plant matter in a composter to make bone meal. Farmers use composters as job blocks, so do not steal livelihoods thoughtlessly.
- [triggers: wheat carrots potatoes beetroots|crop types|villager crops] [topic: vanilla_crop_types] [tags: vanilla, farming, crops] Targeted fact: Wheat, carrots, potatoes, and beetroot feed players and villages. Farmers especially understand these crops and may share or harvest them by schedule.
- [triggers: sugar cane farm|grow sugar cane|paper cane] [topic: vanilla_sugar_cane_v3] [tags: vanilla, farming, sugar cane] Targeted fact: Sugar cane grows beside water and makes paper and sugar. Paper feeds maps, books, fireworks, and trades.
- [triggers: bamboo farm|grow bamboo|bamboo uses] [topic: vanilla_bamboo_v3] [tags: vanilla, farming, bamboo] Targeted fact: Bamboo grows quickly and can become scaffolding, sticks, fuel, and bamboo building blocks. It is a plant with ambition.
- [triggers: cactus farm|grow cactus|cactus green dye] [topic: vanilla_cactus_v3] [tags: vanilla, farming, cactus] Targeted fact: Cactus grows on sand with open sides, hurts touchers, and smelts into green dye. Do not put valuables where cactus can eat them.
- [triggers: cocoa beans|grow cocoa|jungle cocoa] [topic: vanilla_cocoa] [tags: vanilla, farming, cocoa] Targeted fact: Cocoa beans grow on jungle logs and make brown dye and cookies. Jungle wood is the host, not ordinary oak.
- [triggers: melon pumpkin|grow melons pumpkins|gourd farm] [topic: vanilla_gourds] [tags: vanilla, farming, melon pumpkin] Targeted fact: Melons and pumpkins need a planted stem and adjacent space for the fruit block. Leave room beside stems or they sulk.
- [triggers: mushroom farm|grow mushrooms|huge mushroom] [topic: vanilla_mushroom_farming] [tags: vanilla, farming, mushrooms] Targeted fact: Mushrooms need dim suitable space unless using podzol, mycelium, or nylium conditions; bone meal can grow huge mushrooms when space allows.
- [triggers: tree farm|grow trees|saplings] [topic: vanilla_tree_farm] [tags: vanilla, farming, trees] Targeted fact: Plant saplings with enough space and light, then harvest logs and replant. A wood supply is survival, building, fuel, and tool security.

## Animal Handling
- [triggers: breed animals|animal breeding|farm animals] [topic: vanilla_animal_breeding] [tags: vanilla, animals, breeding] Targeted fact: Feed animals their preferred food to breed them: wheat for cows and sheep, seeds for chickens, carrots or potatoes for pigs, and so on.
- [triggers: cows sheep pigs chickens|basic animal farm|what animals keep] [topic: vanilla_basic_animals] [tags: vanilla, animals, farm] Targeted fact: Cows give beef, leather, and milk; sheep give wool and mutton; pigs give pork; chickens give eggs, feathers, and meat.
- [triggers: tame wolf|get dog|wolf bones] [topic: vanilla_tame_wolf] [tags: vanilla, animals, taming] Targeted fact: Wolves can be tamed with bones and become loyal dogs. Keep them away from lava, cliffs, and heroics they did not volunteer for.
- [triggers: tame cat|get cat|cats scare creepers] [topic: vanilla_tame_cat] [tags: vanilla, animals, taming] Targeted fact: Cats can be tamed with raw fish, scare creepers and phantoms, and sit on things because they believe ownership is spiritual law.
- [triggers: tame horse|horse saddle|ride horse] [topic: vanilla_tame_horse] [tags: vanilla, animals, horse] Targeted fact: Tame horses by repeated riding until hearts appear, then use a saddle to control them. Armor and speed vary by horse.
- [triggers: mule donkey chest|donkey storage|mule transport] [topic: vanilla_pack_animals] [tags: vanilla, animals, transport] Targeted fact: Donkeys and mules can carry chests when equipped, making them practical travel companions if you respect terrain.
- [triggers: llama caravan|llama chest|wandering trader llama] [topic: vanilla_llamas] [tags: vanilla, animals, transport] Targeted fact: Llamas can carry chests and form caravans. They spit at trouble, which is funny until trouble is you.
- [triggers: bee farm|bees honey|beehive campfire] [topic: vanilla_bees_v3] [tags: vanilla, animals, bees] Targeted fact: Bees pollinate crops and make honey. Use campfire smoke under hives before harvesting or the bees will hold a meeting on your face.
- [triggers: turtle eggs|scute helmet|breed turtles] [topic: vanilla_turtles] [tags: vanilla, animals, turtle] Targeted fact: Turtles breed with seagrass and lay eggs on home beaches. Protect eggs from mobs if you want scutes.
- [triggers: frog lights|frogs slime|frog magma cube] [topic: vanilla_frogs] [tags: vanilla, animals, frogs] Targeted fact: Frogs eat small slimes and can create froglights from small magma cubes. Strange light often begins with stranger appetites.
- [triggers: allay use|allay item helper|what allays do] [topic: vanilla_allay_use] [tags: vanilla, animals, allay] Targeted fact: Allays collect matching dropped items for the item they hold. They are helpers, not miners, and need safe space to be useful.

## Villages
- [triggers: protect village|village defense|keep villagers safe] [topic: vanilla_village_defense] [tags: vanilla, village, defense] Targeted fact: Protect villages with light, walls, beds, bells, golems, clear paths, and fewer holes. Zombies are less frightening when doors are not decorative.
- [triggers: make iron golem|iron golem village|golem defense] [topic: vanilla_iron_golem_defense] [tags: vanilla, village, golem] Targeted fact: Iron golems defend villagers from many threats. They are strong, not omnipresent, so walls and lights still matter.
- [triggers: villager breeding|make villagers breed|village beds food] [topic: vanilla_villager_breeding_basic] [tags: vanilla, village, villagers] Targeted fact: Villager breeding needs willing villagers, enough beds, and food such as bread, carrots, potatoes, or beetroots. Privacy helps mostly because zombies do not.
- [triggers: villager job blocks|assign profession|change villager job] [topic: vanilla_job_blocks_deep] [tags: vanilla, village, jobs] Targeted fact: Unemployed adult villagers claim available job-site blocks; traded villagers keep their profession. Nitwits and children do not take jobs.
- [triggers: restock trades|villager restock|locked trades] [topic: vanilla_villager_restock_v3] [tags: vanilla, village, trades] Targeted fact: Villagers restock by working at their claimed job site, usually up to twice per day. If trades lock, check access to the workstation.
- [triggers: cure zombie villager|zombie villager cure|discount trades] [topic: vanilla_cure_zombie_villager_v3] [tags: vanilla, village, cure] Targeted fact: Cure a zombie villager with Weakness and a golden apple. Protect the patient during curing unless you enjoy losing both villager and apple.
- [triggers: bell use|village bell|ring bell] [topic: vanilla_village_bell] [tags: vanilla, village, bell] Targeted fact: Bells gather attention, mark village centers, and matter during raids. A bell is not a toy if the whole village knows panic by sound.
- [triggers: hero of the village|raid reward|villager gifts] [topic: vanilla_hero_of_village] [tags: vanilla, village, raid] Targeted fact: Defeating a raid can grant Hero of the Village, improving villager treatment and gifts. Being useful is the one magic villagers respect quickly.
