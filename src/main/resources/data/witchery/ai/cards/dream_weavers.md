# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Dream Weavers

Dream Weaver cards help the witch advise sleepers without treating the Spirit World as harmless.

## Dream Catchers
- [triggers: dream catcher|dreamcatcher|what does a dream catcher do|how use dream catcher|place dream catcher] Targeted fact: A Dream Catcher placed near the sleeping body shapes what happens as consciousness crosses the Spirit World boundary; only one nearby catcher takes effect.
- [triggers: dream weaver|dream weave|dream weavers|weave effects|which dream weave] Targeted fact: Dream Weavers are Dream Catcher threads: Fleet Foot for speed, Iron Arm for strength, Fasting for hunger, Nightmares for dream odds, Intensity for stronger potions.
- [triggers: one catcher|multiple dream catchers|stack dream catchers|stack many dream catchers|many dream catchers|many catchers] Targeted fact: Only one Dream Catcher near the sleeper takes effect, so choose deliberately instead of stacking every weave.

## Weave Recipes
- [triggers: fleet foot weave|weave fleet foot|make fleet foot|fleet foot dream] Targeted fact: Fleet Foot uses the quickness frame: Item Frame, Fanciful Thread, feathers, Diamond Vapour, Tormented Twine, and long swiftness/slowness splash potions.
- [triggers: iron arm weave|weave iron arm|make iron arm|iron arm dream] Targeted fact: Iron Arm uses the same weave frame but swaps in long strength and weakness splash potions.
- [triggers: fasting weave|weave fasting|make fasting dream|fasting dream] Targeted fact: Fasting uses a long poison splash potion and Mellifluous Hunger in the opposing potion slot.
- [triggers: nightmares weave|weave nightmares|make nightmares dream|nightmare weave] Targeted fact: Nightmares is the harsher Tormented Twine frame, woven with Diamond Vapour and long poison/night-vision splash potions.
- [triggers: intensity weave|weave intensity|make intensity dream|intensity dream] Targeted fact: Intensity binds Brew of Flowing Spirit against Brew of Sleeping in the old frame.
- Good witch voice: Sleep is not passive; it is a road with poor signposts.
