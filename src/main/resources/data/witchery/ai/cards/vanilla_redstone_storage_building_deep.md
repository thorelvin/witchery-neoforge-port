# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Redstone, Storage, And Building Deep Pack

These cards keep ordinary technical advice short enough for NPC speech. Prefer practical first steps over full engineering lectures.

## Redstone Basics
- [triggers: redstone basics|how redstone works|start redstone] [topic: vanilla_redstone_basics_v3] [tags: vanilla, redstone, basics] Targeted fact: Redstone carries power through dust, blocks, torches, repeaters, comparators, observers, buttons, levers, and pressure plates. Start small before making a haunted machine.
- [triggers: lever button pressure plate|redstone inputs|what powers redstone] [topic: vanilla_redstone_inputs] [tags: vanilla, redstone, inputs] Targeted fact: Levers stay on, buttons pulse, pressure plates sense weight, tripwires sense crossing, and observers sense block updates. Choose the input that matches the mischief.
- [triggers: repeater use|redstone repeater|extend redstone signal] [topic: vanilla_redstone_repeater] [tags: vanilla, redstone, repeater] Targeted fact: Repeaters extend signals, add delay, and control direction. They are redstone's way of telling power to walk in a line.
- [triggers: comparator use|redstone comparator|container signal] [topic: vanilla_redstone_comparator] [tags: vanilla, redstone, comparator] Targeted fact: Comparators read container fullness, compare signal strength, and support subtraction mode. They are useful for storage, farms, and clever doors.
- [triggers: observer use|redstone observer|detect block update] [topic: vanilla_redstone_observer] [tags: vanilla, redstone, observer] Targeted fact: Observers emit a pulse when the face detects a block change. Use them for crop farms, flying machines, and machinery that blinks first.
- [triggers: piston door|sticky piston|piston basics] [topic: vanilla_piston_basics] [tags: vanilla, redstone, piston] Targeted fact: Pistons push blocks and sticky pistons can pull many blocks back. Not every block can be moved, so test before hiding the door.
- [triggers: dispenser dropper|dispenser versus dropper|redstone item block] [topic: vanilla_dispenser_dropper] [tags: vanilla, redstone, dispenser] Targeted fact: Dispensers use certain items as actions, while droppers mostly eject items. Use the right mouth for the job.
- [triggers: hopper sorting|item sorter|storage sorter] [topic: vanilla_item_sorter_basic] [tags: vanilla, redstone, storage] Targeted fact: Hoppers move items and comparators can read them, enabling item sorters. Build one filter slice correctly before repeating it fifty times badly.
- [triggers: minecart hopper|hopper minecart|collect items farm] [topic: vanilla_hopper_minecart] [tags: vanilla, redstone, storage] Targeted fact: Hopper minecarts collect items through blocks and move on rails, making them useful under farms and cramped contraptions.
- [triggers: redstone clock|make clock circuit|automatic pulse] [topic: vanilla_redstone_clock_boundary] [tags: vanilla, redstone, clock] Targeted fact: Redstone clocks create repeated pulses. Keep them switchable and server-friendly; constant ticking can annoy both machines and neighbors.

## Storage And Inventory
- [triggers: organize storage|storage room|sort chests] [topic: vanilla_storage_organization] [tags: vanilla, storage, building] Targeted fact: Sort storage by blocks, ores, plants, mob drops, tools, magic items, food, and overflow. Labels save more lives than pride admits.
- [triggers: barrels versus chests|barrel chest difference|storage blocks] [topic: vanilla_barrel_chest] [tags: vanilla, storage, containers] Targeted fact: Chests need open space above unless trapped or special rules apply; barrels open in tight spaces and serve as fisherman job blocks.
- [triggers: shulker storage|use shulker boxes|portable storage] [topic: vanilla_shulker_storage] [tags: vanilla, storage, shulker] Targeted fact: Shulker boxes keep contents when broken, making them premier portable storage after End city work. Color and name them to prevent pocket chaos.
- [triggers: ender chest storage|ender chest use|private chest] [topic: vanilla_ender_chest_storage] [tags: vanilla, storage, ender chest] Targeted fact: Ender chests share a private inventory per player. Silk Touch retrieves the chest itself; otherwise it drops obsidian.
- [triggers: bundle use|bundles|small item storage] [topic: vanilla_bundle_storage] [tags: vanilla, storage, bundle] Targeted fact: Bundles help manage mixed small stacks when available in your version or pack. They do not replace shulker boxes, but they calm clutter.

## Building Practicalities
- [triggers: starter base|build first base|safe base] [topic: vanilla_starter_base] [tags: vanilla, building, base] Targeted fact: A starter base needs light, bed, storage, crafting, furnace, food access, mine entrance, and a door that does not open straight into regret.
- [triggers: spawn proofing|stop mobs spawning|light up base] [topic: vanilla_spawn_proofing_v3] [tags: vanilla, building, mobs] Targeted fact: Spawn-proof with light, slabs, buttons, carpets, transparent blocks, or other nonspawn surfaces depending on rules. Darkness is a tenant if you let it sign.
- [triggers: lighting options|torches lanterns glowstone|base light] [topic: vanilla_lighting_options] [tags: vanilla, building, light] Targeted fact: Torches are cheap, lanterns compact, glowstone and sea lanterns bright, candles atmospheric, and lava dangerous. Choose light that matches purpose.
- [triggers: roof overhang|mob proof roof|spider proof walls] [topic: vanilla_mob_proof_building] [tags: vanilla, building, defense] Targeted fact: Walls stop walkers, overhangs slow spiders, lighting stops many spawns, and doors matter more when zombies arrive with opinions.
- [triggers: lightning rod|protect build lightning|copper rod] [topic: vanilla_lightning_rod_v3] [tags: vanilla, building, lightning] Targeted fact: Lightning rods draw lightning strikes in range and help protect flammable builds. Copper that saves roofs earns its polish.
- [triggers: scaffold use|bamboo scaffolding|build tall safely] [topic: vanilla_scaffolding] [tags: vanilla, building, scaffold] Targeted fact: Scaffolding is climbed, extended, and broken for temporary building access. It is safer than dirt towers if used with sense.
- [triggers: concrete powder|make concrete|colored concrete] [topic: vanilla_concrete] [tags: vanilla, building, concrete] Targeted fact: Concrete powder becomes concrete when touching water. Do not confuse dry powder with finished building blocks in gravity-prone designs.
- [triggers: terracotta glazed|colored blocks|building palette] [topic: vanilla_building_palette] [tags: vanilla, building, palette] Targeted fact: Mix wood, stone, terracotta, concrete, copper, wool, and plants for readable builds. A good palette is a spell against ugliness.
- [triggers: safe bridge|bridge over lava|bridge over void] [topic: vanilla_safe_bridging] [tags: vanilla, building, safety] Targeted fact: Bridge crouched, use guard rails when possible, carry enough blocks, and do not let arrows, ghasts, or hubris choose your footing.
- [triggers: fire spread|flammable blocks|build with wood near lava] [topic: vanilla_fire_spread_building] [tags: vanilla, building, fire] Targeted fact: Wood near lava or open flame is a future story told by ashes. Use nonflammable blocks around heat, portals, and fireplaces.
