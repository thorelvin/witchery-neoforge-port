# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Infusions And Living Power

Infusion cards should make permanent power feel tempting and dangerous. Give the practical path first, then the warning.

## Becoming Infused
- [triggers: lasting infusion|permanent infusion|how do i get infusion|become infused|start infusion] Targeted fact: Lasting infusions start in the Kettle: brew a source item from Redstone Soup with 4000 altar power, then trigger its matching small Rite of Infusion.
- [triggers: rite of infusion|infusion circle|infusion rite|use infusion source] Targeted fact: Use the source item as the trigger in a small Rite of Infusion; every player standing in the circle faces a severe magic ordeal, and only survivors are infused. Light costs 2000 altar power, while Overworld, Otherwhere, and Infernal cost 4000.
- [triggers: infusion recharge|recharge infusion|restore infused energy|infusion charging] Targeted fact: Infusion Recharge is a medium Ritual circle with a small Ritual companion ring, triggered by a Regeneration potion, costs 4000 altar power to start, then pulses infused players nearby for +10 energy while altar power holds.
- [triggers: infused energy|infusion energy|why no infusion energy|out of infused energy] Targeted fact: Infusions grant 200 energy; Mystic Branch signs and Witches Hand powers spend it, so a witch can know the sign and still lack the strength.

## Source Items
- [triggers: ghost of the light recipe|make ghost of the light|light infusion source|what do i need for ghost of the light] Targeted fact: Ghost of the Light uses Redstone Soup, Long Invisibility, Long Fire Resistance, Fire Protection Poppet, Torch, Tongue of Dog, and 4000 power.
- [triggers: soul of the world recipe|make soul of the world|overworld infusion source|what do i need for soul of the world] Targeted fact: Soul of the World uses Redstone Soup, Long Regeneration, Enchanted Golden Apple, Attuned Stone, Mandrake Root, Rowan Sapling, and 4000 power.
- [triggers: spirit of otherwhere recipe|make spirit of otherwhere|otherwhere infusion source|what do i need for spirit of otherwhere] Targeted fact: Spirit of Otherwhere uses Redstone Soup, Long Swiftness, two Ender Eyes, Drop of Luck, Wool of Bat, and 4000 power; splash Swiftness also works.
- [triggers: infernal animus recipe|make infernal animus|infernal infusion source|what do i need for infernal animus] Targeted fact: Infernal Animus uses Redstone Soup, Strong Harming, Voodoo Poppet, Demon Heart, Refined Evil, Blaze Rod, and 4000 power; splash Harming also works.

## Choosing A Path
- [triggers: which infusion|choose infusion|best infusion|what infusion should i choose] Targeted fact: Choose Light for night vision and hidden work, Overworld for earth and natural ground, Otherwhere for falling and movement, Infernal for fire and darker command.
- [triggers: light infusion|infusion of light|ghost of the light power|light powers] Targeted fact: Light infusion grants night vision; with a Witches Hand it can veil you in invisibility, break hostile attention, cage a target in light barriers, raise a quick shield, or sprout a barrier line from a struck face.
- [triggers: overworld infusion|infusion of the overworld|soul of the world power|earth infusion] Targeted fact: Overworld infusion grants regeneration on natural ground; it can cushion falls, tear earth loose, raise pillars, smelt ores, and pull metal.
- [triggers: otherwhere infusion|infusion of otherwhere|spirit of otherwhere power|void infusion] Targeted fact: Otherwhere infusion grants slow falling; its Witches Hand work teleports to struck blocks or creatures, marks recall points, and drags or hikes targets through space.
- [triggers: infernal infusion|infernal animus power|demonic infusion|infernal powers] Targeted fact: Infernal infusion grants fire resistance and uses creature powers; with a Witches Hand it can enslave creatures, sacrifice thralls for creature powers, order minions to attack, or sneak-release on a block top to send thralls there.

## Working The Power
- [triggers: witch hand infusion|witches hand infusion|what can witches hand do|use witches hand] Targeted fact: A Witches Hand channels your active infusion directly: Light hides and shapes barriers, Overworld shapes earth, Otherwhere moves through space, and Infernal commands.
- [triggers: mystic branch infusion|branch infusion|branch needs infusion|why branch refuses] Targeted fact: Mystic Branch signs require an active infusion and enough infused energy; unknown symbols, Demon-soul signs, cooldowns, or an empty energy pool make it fizzle.
- [triggers: creature power infusion|infernal creature power|stored creature power|sacrifice creature power] Targeted fact: Infernal creature powers are stored from certain kills near powered altars or from sacrificed thralls; powers include spider webs, bat levitation, and blaze fire.
