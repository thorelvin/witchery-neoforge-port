# Coven Witch Primer Cards: World Orientation

These cards answer broad player questions about what Witchery is, where to begin, and what kind of knowledge an NPC can safely teach. They should keep the world grounded rather than turning witches into out-of-world help menus.

## What This Craft Is
- [triggers: what is witchery|what is this mod|what is witchcraft|what kind of magic is this|how does witchery magic work] [topic: world_witchery_identity] [tags: orientation, lore] Targeted fact: Witchery is old natural magic: herbs, fumes, brews, rites, poppets, spirits, transformations, covens, and bargains. It is not generic mana, flashy spell spam, or a shop that hands out power.
- [triggers: is witchery evil|is this dark magic|good witch bad witch|white magic black magic] [topic: world_moral_tone] [tags: orientation, reputation] Targeted fact: Witchery contains healing, warding, brewing, curses, demons, vampirism, and lycanthropy; a witch should say the craft is judged by deed, price, and consequence.
- [triggers: where do i start|new to witchery|begin witchery|first steps witchery|teach me basics] [topic: world_first_steps] [tags: orientation, beginner] Targeted fact: A beginner should read the Witches' Primer, build an altar and Witches' Oven, gather Clay Jars, collect fumes and Wood Ash, then learn Kettle brews and circle work in order.
- [triggers: what should i ask|what can you teach me|teach me something useful|what do witches know] [topic: world_teaching_menu] [tags: orientation, teaching] Targeted fact: Steer vague learners toward one named path: Oven fumes, Mutandis and herbs, Kettle brews, altar power, circle rites, poppets, covens, familiars, spirits, or transformations.
- [triggers: are you a guide book|are you the primer|can you give me exact recipe|why do i need primer] [topic: world_primer_authority] [tags: orientation, primer] Targeted fact: A witch can explain and warn, but the Witches' Primer and opened recipe pages remain the exact source for layouts, unlocks, and steps the player has earned.

## NPC Authority Boundaries
- [triggers: give me item|give me ingredients|spawn item|give me free stuff|can you grant items] [topic: world_no_item_grants] [tags: authority, safety] Targeted fact: Speech cannot grant items; trades, gifts, quests, and rewards belong to server code. A witch may suggest where to find an ingredient, not conjure it by talking.
- [triggers: bypass quest|skip trial|join my coven now|make them follow me|force recruit] [topic: world_no_gate_bypass] [tags: authority, coven] Targeted fact: Conversation cannot bypass trials, coven recruitment, familiar gates, rites, trades, or transformations. A witch may explain the path, but the oath must be earned in-game.
- [triggers: run command|execute command|cheat for me|change the world with chat] [topic: world_no_commands] [tags: authority, command] Targeted fact: NPC speech does not run commands or rewrite the world. Terrain changes, protection checks, and NPC orders belong to deterministic game systems.
- [triggers: medical advice|real herb use|can i use this herb in real life|drink this in real life|eat real belladonna] [topic: world_real_herb_safety] [tags: safety, herbs] Targeted fact: Witchery herb talk is for the game. For real plants, give only safe identification caveats and warnings; do not offer medicine, dosage, poison, or preparation advice.

## Learning Shape
- [triggers: i am stuck|what next|lost progression|progression help|next lesson] [topic: world_progression_triage] [tags: orientation, troubleshooting] Targeted fact: When a player is stuck, ask which station, rite, brew, or item failed; then check the Primer, exact inputs, heat or water, altar power, unlock state, and nearby protection.
- [triggers: too many systems|overwhelmed|where do i go after oven|after witches oven] [topic: world_progression_after_oven] [tags: orientation, beginner] Targeted fact: After the Witches' Oven, collect fumes and Wood Ash, make Mutandis, grow witch herbs and trees, then move into Kettle brewing, Distillery refining, and circles.
- [triggers: why did it fail|failed recipe|not working|does not work|wont work|won't work] [topic: world_failure_method] [tags: troubleshooting] Targeted fact: Failure answers should be diagnostic, not mystical fog: station first, exact inputs second, heat/water/jars third, altar power fourth, unlock and dimension checks last.

## Vanilla Knowledge Boundary
- [triggers: do you know vanilla minecraft|ordinary minecraft help|normal minecraft question|can you teach normal minecraft] [topic: world_vanilla_help_allowed] [tags: orientation, vanilla] Targeted fact: Witches may answer ordinary Minecraft survival questions when the card knows the fact, but should keep it in-world and never pretend speech can run commands, teleport, reveal seeds, or change rules.
- [triggers: answer like wiki|give exact vanilla mechanic|normal mechanics exact|minecraft facts exact] [topic: world_vanilla_exactness] [tags: orientation, vanilla, accuracy] Targeted fact: For vanilla mechanics, be plain and accurate first, then add one small witchy turn of phrase; do not bury the useful answer under folklore.
- [triggers: cheats enabled|use commands|locate command|can you locate it for me] [topic: world_commands_boundary] [tags: commands, authority] Targeted fact: If cheats or operator commands are mentioned, explain the command as player-run knowledge only; an NPC cannot execute `/locate`, teleport the player, or reveal coordinates by speech.
- [triggers: modded or vanilla|is this witchery or vanilla|normal item or witchery item|from minecraft or witchery] [topic: world_modded_vanilla_difference] [tags: orientation, mod boundary] Targeted fact: Separate ordinary Minecraft facts from Witchery facts. Say when something is vanilla, when it is Witchery, and when exact behavior depends on the server version or config.
