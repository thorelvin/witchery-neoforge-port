# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Potions And Enchantments Advanced

These cards distinguish vanilla brewing/enchanting from Witchery brews and rites. Keep practical facts exact.

## Vanilla Brewing
- [triggers: brewing stand basics|how brew potions|vanilla brewing] [topic: vanilla_brewing_stand_basics] [tags: vanilla, brewing, basics] Targeted fact: Vanilla brewing uses a brewing stand, bottles, water, blaze powder fuel, base ingredients, effect ingredients, and modifiers. It is not the Witchery Kettle.
- [triggers: awkward potion|nether wart potion|base potion] [topic: vanilla_awkward_potion_v3] [tags: vanilla, brewing, base] Targeted fact: Nether wart brewed into water bottles makes Awkward Potions, the usual base for many effect potions.
- [triggers: mundane potion|thick potion|wrong base potion] [topic: vanilla_base_potion_wrong] [tags: vanilla, brewing, troubleshooting] Targeted fact: Mundane and Thick potions are usually wrong bases for common effects. If a potion fails, check ingredient order before blaming spirits.
- [triggers: extend potion|redstone potion|longer potion] [topic: vanilla_potion_redstone] [tags: vanilla, brewing, modifier] Targeted fact: Redstone dust commonly extends potion duration when the potion supports it. Longer is not always stronger.
- [triggers: strengthen potion|glowstone potion|level two potion] [topic: vanilla_potion_glowstone] [tags: vanilla, brewing, modifier] Targeted fact: Glowstone dust strengthens many potions to a higher level when supported, often reducing duration. Power is a trade, as usual.
- [triggers: splash potion|gunpowder potion|throw potion] [topic: vanilla_splash_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, splash] Targeted fact: Gunpowder turns many potions into splash potions. It makes healing generous, harming rude, and mistakes public.
- [triggers: lingering potion|dragon breath potion|cloud potion] [topic: vanilla_lingering_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, lingering] Targeted fact: Dragon's Breath can turn splash potions into lingering potions, leaving an effect cloud. Bottle the breath after the dragon fight, not during panic.
- [triggers: fire resistance potion|brew fire resistance|magma cream potion] [topic: vanilla_fire_resistance_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, fire resistance] Targeted fact: Fire Resistance potions use magma cream from a proper base and are excellent for Nether lava, blazes, and embarrassing feet.
- [triggers: night vision potion|brew night vision|golden carrot potion] [topic: vanilla_night_vision_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, night vision] Targeted fact: Night Vision potions use golden carrots from a proper base and help caves, oceans, ancient cities, and night scouting.
- [triggers: water breathing potion|brew water breathing|pufferfish potion] [topic: vanilla_water_breathing_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, water breathing] Targeted fact: Water Breathing potions use pufferfish from a proper base and help ocean monuments, ruins, and underwater building.
- [triggers: weakness potion|brew weakness|zombie villager cure] [topic: vanilla_weakness_potion_v3] [tags: vanilla, brewing, weakness] Targeted fact: Weakness is needed for curing zombie villagers with a golden apple; check the recipe data because Weakness has special brewing paths.
- [triggers: potion of healing|brew healing|glistering melon] [topic: vanilla_healing_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, healing] Targeted fact: Healing potions use glistering melon from a proper base. Splash healing harms some undead while helping the living.
- [triggers: potion of harming|brew harming|fermented spider eye] [topic: vanilla_harming_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, harming] Targeted fact: Harming potions come from corrupting certain potions with fermented spider eye. Handle splash versions like knives.
- [triggers: invisibility potion|brew invisibility|fermented spider night vision] [topic: vanilla_invisibility_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, invisibility] Targeted fact: Invisibility is commonly made by corrupting Night Vision with fermented spider eye. Armor and held items can still betray you.
- [triggers: slow falling potion|brew slow falling|phantom membrane potion] [topic: vanilla_slow_falling_potion] [tags: vanilla, brewing, slow falling] Targeted fact: Slow Falling potions use phantom membrane from a proper base and are excellent for dragon fights, towers, cliffs, and bad ladders.

## Enchanting
- [triggers: enchanting table setup|level 30 enchanting|bookshelves enchanting] [topic: vanilla_enchanting_setup_deep] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, setup] Targeted fact: A strong enchanting setup uses bookshelves placed correctly around an enchanting table, plus lapis and levels. The table likes libraries more than empty rooms.
- [triggers: anvil combine books|combine enchantments|anvil too expensive] [topic: vanilla_anvil_combining] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, anvil] Targeted fact: Anvils combine and rename gear but add prior-work cost. Plan book order to avoid "too expensive" repairs later.
- [triggers: grindstone use|remove enchantments|disenchant item] [topic: vanilla_grindstone_v3] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, grindstone] Targeted fact: Grindstones remove most enchantments and return some experience, but curses stay stubborn. Some mistakes are sticky.
- [triggers: mending source|get mending|mending villager] [topic: vanilla_mending_source] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, mending] Targeted fact: Mending is treasure-enchantment territory usually chased through villagers, loot, or fishing depending on version and rules. It repairs gear with experience.
- [triggers: unbreaking enchantment|unbreaking useful|durability enchant] [topic: vanilla_unbreaking_v3] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, durability] Targeted fact: Unbreaking makes tools and armor consume durability less often. It is not glamorous, which is why it survives.
- [triggers: protection types|protection fire blast projectile|best protection] [topic: vanilla_protection_types] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, armor] Targeted fact: Protection is general, while Fire, Blast, and Projectile Protection specialize. Choose for the danger you actually face.
- [triggers: sharpness smite bane|weapon enchant choice|best sword enchant] [topic: vanilla_weapon_enchants] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, weapon] Targeted fact: Sharpness is broad melee damage, Smite targets undead, and Bane targets arthropods. Choose by enemy, not by name drama.
- [triggers: fortune versus silk touch|fortune silk touch choose|mining enchant] [topic: vanilla_fortune_silk_choice] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, mining] Targeted fact: Fortune increases drops from many ores; Silk Touch preserves blocks. They conflict on many tools, so keep separate picks.
- [triggers: efficiency pickaxe|fast mining enchant|tool speed] [topic: vanilla_efficiency] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, tool] Targeted fact: Efficiency speeds mining with the right tool. Fast tools are wonderful until they mine the block you stood on.
- [triggers: respiration aqua affinity|helmet water enchants|underwater enchant] [topic: vanilla_water_enchants] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, water] Targeted fact: Respiration extends underwater breathing and Aqua Affinity improves underwater mining. They make monuments and drowned ruins less miserable.
- [triggers: feather falling|fall damage boots|best boots enchant] [topic: vanilla_feather_falling] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, boots] Targeted fact: Feather Falling reduces fall damage and is valuable for caves, End cities, elytra mistakes, and any tower built by confidence.
- [triggers: depth strider frost walker|water boots|ice boots] [topic: vanilla_boots_movement] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, boots] Targeted fact: Depth Strider helps underwater movement; Frost Walker freezes water underfoot. They conflict, because boots too have opinions.
- [triggers: loyalty riptide channeling|trident enchants|best trident] [topic: vanilla_trident_enchants] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, trident] Targeted fact: Loyalty returns thrown tridents, Riptide launches the player in water or rain, and Channeling calls lightning during thunderstorms when conditions allow.
- [triggers: infinity versus mending bow|bow enchant choice|best bow] [topic: vanilla_bow_enchants_v3] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, bow] Targeted fact: Infinity saves arrows and Mending repairs with experience, but they conflict on bows. Choose between endless arrows and long-lived craft.
- [triggers: curse of binding|curse of vanishing|cursed gear] [topic: vanilla_curse_enchants] [tags: vanilla, enchanting, curse] Targeted fact: Curse of Binding sticks worn gear, and Curse of Vanishing destroys items on death. Read loot before wearing jokes from old chests.
