# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Nether Deep Pack

These cards deepen Nether knowledge for coven witches, flame imps, guarded specialists, and exact vanilla teaching contexts.

## Portal Craft And Travel
- [triggers: exact nether portal|portal frame exact|nether portal dimensions] [topic: vanilla_nether_portal_exact] [tags: vanilla, nether, portal] Targeted fact: A Nether portal frame is a vertical rectangle, minimum 4x5 and maximum 23x23 outside dimensions; player-built corners are optional, but the interior must be open and lit.
- [triggers: cast nether portal|lava bucket portal|speedrun portal] [topic: vanilla_cast_portal] [tags: vanilla, nether, portal] Targeted fact: Casting a portal uses water and lava to form obsidian in place. It saves diamond pickaxe time but punishes sloppy bucket work.
- [triggers: portal wont light|why portal not working|nether portal failed] [topic: vanilla_portal_troubleshooting] [tags: vanilla, nether, portal] Targeted fact: Check for missing obsidian, wrong shape, blocked interior, water, crying obsidian, horizontal frame, or an invalid dimension if a portal will not light.
- [triggers: ghast relight portal|trapped in nether|no flint steel nether] [topic: vanilla_trapped_nether_relight] [tags: vanilla, nether, survival] Targeted fact: A dead Nether portal can be relit with flint and steel, fire charge, or sometimes a ghast fireball. Carry relighting tools before learning humility.
- [triggers: safe nether portal room|protect portal nether|portal shelter] [topic: vanilla_nether_portal_shelter] [tags: vanilla, nether, build] Targeted fact: Build a portal shelter from nonflammable blocks, mark coordinates, add doors or gates, and keep a chest with emergency flint and steel, food, and blocks.
- [triggers: nether travel fast|nether highway|use nether for travel] [topic: vanilla_nether_highway_v3] [tags: vanilla, nether, coordinates] Targeted fact: Because Nether horizontal travel maps roughly 8:1 to the Overworld, safe tunnels or bridges can shorten long journeys dramatically.
- [triggers: nether roof|bedrock roof|nether ceiling] [topic: vanilla_nether_roof_boundary_v3] [tags: vanilla, nether, boundary] Targeted fact: Nether roof travel depends on server rules and mechanics that may be restricted. NPCs should not assume roof access is allowed; suggest ordinary safe tunnels first.

## Nether Biomes
- [triggers: nether wastes|basic nether biome|zombified piglins] [topic: vanilla_nether_wastes] [tags: vanilla, nether, biome] Targeted fact: Nether wastes are open, dangerous, and full of lava, ghasts, piglins, and zombified piglins. Build cover before sightseeing.
- [triggers: crimson forest|crimson biome|hoglin crimson] [topic: vanilla_crimson_forest] [tags: vanilla, nether, biome] Targeted fact: Crimson forests contain hoglins, piglins, fungus, and red wood. Bring gold armor, warped fungus for hoglins, and an exit route.
- [triggers: warped forest|warped biome|endermen nether] [topic: vanilla_warped_forest] [tags: vanilla, nether, biome] Targeted fact: Warped forests are safer from many Nether mobs and full of Endermen. Good for pearls, poor for careless eye contact.
- [triggers: basalt delta|basalt biome|magma cubes nether] [topic: vanilla_basalt_delta] [tags: vanilla, nether, biome] Targeted fact: Basalt deltas are jagged, dark, and crowded with magma cubes and lava pockets. Move slowly and bridge like the floor hates you.
- [triggers: soul sand valley|soul valley|skeleton ghast nether] [topic: vanilla_soul_sand_valley] [tags: vanilla, nether, biome] Targeted fact: Soul sand valleys slow movement and invite skeletons and ghasts. Soul speed helps, but walls and cover help more.
- [triggers: blue fire|soul fire|soul soil] [topic: vanilla_soul_fire] [tags: vanilla, nether, fire] Targeted fact: Soul fire burns blue on soul soil or soul sand and fits Nether craft and atmosphere; it is not a toy just because it is pretty.

## Fortresses And Bastions
- [triggers: find nether fortress|nether fortress search|fortress direction] [topic: vanilla_find_fortress] [tags: vanilla, nether, fortress] Targeted fact: Nether fortresses generate in the Nether as long bridge-and-corridor structures. Search broad Nether regions, mark coordinates, and avoid crossing lava without blocks.
- [triggers: blaze spawner|blaze rod farm|blaze rod source] [topic: vanilla_blaze_spawner] [tags: vanilla, nether, blaze] Targeted fact: Blaze rods come from blazes, often around fortress spawners. Bring fire resistance when possible, a shield, blocks, and a plan to avoid being knocked into lava.
- [triggers: nether wart|find nether wart|where nether wart grows] [topic: vanilla_nether_wart_source] [tags: vanilla, nether, brewing] Targeted fact: Nether wart is found in fortress stair gardens and some bastion areas. It is the usual base for awkward potions in brewing stands.
- [triggers: wither skeleton|wither skulls|fortress skeletons] [topic: vanilla_wither_skeletons_v3] [tags: vanilla, nether, wither] Targeted fact: Wither skeletons spawn in fortresses and can inflict Wither. Use height, shields, Smite, and careful corridors if skull hunting.
- [triggers: find bastion|bastion remnant|piglin bastion] [topic: vanilla_bastion_find] [tags: vanilla, nether, bastion] Targeted fact: Bastions are piglin structures with gold, loot, brutes, and steep punishment. Wearing gold helps with piglins, but brutes remain hostile.
- [triggers: piglin brute|brutes attack|bastion brute] [topic: vanilla_piglin_brute_v3] [tags: vanilla, nether, bastion] Targeted fact: Piglin brutes do not care about your gold manners. Treat them as elite guards, not bartering partners.
- [triggers: piglin bartering|barter gold|what piglins trade] [topic: vanilla_piglin_bartering] [tags: vanilla, nether, piglin] Targeted fact: Toss gold ingots to adult piglins to barter. Do it from safety, because greed with tusks is still greed.
- [triggers: opening chests piglins|piglins angry chest|mine gold near piglins] [topic: vanilla_piglin_anger] [tags: vanilla, nether, piglin] Targeted fact: Piglins dislike players opening containers or mining gold near them. Gold armor is courtesy, not permission.
- [triggers: respawn anchor|sleep in nether|bed explodes nether] [topic: vanilla_respawn_anchor_beds] [tags: vanilla, nether, respawn] Targeted fact: Beds explode in the Nether. Use charged respawn anchors for Nether respawn points, and respect glowstone like it is life insurance.

- [triggers: netherite upgrade|smithing template netherite|upgrade diamond netherite] [topic: vanilla_netherite_upgrade_v3] [tags: vanilla, netherite, smithing] Targeted fact: Modern netherite upgrading uses netherite ingots with a smithing template at a smithing table. Check current recipe data if a modpack changes it.
- [triggers: quartz|nether quartz|redstone quartz] [topic: vanilla_nether_quartz] [tags: vanilla, nether, quartz] Targeted fact: Nether quartz is common in the Nether and useful for comparators, observers, daylight sensors, and building. Mine it for experience and redstone craft.
- [triggers: glowstone|get glowstone|glowstone dust] [topic: vanilla_glowstone] [tags: vanilla, nether, lighting] Targeted fact: Glowstone hangs in the Nether and drops dust unless harvested with Silk Touch. Build up carefully; the floor below may be lava with opinions.
- [triggers: magma cream|magma cubes|fire resistance ingredient] [topic: vanilla_magma_cream] [tags: vanilla, brewing, nether] Targeted fact: Magma cream comes from magma cubes or crafting slimeball with blaze powder, and it is used for Fire Resistance potions.
