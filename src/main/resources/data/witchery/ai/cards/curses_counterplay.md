# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Curses And Counterplay

Curse cards should be wary and practical. Teach what the world does, but do not promise that mere talk can harm someone on command.

## Curse Shape
- [triggers: curse rite|curse ritual|curse someone|cast curse|how do curses work] Targeted fact: Most curse rites use a filled Taglock Kit on an Infernal circle; the offerings choose the curse, and wards may block it.
- [triggers: curse blocked|curse failed|curse protected|protective poppet negated curse] Targeted fact: Curse attempts can be stopped by Witch Hunter curse protection or by a bound Voodoo Protection Poppet; the target was protected.
- [triggers: stronger curse|curse potency|cat familiar curse|coven curse] Targeted fact: Curse potency can rise with nearby coven witches and an active cat familiar; spite grows teeth when the coven leans in.
- [triggers: remove curse details|curse cure details|which curse cure|curse treatment] Targeted fact: Cure rites use a filled Taglock Kit for the cursed player and 2000 altar power; choose offerings by curse type, because the wrong cure does nothing.

## Dangerous Work
- [triggers: corrupt poppet|curse corrupt poppet|destroy voodoo protection poppet] Targeted fact: Corrupt Poppet needs a cat familiar, filled taglock, Exhale, Blaze Powder, Spectral Dust, and a Voodoo Protection Poppet; Poppet Protection can bite back.
- [triggers: hell on earth|curse hell on earth|open hell] Targeted fact: Hell on Earth is large Infernal night work with Redstone Soup, Demon Heart, Waystone, Nether Star, and a villager; do not call it casual spite.
- [triggers: wolf curse|curse of the wolf|lycanthropy curse] Targeted fact: The wolf curse belongs to transformation progression and the full moon; do not treat it as a simple prank or casual promise.
- [triggers: remove vampirism|cure vampirism|vampire cure rite] Targeted fact: Vampire cure needs coven of six, cat familiar, filled taglock, Breath, Silver Sword, Garlic, Diamond, Brew of Love, and 10000 power; it may fizzle.

## Counterplay
- [triggers: protect from curse|stop curses|curse protection|ward against curses|blocked by poppet] Targeted fact: Best curse defense is bound Voodoo Protection Poppets, Poppet Protection backup, cat familiars, and Witch Hunter curse-protection gear.
- [triggers: voodoo protection poppet curse|voodoo protection blocks curse|protective poppet negated] Targeted fact: A bound Voodoo Protection Poppet can negate a curse and take damage; Corrupt Poppet is the counter to those wards.
- [triggers: witch hunter curse protection|hunter curse protection|hunter armor curses] Targeted fact: Witch Hunter gear has curse-protection set bonuses; silver and hunter gear matter most when curses, vampires, or werewolves enter the work.
- [triggers: seer stone curse|detect curse|am i cursed|what curse on me] Targeted fact: A Seer Stone can report tracked curses: Misfortune, Insanity, Sinking, Overheating, and Waking Nightmare; check before naming every shadow.
