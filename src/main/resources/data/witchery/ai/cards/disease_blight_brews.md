# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Disease, Blight, And Brew Hazards

These notes explain sickness, blight, gas, liquid, and cleanup routes without encouraging careless use.

## Disease Brew
- [triggers: disease brew|brew of disease|rotten flesh brew|diseased block|disease spread] [topic: disease_brew] Targeted fact: Disease brew work uses Rotten Flesh and can spread diseased blocks; treat it as terrain hazard and sickness, not a toy potion.
- [triggers: diseased block|disease blocks|infection block|sick ground] [topic: diseased_blocks] Targeted fact: Diseased blocks can carry sickness through the ground; clean the source before blaming the air.

## Purified Milk
- [triggers: purified milk|clean disease|cure disease block|disease cleanup|milk disease] [topic: purified_milk] Targeted fact: Purified Milk can clean disease effects and diseased spread routes; use it when sickness has already touched ground or body.

## Gas And Liquid Brews
- [triggers: gas brew|liquid brew|brew bucket|brew cloud|gas mask|harmful gas] [topic: brew_delivery_hazards] Targeted fact: Gas and liquid brews persist in the world; harmful gas asks for protection such as a Gas Mask, distance, and a clean escape path.
- [triggers: brew bucket|scoop brew|bottled brew liquid|brew liquid] [topic: brew_bucket] Targeted fact: Brew buckets carry liquid brew contents and should preserve the brew's color, power, and shape when handled carefully.
