# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: End Progression, Strongholds, And Elytra

These cards cover the route from Ender pearls to elytra. Be accurate and direct; this is common late-game player guidance.

## Finding The Stronghold
- [triggers: how get to the end|find end portal|find stronghold|go to end dimension] [topic: vanilla_end_path_overview] [tags: vanilla, end, progression] Targeted fact: To reach the End in survival, get ender pearls, blaze powder, craft Eyes of Ender, follow them to a stronghold, fill the End portal frames, and jump when ready.
- [triggers: eye of ender recipe|make eyes of ender|ender eye craft|blaze powder ender pearl] [topic: vanilla_eye_of_ender_recipe] [tags: vanilla, end, crafting] Targeted fact: An Eye of Ender is crafted from an ender pearl and blaze powder. Make extras because thrown eyes can break and portals may need many.
- [triggers: use eye of ender|throw eye of ender|eye points where|stronghold eye] [topic: vanilla_eye_of_ender_use] [tags: vanilla, end, stronghold] Targeted fact: Throw an Eye of Ender in the Overworld and follow where it flies; when it dives downward, you are near the stronghold and should dig carefully.
- [triggers: eye of ender broke|ender eye shattered|why eye disappeared|eye break chance] [topic: vanilla_eye_breaks] [tags: vanilla, end, stronghold] Targeted fact: A thrown Eye of Ender can fall for reuse or shatter. Bring more than the bare minimum unless you enjoy bargaining with probability.
- [triggers: stronghold portal room|find portal room|silverfish spawner|end portal lava] [topic: vanilla_portal_room] [tags: vanilla, end, stronghold] Targeted fact: The End portal room has a portal frame over lava and a silverfish spawner. Secure the room, block lava if needed, then fill empty frames.
- [triggers: how many eyes end portal|activate end portal|end portal needs eyes|12 eyes portal] [topic: vanilla_end_portal_eyes] [tags: vanilla, end, portal] Targeted fact: An End portal has 12 frame blocks total; some may already contain eyes, but every frame must be filled before the portal opens.
- [triggers: end portal in creative|make end portal creative|end portal frame direction] [topic: vanilla_creative_end_portal] [tags: vanilla, end, creative] Targeted fact: In creative, End portal frames must face inward correctly. In survival, players find frames in strongholds rather than crafting them.

## The Dragon And Outer End
- [triggers: ender dragon fight|beat dragon|dragon preparation|what bring to end] [topic: vanilla_dragon_prep] [tags: vanilla, end, boss] Targeted fact: For the dragon, bring good armor, bow, arrows, blocks, water, food, slow falling if possible, a carved pumpkin if Endermen trouble you, and courage that can build upward.
- [triggers: end crystals|destroy crystals|dragon healing|obsidian towers] [topic: vanilla_end_crystals] [tags: vanilla, end, boss] Targeted fact: End crystals on obsidian towers heal the dragon. Shoot or break them safely before trying to finish the fight.
- [triggers: endermen in end|avoid endermen|pumpkin endermen|water endermen] [topic: vanilla_endermen_end] [tags: vanilla, end, mobs] Targeted fact: Endermen swarm the End. Avoid staring, use water for safety, or wear a carved pumpkin if vision loss is better than being dismantled.
- [triggers: end gateway|outer islands|after dragon|where end cities] [topic: vanilla_end_gateway] [tags: vanilla, end, outer islands] Targeted fact: After the dragon dies, an End gateway can send players to the outer End islands where chorus plants, End cities, shulkers, and elytra await.
- [triggers: cross end islands|void bridge|ender pearl gateway|end island travel] [topic: vanilla_outer_end_travel] [tags: vanilla, end, travel] Targeted fact: Outer End travel needs blocks, pearls, water, rockets once you have elytra, and respect for the void. The void accepts no apologies.

## End Cities And Elytra
- [triggers: find end city|where end cities|end city search|outer island city] [topic: vanilla_end_city_find] [tags: vanilla, end, end city] Targeted fact: End cities generate on the outer End islands, usually on larger islands with chorus plants. Travel across islands or use gateways until purpur towers show themselves.
- [triggers: end ship|find end ship|which end cities have elytra|ship by end city] [topic: vanilla_end_ship] [tags: vanilla, end, elytra] Targeted fact: Elytra are in End ships, the floating ships that can generate beside some End cities. Not every city has a ship, so keep searching.
- [triggers: get elytra|where elytra|elytra location|find wings minecraft] [topic: vanilla_elytra_location] [tags: vanilla, end, elytra] Targeted fact: Elytra are found in an item frame in the treasure room of an End ship, guarded by a shulker and near valuable chests.
- [triggers: use elytra|how fly elytra|elytra controls|start gliding] [topic: vanilla_elytra_use] [tags: vanilla, elytra, flight] Targeted fact: Equip elytra in the chest slot and jump/fall to glide. They do not make height by themselves; rockets, cliffs, towers, or patience provide lift.
- [triggers: fireworks elytra|rocket boost elytra|fly with rockets|elytra rockets] [topic: vanilla_elytra_rockets] [tags: vanilla, elytra, rockets] Targeted fact: Plain firework rockets boost elytra flight. Avoid fireworks with explosive stars for travel unless pain is the intended destination.
- [triggers: repair elytra|elytra durability|mending elytra|unbreaking elytra] [topic: vanilla_elytra_repair] [tags: vanilla, elytra, enchanting] Targeted fact: Elytra have durability and can break. Mending and Unbreaking make them far safer; repair before the sky becomes a grave.
- [triggers: shulker shell|shulker box|why end city|end city loot] [topic: vanilla_shulker_boxes] [tags: vanilla, end, storage] Targeted fact: Shulkers drop shells used for shulker boxes, portable storage that keeps contents. End cities are worth the bruises for that alone.
- [triggers: dragon head|end ship head|collect dragon head|ship bow] [topic: vanilla_dragon_head] [tags: vanilla, end, cosmetic] Targeted fact: End ships carry a dragon head at the bow. It is trophy, mask, and warning: someone looked at the impossible and brought back its face.
