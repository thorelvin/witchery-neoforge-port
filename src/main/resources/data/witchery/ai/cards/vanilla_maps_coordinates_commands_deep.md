# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Maps, Coordinates, And Commands

These cards teach navigation without giving fake coordinates or pretending NPC speech can inspect the seed.

## Coordinates And Orientation
- [triggers: coordinates|how use coordinates|what are coords|xyz meaning] [topic: vanilla_coordinates_xyz] [tags: vanilla, coordinates, exact] Targeted fact: Coordinates are X east-west, Y height, and Z north-south. Write important places down before wandering; memory is a poor compass.
- [triggers: f3 screen|debug screen|show coordinates|turn on coords] [topic: vanilla_show_coordinates] [tags: vanilla, coordinates, exact] Targeted fact: In Java, the debug screen can show coordinates if allowed. On servers, rules or gamerules may differ, so use maps, landmarks, and written notes as backup.
- [triggers: positive x negative x|positive z negative z|which way coords] [topic: vanilla_coordinate_directions] [tags: vanilla, coordinates, exact] Targeted fact: X changes east-west and Z changes north-south. The exact compass naming is less important than recording portal, base, and death coordinates accurately.
- [triggers: y level|height coordinate|what is y] [topic: vanilla_y_level] [tags: vanilla, coordinates, exact] Targeted fact: Y is height: high Y climbs into sky and mountains, low Y digs toward deepslate, lava danger, ancient cities, and diamonds.
- [triggers: lost in minecraft|how get home|lost from base|find my base] [topic: vanilla_lost_get_home] [tags: vanilla, navigation, exact] Targeted fact: If lost, climb safely, mark coordinates, use compass for world spawn, follow roads or rivers, check maps, build landmarks, and avoid panic travel at night.
- [triggers: death coordinates|lost items after death|find death spot] [topic: vanilla_death_recovery_v3] [tags: vanilla, navigation, exact] Targeted fact: After death, record coordinates if visible, return with spare gear, food, blocks, and fewer valuables; items despawn after time in loaded chunks.
- [triggers: nether coordinates|nether travel coordinates|divide by eight] [topic: vanilla_nether_coordinate_conversion] [tags: vanilla, nether, coordinates, exact] Targeted fact: Nether horizontal travel scales roughly 8:1 with the Overworld: divide Overworld X and Z by 8 for Nether portal planning, then build safely.
- [triggers: portal linking|link nether portals|portals wrong place] [topic: vanilla_portal_linking_basic] [tags: vanilla, nether, coordinates, exact] Targeted fact: Nether portals search for nearby matching portals; bad linking usually means the Nether-side and Overworld-side coordinates are too far from their proper 8:1 match.
- [triggers: mark a trail|breadcrumbs|not get lost cave|cave navigation] [topic: vanilla_trail_marking] [tags: vanilla, navigation, exact] Targeted fact: Mark trails with torches on one side, signs, blocks, or arrows. In caves, place torches consistently so the way back has a pattern.
- [triggers: map wall|use maps|cartography table maps|expand map] [topic: vanilla_map_basics] [tags: vanilla, maps, exact] Targeted fact: Paper maps show explored terrain and can be expanded or copied with cartography work. They do not show every hidden cave or solve bad judgment.
- [triggers: locator map|map marker|player marker map] [topic: vanilla_locator_map] [tags: vanilla, maps, scoped] Targeted fact: Locator-style maps can show player position depending on edition and map type. If the marker is off-map, travel until the marker moves inward.
- [triggers: banner map marker|mark map banner|name place on map] [topic: vanilla_map_banners_java] [tags: vanilla, maps, java] Targeted fact: In Java, named banners can mark maps when used correctly. Use them for bases, portals, villages, and places where foolishness happened.

## Compasses And Lodestones
- [triggers: compass points where|how compass works|compass spawn] [topic: vanilla_compass_spawn] [tags: vanilla, compass, exact] Targeted fact: A normal compass points to world spawn, not your bed or base. If your base is elsewhere, mark coordinates or use maps and signs.
- [triggers: lodestone compass|make compass point base|nether compass] [topic: vanilla_lodestone_compass] [tags: vanilla, compass, exact] Targeted fact: Use a compass on a lodestone to bind it to that lodestone, letting the needle point to a chosen place even outside normal spawn logic.
- [triggers: recovery compass|find death compass|echo shard compass] [topic: vanilla_recovery_compass] [tags: vanilla, compass, death] Targeted fact: A recovery compass points toward the location of the player's last death when usable; it is help, not permission to die carelessly.
- [triggers: compass in nether|compass end spin|why compass spins] [topic: vanilla_compass_dimension_limit] [tags: vanilla, compass, dimensions] Targeted fact: Normal compasses misbehave outside the Overworld unless tied to a lodestone. Do not navigate the Nether by a drunken needle.

## Explorer Maps
- [triggers: explorer map|how explorer maps work|structure map] [topic: vanilla_explorer_map_basics] [tags: vanilla, maps, structures] Targeted fact: Explorer maps point toward certain structures from trade sources. Follow the marker, but prepare for long travel and terrain that resists straight lines.
- [triggers: woodland explorer map|mansion explorer map|cartographer woodland] [topic: vanilla_woodland_explorer_map] [tags: vanilla, maps, mansion] Targeted fact: Woodland explorer maps come from cartographers when trades allow and point toward woodland mansions, which may still be far away.
- [triggers: ocean explorer map|monument map|cartographer ocean map] [topic: vanilla_ocean_explorer_map] [tags: vanilla, maps, monument] Targeted fact: Ocean explorer maps lead toward ocean monuments. Bring boat, water-breathing plan, and respect for guardians.
- [triggers: trial explorer map|trial chamber map|cartographer trial map] [topic: vanilla_trial_explorer_map] [tags: vanilla, maps, trial chamber] Targeted fact: Trial chamber explorer maps can be bought from qualified cartographers in current versions; they point to underground copper-and-tuff trial structures.
- [triggers: treasure map|buried treasure map|shipwreck map] [topic: vanilla_buried_treasure_map_v3] [tags: vanilla, maps, treasure] Targeted fact: Buried treasure maps often come from shipwrecks and underwater ruins. Line up the marker, dig around the X, and watch your breath.

## Commands When Asked
- [triggers: locate command|how use locate|slash locate structure] [topic: vanilla_locate_command_general] [tags: vanilla, commands, cheats] Targeted fact: With cheats or operator permission, `/locate structure <name>` can report a structure direction or coordinates. NPCs may explain it but cannot execute it.
- [triggers: locate mansion command|command find woodland mansion|slash locate mansion] [topic: vanilla_locate_mansion_command] [tags: vanilla, commands, mansion] Targeted fact: With cheats, use a locate-structure command for a mansion. Without cheats, use a cartographer's woodland explorer map.
- [triggers: locate stronghold command|command find stronghold|slash locate stronghold] [topic: vanilla_locate_stronghold_command] [tags: vanilla, commands, stronghold] Targeted fact: With cheats, locate commands can find strongholds, but NPCs cannot run them. In survival, craft Eyes of Ender and follow them in the Overworld.
- [triggers: locate biome command|find biome command|slash locate biome] [topic: vanilla_locate_biome_command] [tags: vanilla, commands, biome] Targeted fact: With cheats, `/locate biome` can help find biomes. In ordinary survival, use climate clues, maps, Nether travel, and patient exploration.
- [triggers: teleport command|tp command|npc teleport me] [topic: vanilla_teleport_boundary] [tags: vanilla, commands, boundary] Targeted fact: Teleporting is server authority, not NPC speech. Explain that operators or cheats control it, then offer survival travel methods.
