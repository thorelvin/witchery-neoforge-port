# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Mining And Resources

These cards teach ordinary resource paths. Avoid over-specific ore distribution promises if the server version may differ; give practical bands and caveats.

## Mining Progression
- [triggers: mining progression|what ore first|ore order|how to get iron diamond] [topic: vanilla_mining_progression] [tags: vanilla, mining, progression] Targeted fact: Mine stone first, then coal or charcoal for light, iron for shields and buckets, diamond for long-term tools, then Nether materials when you can survive heat and spite.
- [triggers: find coal|coal ore|need coal|early fuel] [topic: vanilla_find_coal] [tags: vanilla, mining, coal] Targeted fact: Coal appears in stone-rich terrain and mountains, but charcoal from logs can replace it early. Do not let missing coal stop torches.
- [triggers: find iron|iron ore|where mine iron|need iron] [topic: vanilla_find_iron] [tags: vanilla, mining, iron] Targeted fact: Iron is common enough in caves and mountains; bring a stone pickaxe or better, smelt raw iron, and spend the first ingots on shield, bucket, pickaxe, and armor.
- [triggers: find copper|copper use|what is copper for|copper ore] [topic: vanilla_copper] [tags: vanilla, mining, copper] Targeted fact: Copper is mostly building and utility metal: blocks weather green, lightning rods draw strikes, spyglasses need copper and amethyst, and copper bulbs or grates suit newer builds.
- [triggers: find redstone|redstone dust|where redstone|redstone ore] [topic: vanilla_redstone_resource] [tags: vanilla, mining, redstone] Targeted fact: Redstone is found deep underground and powers contraptions, rails, pistons, lamps, clocks, and nonsense that somehow becomes a door.
- [triggers: find lapis|lapis lazuli|need lapis enchanting|blue ore] [topic: vanilla_lapis] [tags: vanilla, mining, enchanting] Targeted fact: Lapis lazuli is required by the enchanting table. Keep more than you think; blue dust is cheaper than a wasted level-thirty mood.
- [triggers: find gold|gold ore|nether gold|what gold for] [topic: vanilla_gold] [tags: vanilla, mining, gold] Targeted fact: Gold is weak as gear but useful for powered rails, golden apples, piglin bartering, clocks, and Nether etiquette. Wear one gold armor piece near piglins.
- [triggers: find diamonds|diamond level|where mine diamond|diamond mining tips] [topic: vanilla_diamonds] [tags: vanilla, mining, diamond] Targeted fact: Diamonds are found deep, especially in deepslate layers; use an iron pickaxe or better, mine carefully around lava, and enchant with Fortune when patience allows.
- [triggers: fortune pickaxe|fortune diamonds|silk touch ores|best mining enchant] [topic: vanilla_mining_enchants] [tags: vanilla, mining, enchantments] Targeted fact: Fortune increases drops from many ores; Silk Touch preserves ore blocks and delicate blocks. Choose Fortune for yield, Silk Touch for control.
- [triggers: amethyst geode|find amethyst|spyglass amethyst|budding amethyst] [topic: vanilla_amethyst] [tags: vanilla, mining, geode] Targeted fact: Amethyst grows in geodes. Budding amethyst cannot be moved in survival, so mark good geodes and harvest mature clusters gently.
- [triggers: nether quartz|find quartz|quartz xp|comparator quartz] [topic: vanilla_quartz] [tags: vanilla, nether, mining] Targeted fact: Nether quartz is common in the Nether, gives good experience, and feeds observers, comparators, daylight detectors, and pale building vanity.
- [triggers: ancient debris|netherite|find netherite|upgrade diamond gear] [topic: vanilla_ancient_debris] [tags: vanilla, nether, netherite] Targeted fact: Ancient debris hides in the Nether and upgrades diamond gear through smithing. It resists fire, but miners do not; bring fire resistance, blocks, and caution.

## Mining Habits
- [triggers: branch mining|strip mining|best mining method|mine efficiently] [topic: vanilla_branch_mining] [tags: vanilla, mining] Targeted fact: Branch mining is controlled and safe; caving is faster and riskier. Light everything, block off danger, and keep the exit marked.
- [triggers: dig straight down|never dig down|digging rule|mine down safely] [topic: vanilla_never_dig_down] [tags: vanilla, mining, safety] Targeted fact: Never dig straight down unless you accept lava, caves, cliffs, and regret as tutors. Staircase down or stand between two blocks while mining alternately.
- [triggers: dig straight up|gravel suffocation|sand falls|mining ceiling] [topic: vanilla_never_dig_up] [tags: vanilla, mining, safety] Targeted fact: Digging straight up invites lava, gravel, sand, and falling mobs. Put a torch or block ready and mine ceilings like they owe you money.
- [triggers: deepslate mining|deep caves|deepslate slow|deep mining tips] [topic: vanilla_deepslate] [tags: vanilla, mining] Targeted fact: Deepslate is slower to mine but holds deep ores. Bring better picks, more food, more torches, and an exit plan before the dark grows roots.
- [triggers: cave sounds|scary cave noise|ambient cave sounds] [topic: vanilla_cave_ambience] [tags: vanilla, atmosphere] Targeted fact: Cave sounds are ambient warnings, not proof of a specific monster. Still, fear that makes you place torches is useful fear.
