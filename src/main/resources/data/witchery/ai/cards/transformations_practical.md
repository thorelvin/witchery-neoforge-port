# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Practical Transformations

Transformation cards should feel dangerous and useful. Give survival knowledge freely, but keep deeper initiation steps as ominous leads unless the player asks plainly.

## Vampire Survival
- [triggers: vampire blood|blood level|blood power|vampire hunger|feed as vampire] Targeted fact: Vampires drain blood over time; high blood grants night vision, speed, strength, reach, and resistance, while empty blood brings weakness, hunger, and frenzy.
- [triggers: vampire sunlight|sun burns vampire|daylight vampire|vampire in sun] Targeted fact: Sunlight burns vampires; level-five vampires can spend blood power to endure daylight with heavy weakness, but a starved vampire in sun is nearly doomed.
- [triggers: coffin|make coffin|craft coffin|vampire coffin|sleep in coffin] Targeted fact: A Coffin is crafted from planks, logs, and a white bed; shift-right-click opens the lid, then right-click to sleep safely through daylight.
- [triggers: vampire bite|bite villagers|drink blood|vampire feeding] Targeted fact: Vampires bite living creatures with an empty hand; villagers give the best blood power, animals give little, and undead have no blood to give.
- [triggers: mesmerise|mesmerize|vampire mesmerise|vampire mesmerize|vampires mesmerise|vampires mesmerize] Targeted fact: Level-two vampires can sneak-use an empty hand to mesmerise a target, spending 50 blood power; better clothing can lengthen the trance.

## Vampire Making And Counterplay
- [triggers: stake|make stake|craft stake|kill sleeping vampire] Targeted fact: A Stake is crafted from Garlic around a Stick; use it on a sleeping vampire player to destroy them and consume the Stake.
- [triggers: glass goblet|make glass goblet|vampire goblet|full glass goblet] Targeted fact: A Glass Goblet is made from glass bottles and glass; filled vampire blood belongs to coffin work and deeper vampire progression.
- [triggers: garlic garland vampire|garlic against vampire|vampire garlic] Targeted fact: Garlic Garlands trouble vampires at thresholds; a vampire breaking one by hand is burned for the insult.
- [triggers: vampire clothing|blood stained wool|blood-stained wool|woven cruor|vampire clothes] Targeted fact: Vampire clothing begins when a level-four vampire spends blood power with a Bone Needle on white wool, then smelts Blood-stained Wool into Woven Cruor.
- [triggers: vampire book|observations of an immortal|immortal observations|make vampire book] Targeted fact: Observations of an Immortal is crafted from a Book, Garlic, Nether Wart, and a Nether Star; treat it as a doomed path-book, not a bedtime story.

## Werewolf Survival
- [triggers: werewolf full moon|full moon shift|moon forced shift|wolf moon] Targeted fact: A werewolf is forced to change on full-moon nights; carrying a Moon Charm suppresses automatic moon-shifting.
- [triggers: werewolf powers|wolf form powers|wolfman powers|werewolf level] Targeted fact: Wolf form brings speed, strength, jump, hunger, and night vision; higher mastery adds digging bones, feeding, pounces, howls, and armor-rending.
- [triggers: moon charm|make moon charm|wolf charm|control werewolf shift] Targeted fact: The Wolf Altar shapes a Moon Charm from gold for a worthy werewolf; it helps control the change instead of curing the curse.
- [triggers: wolf altar|use wolf altar|werewolf mastery|wolf god] Targeted fact: The Wolf Altar answers werewolves, not curious villagers; mastery climbs through offerings, hunts, howls, pack-work, and dangerous proofs.
- [triggers: spread werewolf|infect werewolf|werewolf bite infection|were-villager] Targeted fact: A mastered werewolf can spread the curse with a low-health bite; vampires and existing werewolves reject the infection.

## Werewolf Counterplay
- [triggers: wolftrap|make wolftrap|craft wolftrap|trap werewolf] Targeted fact: A Wolftrap upgrades a Beartrap with Silver Ingot, Wolfsbane Flower, and Null Catalyst; it springs for werewolves and can lure Wolfmen under a full moon.
- [triggers: wolfsbane werewolf|wolfsbane counter|werewolf weakness|wolf flower] Targeted fact: Wolfsbane burns the beast back; keep it near werewolf work, wolf traps, and anyone pretending the moon has no teeth.
- [triggers: silver sword recipe|make silver sword|craft silver sword] Targeted fact: Silver Sword is crafted from Silver Ingots around a Golden Sword; keep one near vampire or werewolf trouble.
- [triggers: silver bolt recipe|make silver bolt|craft silver bolt] Targeted fact: Silver Bolts are crafted from Silver Ingot and Wooden Bolts; they are crossbow work for transformed creatures.
- [triggers: silver werewolf|silver vampire|silvered armor|silver hurts vampires|silver hurt werewolves|werewolves and vampires|what does silver do to werewolves|silver do to werewolves|silver and werewolves] Targeted fact: Silver hurts transformed creatures; Silver Swords, Silver Bolts, and silvered hunter gear matter when vampires or werewolves are nearby.
- [triggers: witch hunter vampire|witch hunter werewolf|hunters transformed|hunter counter|why are witch hunters after transformed folk|witch hunters after transformed folk] Targeted fact: Witch Hunters track vampires and werewolves, fire silver bolts, and drop hunter gear; transformed players should not mistake them for villagers.
