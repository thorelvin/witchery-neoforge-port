# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: End And Elytra Deep Pack

These cards expand the End path. Use exact answers for Coven Witches and explicit vanilla-teacher contexts; use rumor versions for villagers.

## Stronghold Path
- [triggers: end progression checklist|steps to beat minecraft|main progression end] [topic: vanilla_end_progression_checklist] [tags: vanilla, end, progression] Targeted fact: The usual End path is iron gear, diamonds if wanted, Nether portal, fortress blazes, ender pearls, Eyes of Ender, stronghold, dragon, gateway, End city, elytra.
- [triggers: get ender pearls|farm ender pearls|where pearls] [topic: vanilla_ender_pearl_sources] [tags: vanilla, end, pearls] Targeted fact: Ender pearls come from Endermen and sometimes trades or loot. Warped forests and deserts at night can help, but eye contact charges interest.
- [triggers: get blaze powder|make blaze powder|blaze rod powder] [topic: vanilla_blaze_powder] [tags: vanilla, nether, end] Targeted fact: Blaze powder is crafted from blaze rods and is needed for Eyes of Ender and brewing stand fuel. That ties the End path to Nether fortresses.
- [triggers: how many eyes bring|eyes needed stronghold|end eyes count] [topic: vanilla_eye_count_practical] [tags: vanilla, end, planning] Targeted fact: Bring more Eyes of Ender than 12 because thrown eyes can shatter and portal frames may be empty. Sixteen to twenty is a safer ordinary target.
- [triggers: triangulate stronghold|stronghold triangulation|use eyes fewer] [topic: vanilla_stronghold_triangulation] [tags: vanilla, end, navigation] Targeted fact: Throw eyes from separated points and record directions to estimate the stronghold path. This saves eyes but asks for patience and notes.
- [triggers: dig down to stronghold|eye goes down|stronghold below] [topic: vanilla_dig_to_stronghold] [tags: vanilla, end, safety] Targeted fact: When Eyes of Ender dive downward, dig a stair or safe shaft, not straight down. Strongholds enjoy lava, caves, and embarrassing falls.
- [triggers: stronghold library|stronghold loot|stronghold rooms] [topic: vanilla_stronghold_rooms] [tags: vanilla, stronghold, loot] Targeted fact: Strongholds can hold libraries, corridors, fountains, chests, and a portal room. Mark explored paths so stone corridors do not eat your sense of direction.
- [triggers: silverfish portal room|break silverfish spawner|stronghold silverfish] [topic: vanilla_silverfish_portal_room] [tags: vanilla, stronghold, mobs] Targeted fact: The portal room usually has a silverfish spawner. Disable it or control the room before placing eyes.
- [triggers: end portal one way|leave end before dragon|escape end] [topic: vanilla_end_entry_commitment] [tags: vanilla, end, boundary] Targeted fact: Entering the End is a commitment. In normal survival, leaving before the dragon dies is not as simple as stepping back through a door.

## Dragon Fight
- [triggers: dragon crystals cages|crystal cages|iron bars crystals] [topic: vanilla_dragon_crystal_cages] [tags: vanilla, end, dragon] Targeted fact: Some End crystals are protected by iron bars. Climb, pearl, bridge, or shoot carefully, and do not stand beside a crystal when it breaks.
- [triggers: dragon perch|hit dragon when perched|dragon fountain] [topic: vanilla_dragon_perch] [tags: vanilla, end, dragon] Targeted fact: When the dragon perches at the exit fountain, melee can work, but watch breath clouds, knockback, and Endermen stirred by panic.
- [triggers: water in end|endermen protection water|end water bucket] [topic: vanilla_end_water] [tags: vanilla, end, safety] Targeted fact: Water helps against Endermen and falls, but the End's terrain still punishes bad placement. Carry buckets if your server rules allow normal water use there.
- [triggers: carved pumpkin endermen|pumpkin end fight|avoid endermen eyes] [topic: vanilla_pumpkin_endermen] [tags: vanilla, end, endermen] Targeted fact: A carved pumpkin prevents Endermen from becoming angry when looked at, but it narrows vision. Safety always charges a price.
- [triggers: slow falling dragon|feather falling end|dragon knocks me up] [topic: vanilla_dragon_fall_protection] [tags: vanilla, end, safety] Targeted fact: Slow Falling potions, Feather Falling boots, water, pearls, and blocks reduce dragon-launch deaths. Bring at least one answer to gravity.
- [triggers: dragon egg|get dragon egg|dragon egg teleports] [topic: vanilla_dragon_egg] [tags: vanilla, end, trophy] Targeted fact: The dragon egg is a trophy that teleports when punched. Use a torch, piston, or other safe method if you mean to collect it.
- [triggers: respawn dragon|end crystals respawn dragon|dragon again] [topic: vanilla_respawn_dragon] [tags: vanilla, end, dragon] Targeted fact: End crystals can respawn the dragon when placed around the exit portal. Do that only when prepared to fight again.
- [triggers: end gateway|outer end portal|small bedrock portal] [topic: vanilla_end_gateway_v3] [tags: vanilla, end, outer islands] Targeted fact: After the dragon, End gateways can send players to outer islands. Use an ender pearl carefully through the small gateway opening.
- [triggers: return from outer end|get back from end city|end gateway home] [topic: vanilla_outer_end_return] [tags: vanilla, end, navigation] Targeted fact: Mark outer-island gateways and coordinates. Getting lost in the End is quiet, wide, and poor theater.

## End Cities, Ships, Elytra
- [triggers: find end city|where end cities|outer end islands] [topic: vanilla_find_end_city] [tags: vanilla, end city, exploration] Targeted fact: End cities generate on the outer End islands after gateway travel. Bridge, pearl, or fly carefully between islands; the void accepts all donations.
- [triggers: end ship chance|find end ship|ship beside end city] [topic: vanilla_end_ship_search] [tags: vanilla, end ship, elytra] Targeted fact: Some End cities have End ships nearby and some do not. Search multiple cities if the first towers have no ship.
- [triggers: where elytra exactly|elytra item frame|end ship treasure room] [topic: vanilla_elytra_item_frame_exact] [tags: vanilla, elytra, end ship] Targeted fact: Elytra are found in an item frame in the treasure room of an End ship, guarded by shulkers and near loot chests.
- [triggers: shulker fight|fight shulkers|shulker levitation] [topic: vanilla_shulker_fight] [tags: vanilla, end city, shulker] Targeted fact: Shulkers cause levitation with projectiles. Bring Feather Falling, slow falling, blocks overhead, bow, shield timing, and patience.
- [triggers: shulker shells|make shulker box|shulker box recipe] [topic: vanilla_shulker_box] [tags: vanilla, shulker, storage] Targeted fact: Shulker boxes are crafted from a chest and shulker shells. They keep contents when broken, which makes long journeys less cruel.
- [triggers: use elytra|how fly elytra|elytra controls] [topic: vanilla_elytra_flying] [tags: vanilla, elytra, flight] Targeted fact: Equip elytra in the chest slot, jump while falling to glide, aim to steer, and use rockets for powered flight if you have them.
- [triggers: rockets for elytra|firework rockets flight|elytra boost] [topic: vanilla_elytra_rockets_v3] [tags: vanilla, elytra, rockets] Targeted fact: Firework rockets without explosion stars are preferred for elytra boosting; explosive rockets can hurt the user.
- [triggers: repair elytra|elytra mending|phantom membrane elytra] [topic: vanilla_elytra_repair_v3] [tags: vanilla, elytra, repair] Targeted fact: Elytra can be repaired with Mending experience or with phantom membranes at an anvil. Mending and Unbreaking make the wings less hungry.
- [triggers: elytra broke midair|elytra durability|elytra almost broken] [topic: vanilla_elytra_durability_warning] [tags: vanilla, elytra, safety] Targeted fact: Watch elytra durability before long flights. Carry rockets, backup gear, and a landing plan; the sky is not responsible for your bookkeeping.
- [triggers: first flight elytra|practice elytra|safe elytra launch] [topic: vanilla_elytra_practice] [tags: vanilla, elytra, safety] Targeted fact: Practice over water or open ground before cliff theatrics. Learn shallow descents, turns, and rocket timing where mistakes bruise less.
- [triggers: end city loot|end ship loot|dragon head] [topic: vanilla_end_city_loot] [tags: vanilla, end city, loot] Targeted fact: End cities and ships may hold strong loot, shulker shells, elytra, and a dragon head on the ship's prow. Loot after safety, not before.
