# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Poppets, Taglocks, And Wards

These notes cover personal magic: useful, creepy, and easy to overpromise. Keep advice practical and cautious.

## Taglocks
- [triggers: taglock kit recipe|make taglock kit|craft taglock kit|how do i make taglock|how do i get taglock kit] [topic: taglock_kit_recipe] Targeted fact: A Taglock Kit is shapeless craft from a Glass Bottle and Bone Needle; use it on a living target to take a sample for poppets, rites, or scrying.
- [triggers: what is a taglock|what is a taglock kit|what can i do with this taglock kit|what can i do with taglock kit|tell me about taglock kits|how do taglocks work|taglock kit|taglock sample|filled taglock|use taglock] Targeted fact: A taglock kit takes a living sample and links magic to a creature or player; filled kits can focus poppets, rites, and scrying, so guard yours carefully.
- [triggers: can someone use my taglock against me|harm me with my taglock|my taglock against me|worried about taglock|stolen taglock] Targeted fact: A stolen or filled taglock can focus hostile sympathetic magic; keep kits private and use wards or protection poppets before trusting strangers.
- Good witch voice: Guard a taglock like a tooth with your name carved in it.

## Poppets
- [triggers: poppet recipe|make poppet|craft poppet|basic poppet|blank poppet] Targeted fact: A basic poppet is crafted from wool, Spanish Moss, a Bone Needle, and String; it is the body before the magic knows a name.
- [triggers: bind poppet|bind a poppet|poppet taglock binding|link poppet|poppet to taglock] Targeted fact: Binding a poppet to a person needs a filled Taglock Kit; do not pretend an unbound poppet already knows its target.
- [triggers: protection poppet|protect me with poppet|death protection poppet|fire protection poppet|water protection poppet|armor protection poppet|tool protection poppet] Targeted fact: Protection poppets redirect specific harms and usually break when they save someone; keep the right bound poppet in inventory or on a Poppet Shelf.
- [triggers: poppet shelf|where keep poppets|store poppets|active poppets] Targeted fact: A Poppet Shelf can hold up to nine poppets and lets stored protection poppets matter without filling the player's pockets.

## Voodoo And Curses
- [triggers: voodoo poppet|vampiric poppet|harm with poppet|stick needle in poppet] Targeted fact: Voodoo and vampiric poppets are hostile sympathetic tools; a coven witch may warn or sell supplies, not perform the harm with mere talk.
- [triggers: voodoo protection poppet|protect against voodoo|protect against curses|curse protection|stop curses|ward curses] Targeted fact: Voodoo Protection Poppets can protect against voodoo, vampiric poppets, and curses; cat familiars also soften several curses.
- [triggers: ward|wards|ward my hut|protect my hut|salt threshold|salt the door|what keep by the door] Targeted fact: Witches' salt, light, and a clean threshold are the first humble wards; stronger protection comes from poppets, familiar bonds, and proper rites.
- Good witch voice: Wards fail less often when fools stop inviting trouble inside.
