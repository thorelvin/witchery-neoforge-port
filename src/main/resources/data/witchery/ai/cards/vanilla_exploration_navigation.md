# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Exploration And Navigation

These cards cover travel, maps, coordinates, and finding places. Mention commands only when the player asks about cheats or commands.

## Getting Around
- [triggers: how explore safely|exploration tips|travel far from base|long journey tips] [topic: vanilla_explore_safely] [tags: vanilla, exploration] Targeted fact: For long travel, carry food, bed, boat, blocks, torches, shield, spare tools, and coordinates for home. Curiosity is lighter when packed properly.
- [triggers: boat travel|boat on ice|cross ocean|travel by boat] [topic: vanilla_boat_travel] [tags: vanilla, travel, boat] Targeted fact: Boats are cheap fast travel across water and safe enough for early exploration; they also help carry some mobs when patience has failed.
- [triggers: horse travel|saddle horse|find saddle|horse fast travel] [topic: vanilla_horse_travel] [tags: vanilla, travel, horse] Targeted fact: Horses make overworld travel faster, but need a saddle from loot or trades. Test jump and speed before growing attached to a slow noble sausage.
- [triggers: minecart travel|rails travel|railway base|powered rails] [topic: vanilla_rail_travel] [tags: vanilla, travel, redstone] Targeted fact: Minecarts need rails and powered rails to be useful over distance. They are expensive early, but reliable for mines, bases, and controlled mob transport.
- [triggers: nether highway|nether travel shortcut|travel faster in nether|8 to 1 travel] [topic: vanilla_nether_highway] [tags: vanilla, nether, navigation] Targeted fact: Nether travel compresses horizontal distance: one block in the Nether equals eight in the Overworld on X and Z. A safe tunnel there can shrink a long road.
- [triggers: lodestone compass|lodestone use|compass to base|nether compass] [topic: vanilla_lodestone] [tags: vanilla, navigation, compass] Targeted fact: A compass used on a lodestone points back to that lodestone, even outside ordinary spawn logic. It is expensive, but obedient.
- [triggers: landmark tips|how not get lost|navigation without coordinates|lost in forest] [topic: vanilla_landmarks] [tags: vanilla, navigation] Targeted fact: Build visible landmarks, leave torch trails, mark paths with blocks, and record coordinates. Forests love eating confident travelers.
- [triggers: buried treasure map|treasure map red x|find buried treasure|shipwreck treasure] [topic: vanilla_buried_treasure_map] [tags: vanilla, exploration, treasure] Targeted fact: Buried treasure maps usually come from shipwrecks and ocean ruins. Follow the map to the red X, then dig around the marked chunk until the chest admits defeat.

## Commands And Out-Of-World Tools
- [triggers: locate command|slash locate|find structure command|cheat find mansion|cheat find stronghold] [topic: vanilla_locate_command_boundary] [tags: vanilla, commands, boundary] Targeted fact: With cheats or operator permission, `/locate structure ...` can find structures. An NPC may explain it, but cannot run it, teleport you, or make it fair.
- [triggers: seed map|chunkbase|external map|use seed finder|seed tool] [topic: vanilla_seed_tools_boundary] [tags: vanilla, external, boundary] Targeted fact: Seed-map tools are out-of-world aids. Mention them only when asked plainly, and keep NPC voice aware that this is player knowledge, not witch-sight.
- [triggers: no cheats survival find|find without cheats|legit find structures|survival exploration] [topic: vanilla_no_cheat_finding] [tags: vanilla, exploration] Targeted fact: Without cheats, use villager maps, Eyes of Ender, biome clues, ruined portals, ocean maps, and patient travel. Survival knowledge is slower, but cleaner.
- [triggers: write coordinates|important coordinates|what coords save|coordinate notes] [topic: vanilla_coordinate_notes] [tags: vanilla, navigation] Targeted fact: Save coordinates for base, spawn, portals on both sides, villages, strongholds, mansions, trial chambers, ancient cities, and death points. Ink is cheaper than wandering.
- [triggers: compass vs map|map or compass|navigation items difference] [topic: vanilla_map_compass_difference] [tags: vanilla, navigation] Targeted fact: A compass points to spawn unless tied to a lodestone; a map shows explored surface area. Neither knows your heart's home unless you teach it.
