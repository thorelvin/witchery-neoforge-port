# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Basic Recipes

Compact recipe notes for practical player teaching. These notes are grounded in local recipes and Primer text.

## Whiff of Magic
- [triggers: whiff|whif|whiff of magic|whif of magic|rowan sapling|circle reagent] Targeted fact: Whiff of Magic comes from cooking a Rowan Sapling in a Witch's Oven; use fuel and a Clay Jar, and expect Wood Ash as the other oven output.
- [triggers: what uses whiff of magic|what use whiff of magic|uses whiff of magic|use whiff of magic|recipe uses whiff|recipes use whiff|specific recipe uses whiff] Targeted fact: Whiff of Magic is used in Brew of Sleeping, Brew of Love, Attuned Stone, Book of Circle Magic, and later workings.
- Whiff of Magic is an oven fume from Rowan Saplings.
- It is useful in circle magic, Brew of Sleeping, Brew of Love, Attuned Stone, and several later workings.
- Good witch voice: Cook rowan in the oven with a clay jar ready; that is where the Whiff curls out.

## Anointing Paste
- [triggers: anointing paste|anointing paste recipe|make anointing paste|craft anointing paste|what do i need for anointing paste|first paste|starter paste|start the kettle] Targeted fact: Anointing Paste is shapeless craft from Artichoke Seeds, Mandrake Seeds, Belladonna Seeds, and Snowbell Seeds; it makes four paste.
- Anointing Paste belongs to early cauldron and kettle progression.
- Tell beginners to gather the four herb seeds before chasing stranger brews.

## Chalk
- [triggers: chalk recipe|make chalk|craft chalk|ritual chalk] [topic: ritual_chalk_recipe] Targeted fact: Ritual Chalk is crafted from Wood Ash, Gypsum, and Tear of the Goddess in the chalk recipe pattern; use it for circle glyphs and rites.
- Chalk is the first serious circle tool.
- If a player asks exact placement, tell them to check the recipe page their book shows.
