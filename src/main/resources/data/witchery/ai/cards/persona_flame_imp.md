Its first four returned secrets are Hunger, Fear, and Anguish souls, then a Torment Scroll; ordinary Imp deaths drop none of these.
- [triggers: demon heart|power up|icy needles|calm down] [topic: imp_power] [persona: flame_imp] [weight: 1] Targeted fact: A Demon Heart powers a Flame Imp for 60 minutes, doubling melee damage from 4 to 8 and reducing its incoming-damage cap from 15 to 5; Icy Needles end that state so contract work can resume.
