# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Special Tools And Curious Gear

These notes cover utility items that read, guide, or bend movement without replacing full recipes.

## Tracking And Reading Tools
- [triggers: book of biomes|biome book|biome climate|climate stats] [topic: book_of_biomes] [tags: climate book, land reader, biome stats, travel planning] Targeted fact: Book of Biomes pages show practical climate stats; use it when rites, crops, or travel depend on the land itself.
- [triggers: diviner|scan block|wooden scanner|scanner tool] [topic: scanner_tools] [tags: reading tool, world scanner, block reader, setup check] Targeted fact: Divining tools read the world rather than changing it; use them before blaming spirits for a bad setup.
- [triggers: player compass|bound compass|compass needle|find player] [topic: player_compass] [tags: tracking tool, bound player, taglock link, compass needle] Targeted fact: A Player Compass follows a bound player or taglock-like link with a source-style needle; it points, but it does not make the path safe.
- [triggers: shelf compass|poppet shelf compass|which shelf|find poppet shelf] [topic: poppet_shelf_compass] [tags: tracking tool, linked shelf, ward storage, compass needle] Targeted fact: A Poppet Shelf Compass helps track a linked shelf; it is useful when wards are scattered across houses, huts, and hidden rooms.

## Odd Movement Gear
- [triggers: ruby slippers|home teleport|return home] [topic: ruby_slippers] [tags: movement gear, return path, travel gear, home magic] Targeted fact: Ruby Slippers are movement magic; treat them as a return path, not a substitute for planning.
- [triggers: bark belt|tree armor|bark protection] [topic: bark_belt] [tags: protection gear, bark armor, tree gear, defensive belt] Targeted fact: The Bark Belt protects like stubborn wood; useful gear still has limits when fire or silver enters the story.

## Weapons And Signs
- [triggers: cane sword|hidden sword|draw cane sword|sheathe sword] [topic: cane_sword] [tags: hidden weapon, civil blade, concealed sword, weapon tool] Targeted fact: The Cane Sword hides steel in civility; drawing and sheathing matter as much as the strike.
- [triggers: death's hand|deaths hand|scythe hand|death scythe] [topic: deaths_hand] [tags: death weapon, scythe state, boss sign, weapon tool] Targeted fact: Death's Hand can answer as a scythe-like state on the same item; do not confuse the sign of Death with ordinary farming iron.
