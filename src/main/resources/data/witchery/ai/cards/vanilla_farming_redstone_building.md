# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Farming, Redstone, And Building

These cards broaden NPC knowledge into ordinary base life. Keep technical builds conceptual unless the player asks for a specific design.

## Food And Farming
- [triggers: start farm|crop farm basics|grow wheat carrots potatoes|early farm] [topic: vanilla_crop_farming] [tags: vanilla, farming] Targeted fact: Crops need hydrated farmland and enough light. Water hydrates farmland up to four blocks away, so a small pond can feed many rows.
- [triggers: animal farm|breed animals|cow sheep chicken pig|animal breeding] [topic: vanilla_animal_farming] [tags: vanilla, farming, animals] Targeted fact: Animals breed with their preferred foods: wheat for cows and sheep, seeds for chickens, carrots or similar crops for pigs, and patience for the farmer.
- [triggers: sugar cane farm|grow sugar cane|paper farm|bookshelf paper] [topic: vanilla_sugar_cane] [tags: vanilla, farming, paper] Targeted fact: Sugar cane grows beside water on sand, dirt, or similar blocks and becomes paper, maps, books, rockets, and the quiet empire of late-game utility.
- [triggers: bamboo farm|bamboo use|scaffolding bamboo|bamboo wood] [topic: vanilla_bamboo] [tags: vanilla, farming, bamboo] Targeted fact: Bamboo grows quickly and feeds scaffolding, sticks, fuel, and bamboo building blocks. It is a plant that behaves like stored impatience.
- [triggers: cactus farm|cactus use|green dye|cactus smelt] [topic: vanilla_cactus] [tags: vanilla, farming, cactus] Targeted fact: Cactus grows on sand with empty sides, damages entities, and smelts into green dye. Do not hug ingredients with spines.
- [triggers: mushroom farm|grow mushrooms|huge mushroom|mushroom stew] [topic: vanilla_mushrooms] [tags: vanilla, farming, mushroom] Targeted fact: Mushrooms like low light or suitable blocks and can become huge mushrooms with bone meal. They suit stews, dyes, and suspicious little dinners.
- [triggers: bee farm|bees honey|honeycomb|campfire bees] [topic: vanilla_bees] [tags: vanilla, farming, bees] Targeted fact: Bees pollinate crops and produce honey or honeycomb from filled nests and hives. Use a campfire beneath before harvesting, unless stings are the desired chorus.
- [triggers: tree farm|grow trees|saplings bone meal|wood farm] [topic: vanilla_tree_farming] [tags: vanilla, farming, wood] Targeted fact: Tree farms need saplings, space, and light; bone meal hastens growth. Replant what you cut unless you enjoy living in a stump field.

## Redstone Basics
- [triggers: what is redstone|redstone basics|how redstone works|redstone beginner] [topic: vanilla_redstone_basics] [tags: vanilla, redstone] Targeted fact: Redstone carries power through dust and components. Start with levers, buttons, pressure plates, lamps, doors, pistons, repeaters, and observers before building madness.
- [triggers: repeater use|redstone repeater|extend signal|redstone delay] [topic: vanilla_repeater] [tags: vanilla, redstone] Targeted fact: Repeaters extend redstone signals, add delay, and control direction. They are the old schoolmasters of timing.
- [triggers: comparator use|redstone comparator|read container|compare signal] [topic: vanilla_comparator] [tags: vanilla, redstone] Targeted fact: Comparators read container fullness, compare signals, and subtract signals. They are for builds that want to know how much is in the cupboard.
- [triggers: observer use|observer block|detect block update|observer clock] [topic: vanilla_observer] [tags: vanilla, redstone] Targeted fact: Observers detect block changes and emit pulses. They make farms, clocks, flying machines, and many accidents look intentional.
- [triggers: hopper use|hopper sorting|move items|item transport] [topic: vanilla_hopper] [tags: vanilla, redstone, storage] Targeted fact: Hoppers move items between inventories and can feed sorters, furnaces, composters, and storage systems. They are funnels with opinions.
- [triggers: piston door|piston basics|sticky piston|move blocks] [topic: vanilla_pistons] [tags: vanilla, redstone, building] Targeted fact: Pistons push blocks; sticky pistons can pull many blocks back. Doors, farms, elevators, and traps all begin with a shove.
- [triggers: sculk sensor redstone|wireless redstone|vibration sensor|deep dark redstone] [topic: vanilla_sculk_sensor_redstone] [tags: vanilla, redstone, sculk] Targeted fact: Sculk sensors detect vibrations and output redstone, enabling wireless-ish contraptions. Wool can block vibrations and quiet the gossip.

## Building And Base Care
- [triggers: mob proof base|stop mobs spawning|spawn proof build|light base] [topic: vanilla_spawn_proofing] [tags: vanilla, building, safety] Targeted fact: Spawn-proof with light, slabs, carpets, buttons, leaves, glass, water, or other non-spawnable surfaces depending on mob and version. Darkness is only one invitation.
- [triggers: storage room|organize chests|item sorting|base storage] [topic: vanilla_storage] [tags: vanilla, building, storage] Targeted fact: Good storage uses labeled chests, barrels, shulker boxes later, and categories players actually remember. A messy chest is a curse with hinges.
- [triggers: build starter base|starter house|base location|where build base] [topic: vanilla_base_location] [tags: vanilla, building, base] Targeted fact: A good base has food, wood, stone, water, safe terrain, expansion space, and known coordinates. Beauty comes after the creeper insurance.
- [triggers: nether portal room|safe portal room|protect portal|portal base] [topic: vanilla_portal_room_build] [tags: vanilla, building, nether] Targeted fact: Protect portals with nonflammable blocks, walls, lighting, doors or gates, and ghast line-of-sight control on the Nether side.
- [triggers: automatic farm|farm automation|villager farm|redstone farm] [topic: vanilla_automation_boundary] [tags: vanilla, redstone, boundary] Targeted fact: Automation is possible for many resources, but exact builds depend on edition and version. Give principles first: growth, collection, storage, safety, and lag restraint.
