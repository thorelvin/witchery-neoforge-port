# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Nether Progression

These cards cover how to enter, survive, and use the Nether. Keep portal facts exact and survival advice cautious.

## Reaching The Nether
- [triggers: how get to nether|enter nether|make nether portal|nether portal recipe] [topic: vanilla_nether_portal_basic] [tags: vanilla, nether, portal] Targeted fact: Build a vertical obsidian frame at least 4 by 5 outside, with the corners optional, then light the inside with flint and steel, fire charge, or other fire source.
- [triggers: nether portal size|minimum portal|portal corners optional|obsidian portal shape] [topic: vanilla_nether_portal_size] [tags: vanilla, nether, portal] Targeted fact: A Nether portal frame is minimum 4x5 and maximum 23x23; the four corner obsidian blocks are optional for player-built portals.
- [triggers: get obsidian|make obsidian|mine obsidian|water lava obsidian] [topic: vanilla_obsidian] [tags: vanilla, obsidian, portal] Targeted fact: Obsidian forms when water touches lava source blocks and needs a diamond pickaxe or better to mine. Cast portals if picks are poor and nerve is strong.
- [triggers: flint and steel|light portal|activate nether portal|portal won't light] [topic: vanilla_light_portal] [tags: vanilla, nether, portal] Targeted fact: Light fire inside a complete obsidian frame to activate the portal. Check shape, missing obsidian, wrong dimension, water, and whether the frame is vertical.
- [triggers: ruined portal|find ruined portal|repair ruined portal|portal ruins] [topic: vanilla_ruined_portal] [tags: vanilla, exploration, nether] Targeted fact: Ruined portals can supply obsidian, flint and steel, gold loot, and a shortcut toward Nether access. Mind crying obsidian: it decorates, but does not count in the portal frame.

## Nether Safety
- [triggers: first nether trip|nether survival|what bring to nether|nether checklist] [topic: vanilla_nether_checklist] [tags: vanilla, nether, safety] Targeted fact: Bring gold armor, food, blocks, bow, arrows, shield, flint and steel, spare obsidian if possible, fire resistance later, and coordinates for both portal sides.
- [triggers: piglins angry|piglin gold armor|wear gold nether|barter piglins] [topic: vanilla_piglin_gold] [tags: vanilla, nether, piglin] Targeted fact: Piglins tolerate players wearing gold armor but still hate opened chests, mined gold near them, and bad manners. Barter with gold ingots from a respectful distance.
- [triggers: hoglins|hoglin danger|warped fungus hoglin|nether pigs attack] [topic: vanilla_hoglins] [tags: vanilla, nether, mobs] Targeted fact: Hoglins hit hard and breed in crimson places; warped fungus repels them. Do not confuse a snout with a friend.
- [triggers: ghast fireball|ghast broke portal|portal got broken in nether|reactivate portal] [topic: vanilla_ghast_portal_safety] [tags: vanilla, nether, ghast] Targeted fact: Ghasts can break portal blocks but not obsidian. Carry flint and steel or fire charges so a dead portal can be lit again.
- [triggers: nether coordinates|link portals|portal linking|8 to 1 nether] [topic: vanilla_portal_linking] [tags: vanilla, nether, navigation] Targeted fact: Nether and Overworld horizontal coordinates use an 8:1 ratio. Divide Overworld X and Z by 8 to place matching Nether portals more cleanly.
- [triggers: nether roof|bedrock roof|break nether roof|roof travel] [topic: vanilla_nether_roof_boundary] [tags: vanilla, nether, boundary] Targeted fact: Nether roof travel can depend on edition, rules, and glitches. A lore NPC should avoid exploit instructions unless the server openly supports them.

## Nether Goals
- [triggers: find nether fortress|nether fortress|need blaze rods|where blazes] [topic: vanilla_nether_fortress] [tags: vanilla, nether, fortress] Targeted fact: Nether fortresses hold blazes and nether wart, both key for brewing and Eyes of Ender. Search carefully across Nether terrain and mark the path back.
- [triggers: blaze rods|kill blazes|blaze powder|need blaze powder] [topic: vanilla_blaze_rods] [tags: vanilla, nether, blaze] Targeted fact: Blazes drop blaze rods; blaze powder fuels brewing stands and combines with ender pearls to make Eyes of Ender. Fire resistance turns panic into a fairer fight.
- [triggers: nether wart|find nether wart|brew potions|soul sand wart] [topic: vanilla_nether_wart] [tags: vanilla, nether, brewing] Targeted fact: Nether wart grows in fortresses and some bastions, usually on soul sand. It begins awkward potions, which begin most proper vanilla brewing.
- [triggers: bastion remnant|find bastion|piglin bastion|bastion loot] [topic: vanilla_bastion] [tags: vanilla, nether, structure] Targeted fact: Bastions hold strong loot and strong piglin anger. Wear gold, avoid brute confidence, and remember that opening containers is an insult there.
- [triggers: respawn anchor|set spawn nether|sleep in nether|nether bed] [topic: vanilla_respawn_anchor] [tags: vanilla, nether, spawn] Targeted fact: Beds explode in the Nether. Respawn anchors set Nether spawn when charged with glowstone, but explode if used in the Overworld or the End.
- [triggers: fire resistance potion|survive lava nether|brew fire resistance] [topic: vanilla_fire_resistance] [tags: vanilla, nether, brewing] Targeted fact: Fire Resistance is the great Nether comfort: it protects against fire and lava. Brew it before treating lava oceans like roads.
