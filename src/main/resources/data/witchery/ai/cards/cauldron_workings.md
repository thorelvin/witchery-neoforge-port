# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Cauldron Workings

Cauldron cards teach station use, early herbcraft progression, and dangerous late workings without turning every answer into a recipe dump.

## Waking The Cauldron
- [triggers: witch cauldron|witch s cauldron|make witch cauldron|make witch s cauldron|anoint cauldron|wake cauldron] Targeted fact: Use Anointing Paste on a placed vanilla Cauldron to wake a Witch's Cauldron, then give it heat below and water before adding reagents.
- [triggers: use witch cauldron|use witch s cauldron|how do i use witch cauldron|how do i use witch s cauldron|cauldron working] Targeted fact: A Witch's Cauldron wants heat, water, dropped reagents in the bowl, and a final trigger item used by hand; harder workings also spend altar power.
- [triggers: anointing paste for|what is anointing paste for|anointing paste use|paste cauldron] Targeted fact: Anointing Paste wakes a normal Cauldron into a Witch's Cauldron; the paste is made from Artichoke, Mandrake, Belladonna, and Snowbell seeds.

## Green Cauldron Work
- [triggers: cauldron mutandis|mutandis in cauldron|make mutandis in cauldron|cauldron makes mutandis] Targeted fact: For cauldron Mutandis, drop Mandrake Root and an Egg into a heated, filled Witch's Cauldron, then use Exhale of the Horned One; six Mutandis rise.
- [triggers: cauldron mutandis extremis|mutandis extremis in cauldron|make extremis in cauldron|nether wart mutandis cauldron] Targeted fact: For cauldron Mutandis Extremis, drop Nether Wart into a heated, filled Witch's Cauldron, then use Mutandis by hand.
- [triggers: cauldron mutating sprig|mutating sprig in cauldron|make sprig in cauldron|ent twig cauldron] Targeted fact: A cauldron Mutating Sprig uses Nether Wart and an Ent Twig, then Mutandis Extremis by hand; it needs about 3080 altar power.
- [triggers: how do i make drop of luck in cauldron|make drop of luck in cauldron|drop of luck in cauldron|cauldron drop of luck|witch cauldron drop of luck|witch s cauldron drop of luck] Targeted fact: Cauldron Drop of Luck uses Mandrake Root, Nether Wart, Tear of the Goddess, and Refined Evil, then Mutandis Extremis by hand with 6930 altar power.

## Chalk And Hidden Ground
- [triggers: cauldron chalk|upgrade chalk in cauldron|golden chalk cauldron|infernal chalk cauldron|otherwhere chalk cauldron] Targeted fact: Cauldron chalk upgrades: Golden needs Mandrake Root and Gold Nugget; Infernal needs Nether Wart and Blaze Powder; Otherwhere needs Nether Wart, Tear, and Ender Pearl.
- [triggers: pit dirt|pit grass|false floor|cauldron pit trap|hidden floor cauldron] Targeted fact: Cauldron pit traps use Tormented Twine: Dirt and Mandrake make Pit Dirt; Dirt, Wheat Seeds, and Dandelion make Pit Grass; both need 4000 altar power.

## Leonard's Bowl
- [triggers: how do i summon leonard with cauldron|summon leonard with cauldron|summon leonard cauldron|shade of leonard cauldron|call leonard|leonard cauldron|witches hat leonard] Targeted fact: To summon Leonard, dissolve Nether Wart, Tear of the Goddess, Diamond Vapour, Diamond, and Nether Star in order, then use a Witches' Hat with 10000 altar power.
- [triggers: leonard s urn|leonards urn|what is leonards urn|use leonards urn|urn focus] Targeted fact: Leonard's Urn stores thrown brews from your other hand, sneak-use changes focus, use releases the chosen brew, and crafting urns together combines capacity up to four slots.
