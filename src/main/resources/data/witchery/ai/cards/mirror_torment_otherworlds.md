# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Mirror And Torment

These notes cover dangerous side worlds and mirror craft. Keep advice short and caution-heavy.

## Mirror World
- [triggers: mirror world|mirror portal|reflection guardian|other mirror|inside mirror] [topic: mirror_world] Targeted fact: Mirror World work uses mirror portals and reflection danger; do not step through strange glass without a return plan.
- [triggers: reflection guardian|mirror guardian|guardian in mirror|fight reflection] [topic: mirror_guardian] Targeted fact: Reflection guardians are mirror dangers, not ordinary villagers or spirits; expect the glass to answer violence with a familiar shape.
- [triggers: quartz sphere mirror|inhabited mirror|mirror charge|charge from mirror] [topic: mirror_charging] Targeted fact: Inhabited mirrors can feed certain mirror workings when paired with a Quartz Sphere and altar power.

## Duplication Grenade
- [triggers: duplication grenade|charge grenade|copy item grenade|quartz sphere grenade] [topic: duplication_grenade] Targeted fact: Duplication Grenades can be charged from inhabited mirrors with a Quartz Sphere and altar power; this is mirror craft, not ordinary copying.

## Torment
- [triggers: torment|torment scroll|lord of torment|torment maze|torment dimension] [topic: torment] Targeted fact: Torment is a punishment realm with maze danger and a boss path; treat Torment scrolls as deliberate entry, not travel convenience.
- [triggers: escape torment|lost in torment|torment maze help|torment boss] [topic: torment_survival] Targeted fact: In Torment, survival means reading the maze, keeping supplies, and not trusting straight paths; the realm is built to punish haste.
