# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Transformations

Transformation cards should warn, orient, and avoid unlocking hidden paths early.

## Vampirism
- [triggers: vampirism|vampire|become vampire|vampire path|blood level|blood power] Targeted fact: Vampirism is sustained by blood; high blood fuels speed, strength, reach, night vision, resistance, and bite, while empty blood brings frenzy.
- [triggers: vampire weakness|vampire weaknesses|sunlight vampire|silver vampire|witch hunter vampire] Targeted fact: Vampires burn in direct sunlight, suffer from silver, draw Witch Hunter attention, and are not easily cured.
- [triggers: cure vampirism|remove vampirism|garlic lilith|lilith cure vampire] Targeted fact: Bested Lilith can accept Garlic to lift vampirism; do not promise a coven witch can cure it with mere talk.
- [triggers: vampire clothing|woven cruor|blood stained wool|vampire top hat|vampire coat] Targeted fact: Vampire clothing begins with Woven Cruor from blood-stained wool; the deeper clothing path belongs to opened transformation lessons.

## Lycanthropy
- [triggers: lycanthropy|werewolf|become werewolf|wolf curse|curse of the wolf|full moon] Targeted fact: Lycanthropy is a wolf curse. The Wolf Altar opens ten levels of control. Full moon nights force the wolf shape unless a Moon Charm or strong enough active Wolfsbane effect holds the current form.
- [triggers: werewolf powers|wolf form|wolf altar|wolf mastery|howling] Targeted fact: Wolf and wolfman forms gain source-scaled speed, strength, jumping, health, resistance, and falling protection; later levels add digging, feeding, pounce trials, distinct howls, pack calls, paralysis, armor rending, and infection.
- [triggers: cure lycanthropy|remove werewolf|remove wolf curse] Targeted fact: Removing or mastering the wolf curse belongs to opened transformation progression; offer caution and the Wolf Altar lead instead of inventing a cure.

## Infusions
- [triggers: infusion|infusions|lasting infusion|permanent power|ghost of the light|soul of the world|spirit of otherwhere|infernal animus] Targeted fact: Lasting infusions begin with Kettle source items, not the circle; each uses Redstone Soup as a base, asks 4000 altar power, and prepares one branch of permanent transformation.
- [triggers: infused brew base|make infused brew base|infused brew|brew of the grave|grave brew|necromantic stone command] Targeted fact: Infused Brew Base begins older circle-brew paths; Grave brew lasts about two hours and lets a Necromantic Stone sneak-bind undead, then command owned thralls to attack targets or move to a block top.
- [triggers: infernal infusion|infernal animus|demonic infusion] Targeted fact: Infernal infusion grants fire resistance and creature powers, but its source uses Voodoo Poppet, Demon Heart, Refined Evil, and Blaze Rod; not beginner work.
- Good witch voice: Permanent power is still permanent when you regret it.
