# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Symbology And The Mystic Branch

Symbology cards teach the branch as a living focus: how to make it, how to draw signs, what can fail, and which powers are worth remembering.

## Mystic Branch Basics
- [triggers: mystic branch|make mystic branch|get mystic branch|craft mystic branch|how do i get mystic branch] Targeted fact: A Mystic Branch comes from a night Rite of Infusion using Ent Twig and Mystic Unguent with 3000 altar power; it is not a bench-made wand.
- [triggers: use mystic branch|how do i use mystic branch|draw symbol|draw glyph with branch|air glyph] Targeted fact: Hold use with the Mystic Branch, flick your view up, down, left, and right to draw a sign, then release when the HUD names the spell; casting spends infused energy.
- [triggers: why does my mystic branch fizzle|mystic branch fizzle|branch fizzle|branch fizzles|mystic branch fizzles|unknown symbol|branch no charges|branch failed] Targeted fact: A Mystic Branch fizzles if no symbol was drawn, you lack infused energy, you lack any infusion, the sign is still cooling, or the sign needs Demon-soul knowledge you have not learned.
- [triggers: stronger branch sign|level 2 branch|level 3 branch|stronger glyph|enhanced glyph|worship potion] Targeted fact: Enhanced branch signs need Worship; first strength permits level 2 signs, stronger Worship permits level 3, and without it longer strokes answer at first strength.
- [triggers: branch on altar|place branch altar|mystic branch focus|altar focus branch] Targeted fact: Right-click the altar top with a Mystic Branch to place it as a focus item; hold use elsewhere when you mean to draw air-symbols.

## Useful Signs
- [triggers: branch spells|symbol spells|what spells branch|what can mystic branch do|branch spell list] Targeted fact: Practical branch signs include Accio for items, Episkey for healing, Expelliarmus to disarm, Flipendo to shove, Incendio for fire, and Lumos or Nox for light.
- [triggers: utility branch spells|alohomora|defodio|protego|flagrate|colloportus] Targeted fact: Utility signs include Alohomora for doors, Defodio for soft blocks, Protego for a brief barrier shield, Flagrate for drawing infernal marks on a struck top face, and Colloportus for sealed Rowan doors.
- [triggers: aguamenti|branch water spell|fill kettle with branch|fill cauldron with branch] Targeted fact: Aguamenti can fill kettles and cauldrons; stronger strokes spill water in a small cross, while Nether heat resists all but the strongest form.
- [triggers: meteolojinx|meteolojinx recanto|clear rain with branch|clear storm with branch|branch weather spell] Targeted fact: Meteolojinx Recanto is a costly branch sign that clears rain and thunder; if the sky is already clear, the branch only snaps back with a dry note.
- [triggers: accio|episkey|expelliarmus|flipendo|incendio|lumos|nox|stupefy] Targeted fact: Accio pulls items, Episkey heals, Expelliarmus disarms, Flipendo flings, Incendio sets fire, Lumos makes light, Nox snuffs light, and Stupefy stuns.
- [triggers: deflect branch spell|block branch spell|swat spell orb|mystic branch deflect|spell orb] Targeted fact: A Mystic Branch can swat most incoming branch spell orbs back along the holder's gaze; Cave Inimicum will not turn.

## Demon-Soul Signs
- [triggers: demon soul glyph|demon soul symbol|learn branch spell|learn symbol|learn demonic glyph] Targeted fact: Right-clicking the right Demon soul teaches a demonic branch sign and consumes the soul; learned signs are remembered by the player.
- [triggers: ignianima|carnosa diem|tormentum|morsmordre|demonic branch spells] Targeted fact: Anguish soul teaches Ignianima, Hunger soul teaches Carnosa Diem, Torment soul teaches Tormentum, and Fear soul teaches Morsmordre.
- [triggers: what does tormentum do|tormentum effect|tormentum spell|torment sign] Targeted fact: Tormentum casts players into Torment and unmakes ordinary non-boss living mobs; it is not a casual working.
- [triggers: tormentum cooldown|symbol cooldown|branch cooldown|glyph cooldown|why is tormentum cooling] Targeted fact: Tormentum is powerful enough to carry a long branch cooldown; wait for the sign to cool or use another working.
- [triggers: unforgivable branch spells|crucio|imperio|avada kedavra|killing curse] Targeted fact: Some dark curse signs exist in the registry, but ordinary survival branch work should treat them as forbidden or unavailable unless the path explicitly opens.
- [triggers: what does imperio do|imperio effect|imperio spell|enslave with branch|branch enslave] Targeted fact: Imperio is a forbidden branch sign that binds eligible struck creatures as thralls; bosses, golems, witches, and infernal natives resist it.

## Infusions And Creature Powers
- [triggers: infused energy|branch energy|infusion energy|why no infused energy] Targeted fact: Mystic Branch signs require an active infusion and infused energy; when higher strengths are available they cost more energy, so a tired branch may know the shape and still refuse.
- [triggers: witch hand|witches hand|what is witches hand|use witches hand|infusion hand] Targeted fact: A Witches Hand channels the player's infusion directly: Light protects, Overworld moves earth and metal, Otherwhere teleports, and Infernal commands or sacrifices thralls.
- [triggers: creature power|creature powers|altar kill power|sacrifice creature power|stored creature power] Targeted fact: Killing certain creatures near an altar with at least 50 power stores a creature power; infernal Witches Hand work can also sacrifice your own thralls for charges.
- [triggers: what creature powers|which creature powers|spider power|creeper power|bat power|enderman power|blaze power] Targeted fact: Creature powers include Spider webs, Creeper explosion, Bat levitation, Squid blindness, Ghast and Blaze fire, Enderman teleport, Speed, Jump, Heal, Zombie, Pigman, and Skeleton.

## Leonard's Urn Signs
- [triggers: caelum|baratrum|ortus|occasus|urn glyph|urn signs|leonard urn glyph] Targeted fact: Caelum, Baratrum, Ortus, and Occasus are Mystic Branch signs that release brew focuses stored in Leonard's Urn slots one through four.
