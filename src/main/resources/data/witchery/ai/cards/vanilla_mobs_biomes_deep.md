# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Mobs, Biomes, And Weather Deep Pack

These cards help NPCs answer broad world questions with practical caution.

## Common Hostile Mobs
- [triggers: creeper advice|avoid creepers|creeper explosion] [topic: vanilla_creeper_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, creeper] Targeted fact: Creepers punish surprise and poor spacing. Keep light, listen for hiss, use shield if caught, and do not fight them beside valuables.
- [triggers: skeleton advice|fight skeletons|skeleton arrows] [topic: vanilla_skeleton_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, skeleton] Targeted fact: Skeletons are easier with shield, cover, and zigzag approach. At range, answer arrow with arrow or patience.
- [triggers: zombie advice|zombies door|baby zombie] [topic: vanilla_zombie_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, zombie] Targeted fact: Zombies are simple until numbers, doors, villagers, and baby zombies join. Light villages and protect the small loud ones first.
- [triggers: spider advice|fight spiders|cave spiders poison] [topic: vanilla_spider_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, spider] Targeted fact: Spiders climb and cave spiders poison. Use blocks, light, and range; never let webs turn a fight into a waiting room.
- [triggers: enderman advice|fight endermen|looked at enderman] [topic: vanilla_enderman_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, enderman] Targeted fact: Avoid eye contact with Endermen unless ready. Water, low ceilings, boats, and shields can control the fight.
- [triggers: witch mob advice|vanilla witch fight|fight witch] [topic: vanilla_witch_mob_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, witch] Targeted fact: Vanilla witch mobs throw harmful potions and drink defensive ones. Fight decisively at range or rush safely; do not confuse them with Coven Witches.
- [triggers: slime chunks|find slimes|slime farm] [topic: vanilla_slimes_v3] [tags: vanilla, mobs, slime] Targeted fact: Slimes spawn in swamps under moon and in slime chunks deep underground. Slimeballs feed leads, sticky pistons, and other useful mischief.
- [triggers: phantom advice|phantoms spawn|stop phantoms] [topic: vanilla_phantoms_v3] [tags: vanilla, mobs, phantom] Targeted fact: Phantoms punish sleepless players in open sky. Sleep, shelter, cats, or ranged weapons make the night less winged.
- [triggers: drowned trident|drowned advice|underwater zombie] [topic: vanilla_drowned] [tags: vanilla, mobs, drowned] Targeted fact: Drowned lurk in water and some throw tridents. Use doors, air pockets, shields, and do not fight the sea on the sea's terms.
- [triggers: guardian advice|elder guardian|mining fatigue] [topic: vanilla_guardians] [tags: vanilla, mobs, guardian] Targeted fact: Guardians attack with beams and elder guardians inflict Mining Fatigue. Break line of sight, bring potions, and plan around slow mining.
- [triggers: warden advice|fight warden|should i kill warden] [topic: vanilla_warden_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, warden] Targeted fact: The Warden is meant to be avoided, not farmed by ordinary sense. Sneak, use wool, distract, and leave before sound becomes judgment.
- [triggers: blaze advice|fight blazes|blaze fireballs] [topic: vanilla_blaze_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, blaze] Targeted fact: Blazes hover, shoot fire, and drop rods. Fire resistance, cover, snowballs in some contexts, and quick kills make fortress work less fatal.
- [triggers: ghast advice|deflect ghast fireball|ghast tears] [topic: vanilla_ghast_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, ghast] Targeted fact: Ghast fireballs can be hit back, and ghasts drop tears for brewing and End crystals. Fight from cover, not bridges made of nerves.
- [triggers: piglin advice|piglin brute advice|zombified piglin] [topic: vanilla_piglin_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, piglin] Targeted fact: Piglins respect worn gold but hate chest theft and gold mining; brutes ignore gold; zombified piglins punish group aggression.
- [triggers: hoglin advice|avoid hoglins|hoglin knockback] [topic: vanilla_hoglin_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, hoglin] Targeted fact: Hoglins hit hard and fear warped fungus. Use range, terrain, or repellent fungus rather than letting tusks count your ribs.
- [triggers: shulker advice|avoid levitation|end city shulkers] [topic: vanilla_shulker_advice] [tags: vanilla, mobs, shulker] Targeted fact: Shulker bullets levitate. Carry Feather Falling, blocks overhead, milk if useful, and avoid floating above void or open towers.

## Biome And Climate Knowledge
- [triggers: find desert|desert biome|need cactus sand] [topic: vanilla_find_desert] [tags: vanilla, biome, desert] Targeted fact: Deserts are hot, sandy biomes with cactus, dead bushes, villages, temples, and exposed sightlines. Bring water in spirit even if buckets do the work.
- [triggers: find jungle|jungle biome|need bamboo cocoa] [topic: vanilla_find_jungle] [tags: vanilla, biome, jungle] Targeted fact: Jungles hold cocoa, bamboo in some variants, melons, parrots, pandas, and thick leaves that hide both beauty and holes.
- [triggers: find swamp|swamp biome|slimes swamp] [topic: vanilla_find_swamp] [tags: vanilla, biome, swamp] Targeted fact: Swamps have shallow water, vines, clay, frogs or slimes depending on type and conditions, and a mood villagers dislike after dark.
- [triggers: find mangrove|mangrove swamp|mud roots frogs] [topic: vanilla_mangrove_swamp] [tags: vanilla, biome, mangrove] Targeted fact: Mangrove swamps provide mud, mangrove wood, roots, propagules, and frogs. Travel is slow unless you respect water paths.
- [triggers: find snowy biome|snow village|powder snow] [topic: vanilla_snowy_biomes] [tags: vanilla, biome, snow] Targeted fact: Snowy biomes offer snow, ice, spruce, igloos sometimes, and powder snow danger. Leather boots can save you from sinking.
- [triggers: find badlands|mesa biome|terracotta gold] [topic: vanilla_badlands_v3] [tags: vanilla, biome, badlands] Targeted fact: Badlands offer terracotta, exposed mineshafts, and extra gold opportunities. They look dry because they are not promising mercy.
- [triggers: find lush cave|azalea tree|lush caves] [topic: vanilla_lush_caves] [tags: vanilla, biome, cave] Targeted fact: Azalea trees can hint at lush caves below, where moss, clay, glow berries, axolotls, and drip leaves may appear.
- [triggers: find dripstone cave|dripstone|pointed dripstone] [topic: vanilla_dripstone_caves] [tags: vanilla, biome, cave] Targeted fact: Dripstone caves hold pointed dripstone and dripstone blocks. Stalactites and stalagmites make falling and falling-on things worse.
- [triggers: mushroom island|mushroom fields|mooshrooms] [topic: vanilla_mushroom_fields_v3] [tags: vanilla, biome, mushroom] Targeted fact: Mushroom fields are rare, have mycelium and mooshrooms, and are unusually peaceful for hostile spawning. Even the world needs a quiet patch.
- [triggers: pale garden|pale oak|creaking biome] [topic: vanilla_pale_garden_scoped] [tags: vanilla, biome, current] Targeted fact: If the server version includes pale gardens, treat them as eerie pale-oak places tied to Creaking danger; if not present, say the world may not know that wood yet.
- [triggers: weather effects|rain thunder snow|what weather does] [topic: vanilla_weather_basics] [tags: vanilla, weather] Targeted fact: Rain darkens mood, fills cauldrons in some cases, helps fishing, and affects undead burning; thunderstorms allow lightning and trident channeling danger.
- [triggers: lightning danger|thunderstorm safety|charged creeper] [topic: vanilla_lightning_danger] [tags: vanilla, weather, lightning] Targeted fact: Lightning can start fires, empower creepers, change mobs, and strike exposed places. Use lightning rods and stay out of tall lonely pride.
