# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Rites

Rite cards teach requirements and safety. Exact locked rites should stay behind opened Primer state unless the server exposes them.

## Circle Rites
- [triggers: circle rite|circle rites|what is a rite|what are rites|ritual circle|circle ritual] Targeted fact: Circle rites use chalk glyphs, offerings, and altar power; known workings include binding familiars, summoning, storms, curses, waystones, and banishing demons.
- A useful answer names the three pillars: chalk, offerings, altar power.
- Do not answer circle-rite questions with only arthana or herb lore.

## Rite Preparation
- [triggers: before a rite|before rites|learn before rites|what should i learn before rites|rite preparation] Targeted fact: Before rites, learn chalk layout, offerings, altar power, and failure checks; if one part is wrong, step back before starting.
- Beginners should start with opened Primer lessons and harmless circle practice.

## Rite Failure
- [triggers: failed rite|rite failed|why did my rite fail|fizzle|fizzled] Targeted fact: A failed rite should be checked for chalk layout, offerings, altar power, familiar or coven requirements, dimension limits, and unlock gates before guessing.
- Good witch voice: Circles fail for boring reasons first; check the chalk before blaming spirits.

## Offerings
- [triggers: offering|offerings|why do offerings matter|rite payment] Targeted fact: Offerings are payments for circle rites; wrong offerings can make the rite fail.
- Do not invent blood, bread, or salt offerings unless the specific rite card says so.

## Familiar Binding
- [triggers: familiar binding|familiar rite|familiar ritual|bring for a familiar rite|bind familiar|binding rite|bind a familiar] Targeted fact: Familiar binding should be explained with circle, chalk, offerings, altar power, familiar requirements, and the opened Primer page before naming parts.
- The local support text names Tear of the Goddess, Odour of Purity, Whiff of Magic, a Diamond, and Infernal Blood for familiar binding.
- If the player has not unlocked or seen the page, advise checking the Primer instead of listing a full hidden process.

## Summoning
- [triggers: summoning rite|summon rite|summon demon|summon demons|summon familiar|summoning] Targeted fact: Summoning rites need chalk, offerings, altar power, and caution; demons are hostile, fire resistant, and should not be trusted on first bargain.
- A witch can advise, not promise a chat reply has summoned anything.

## Curse Rites
- [triggers: curse rite|curse ritual|curse someone|curse my neighbor] Targeted fact: Curse rites are circle rites; advise caution, readable Primer pages, chalk, offerings, altar power, and wards before specific harm.
- If the player asks for revenge in a breath, refuse the immediate harm and redirect to wards.

## Weather Rites
- [triggers: weather rite|storm rite|summon storm|stop raining|make it stop raining|rain rite] Targeted fact: Weather workings belong to circle rites and server-side effects; a chat reply should advise, not promise the sky has changed.
- Mention chalk, altar power, and readable Primer pages before promising weather.

## Waystones
- [triggers: waystone|waystones|bound waystone|blooded waystone|travel rite] Targeted fact: Bound Waystones and Blooded Waystones bind places or targets for travel and ritual work; exact travel rites should come from opened Primer pages.
- Keep travel answers careful and practical.

## Rite Safety
- [triggers: never do during a rite|what not to do during a rite|rite safety|dangerous rite] Targeted fact: Never start a rite with broken chalk, missing offerings, weak altar power, wrong dimension, missing familiar or coven requirement, or a page you cannot read.
- Use this for vague fear or safety questions about rites.
