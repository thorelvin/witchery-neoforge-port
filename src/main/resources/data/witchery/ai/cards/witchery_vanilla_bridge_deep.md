# Witchery And Vanilla Bridge Deep Cards

These cards keep Witchery and vanilla mechanics separated while letting NPCs connect them naturally.

## System Boundaries
- [triggers: vanilla witch versus coven witch|witch mob coven witch|normal witch witchery] [topic: bridge_vanilla_witch_coven_witch] [tags: vanilla, witchery, boundary] Targeted fact: A vanilla witch mob is a hostile potion-throwing mob; a Witchery Coven Witch is a named practitioner, trader, teacher, and possible coven recruit.
- [triggers: brewing stand versus kettle|potion vs brew|vanilla brewing witchery brewing] [topic: bridge_brewing_stand_kettle] [tags: vanilla, witchery, brewing] Targeted fact: A brewing stand makes vanilla potions; the Witchery Kettle and cauldron systems make Witchery brews and workings. Similar smell, different law.
- [triggers: enchanting versus infusion|enchanting table infusion|vanilla enchant witchery power] [topic: bridge_enchanting_infusion] [tags: vanilla, witchery, enchanting] Targeted fact: Vanilla enchanting improves gear with lapis, levels, and bookshelves; Witchery infusions change the witch through rites, brews, and deeper systems.
- [triggers: nether versus torment|is torment nether|torment dimension nether] [topic: bridge_nether_torment] [tags: vanilla, witchery, dimensions] Targeted fact: The Nether is vanilla's fire dimension; Torment is Witchery punishment-space. Do not merge them just because both are bad travel.
- [triggers: end versus mirror world|is end mirror world|void mirror] [topic: bridge_end_mirror] [tags: vanilla, witchery, dimensions] Targeted fact: The End is vanilla dragon-and-void progression; Mirror World is Witchery reflection craft. Both are otherworlds, not the same door.
- [triggers: spirit world versus end|is spirit world end|dream world end] [topic: bridge_spirit_world_end] [tags: vanilla, witchery, dimensions] Targeted fact: The Spirit World belongs to dreams, sleeping brews, spirits, and nightmares; the End belongs to strongholds, dragon, outer islands, and elytra.
- [triggers: woodland mansion versus witch hut|mansion vanishing hut|illager house witch hut] [topic: bridge_mansion_vanishing_hut] [tags: vanilla, witchery, structures] Targeted fact: A woodland mansion is an illager structure found by exploration maps; a Vanishing Witch Hut is Witchery folklore worldgen that moves when unwatched.
- [triggers: ancient city versus spirit world|deep dark spirits|warden spirit] [topic: bridge_ancient_city_spirits] [tags: vanilla, witchery, structures] Targeted fact: Ancient cities and wardens are vanilla deep-dark danger; spirits may make a witch poetic about them, but do not claim they are Spirit World mechanics.
- [triggers: elytra versus flying monkey|wings or monkey|fly witchery vanilla] [topic: bridge_elytra_flying_monkey_v3] [tags: vanilla, witchery, travel] Targeted fact: Elytra are vanilla End ship wings; Flying Monkeys are Witchery mutation/travel companions. Both move bodies, but only one lives to judge you.
- [triggers: compass versus player compass|vanilla compass witchery compass|bound compass] [topic: bridge_compasses] [tags: vanilla, witchery, tools] Targeted fact: A vanilla compass points to world spawn unless lodestone-bound; Witchery compasses can have special scrying or player-binding behavior depending on item.
- [triggers: map versus crystal ball|cartographer map scrying|find player map] [topic: bridge_maps_scrying] [tags: vanilla, witchery, tools] Targeted fact: Maps and explorer maps are vanilla navigation; Crystal Ball and Waystone scrying are Witchery systems with their own foci, costs, and limits.
- [triggers: nether wart witchery|nether wart in witchery|same ingredient different station] [topic: bridge_shared_ingredients] [tags: vanilla, witchery, ingredients] Targeted fact: Some ingredients cross systems. Nether Wart can matter in vanilla brewing and Witchery work, but station and recipe decide what it becomes.
- [triggers: silver vanilla or witchery|silver not vanilla|witchery silver] [topic: bridge_silver_modded] [tags: vanilla, witchery, resources] Targeted fact: Silver is Witchery/modded content, not ordinary vanilla progression. Use it for Witchery hunters, werewolves, and vampires when the mod supplies it.
- [triggers: garlic vanilla or witchery|garlic modded|vampire garlic] [topic: bridge_garlic_modded] [tags: vanilla, witchery, resources] Targeted fact: Garlic is Witchery/modded content here, not vanilla food. Treat it as vampire counterplay and Witchery craft, not ordinary farming unless the mod adds crops.
- [triggers: wolfsbane vanilla or witchery|wolfsbane modded|wolf flower] [topic: bridge_wolfsbane_modded] [tags: vanilla, witchery, herbs] Targeted fact: Wolfsbane is a Witchery herb tied to werewolf work; vanilla Minecraft has wolves, but not wolfsbane by default.
- [triggers: mandrake vanilla or witchery|mandrake root|screaming plant] [topic: bridge_mandrake_modded] [tags: vanilla, witchery, herbs] Targeted fact: Mandrake is Witchery herb lore with screaming harvest behavior; do not treat it as vanilla crop advice.
- [triggers: rowan alder hawthorn vanilla|witch trees vanilla|modded trees] [topic: bridge_witch_trees_modded] [tags: vanilla, witchery, trees] Targeted fact: Rowan, Alder, and Hawthorn are Witchery trees from mutation work; vanilla trees follow ordinary sapling and biome rules.
- [triggers: altar versus beacon|altar power beacon|witchery altar vanilla beacon] [topic: bridge_altar_beacon] [tags: vanilla, witchery, power] Targeted fact: A vanilla beacon grants area effects from a mineral pyramid; a Witchery altar gathers magical power from nature and setup. Do not swap their rules.
- [triggers: potion effect versus curse|vanilla effect witchery curse|status curse] [topic: bridge_effects_curses] [tags: vanilla, witchery, effects] Targeted fact: Vanilla status effects come from potions, mobs, beacons, and items; Witchery curses and transformations follow separate server-authoritative systems.
- [triggers: vanilla book versus primer|recipe book primer|witchery guide] [topic: bridge_recipe_book_primer] [tags: vanilla, witchery, books] Targeted fact: The vanilla recipe book covers ordinary crafting unlocks; the Witches' Primer teaches Witchery progression, stations, rites, and modded discovery.
