# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Biomes, Weather, And Worldgen

These cards help NPCs answer broad geography questions without pretending to know hidden seed data.

## Biome Finding
- [triggers: find biome|where is biome|specific biome search|biome hunting] [topic: vanilla_find_biomes] [tags: vanilla, biome, exploration] Targeted fact: Without commands, find biomes by traveling, using maps, watching climate patterns, and following terrain clues. With cheats, `/locate biome` can help where available.
- [triggers: find jungle|jungle biome|bamboo jungle|need cocoa beans] [topic: vanilla_jungle] [tags: vanilla, biome, jungle] Targeted fact: Jungles hold cocoa beans, melons, bamboo in bamboo variants, parrots, pandas in some areas, and jungle temples. Thick leaves hide both treasure and inconvenience.
- [triggers: find swamp|swamp biome|slime swamp|mangrove swamp] [topic: vanilla_swamp] [tags: vanilla, biome, swamp] Targeted fact: Swamps and mangrove swamps bring mud, frogs, slime conditions, vines, and waterlogged travel. They are good places to lose boots and find strange uses.
- [triggers: find desert|desert biome|cactus sand temple|warm biome] [topic: vanilla_desert] [tags: vanilla, biome, desert] Targeted fact: Deserts offer sand, cactus, villages, wells, fossils, and pyramids, but little wood. Bring supplies before admiring all that honest emptiness.
- [triggers: find badlands|mesa biome|terracotta biome|mineshaft gold] [topic: vanilla_badlands] [tags: vanilla, biome, badlands] Targeted fact: Badlands are rich in terracotta colors and exposed mineshafts, with extra gold ore in some rulesets. The land looks baked because it means to be.
- [triggers: find mushroom island|mushroom fields|mooshroom island|safe biome] [topic: vanilla_mushroom_fields] [tags: vanilla, biome, mushroom] Targeted fact: Mushroom fields are rare and unusually safe from ordinary hostile spawning on the mycelium surface. Mooshrooms are cows that made a theological choice.
- [triggers: find cherry grove|cherry biome|pink trees|cherry wood] [topic: vanilla_cherry_grove] [tags: vanilla, biome, cherry] Targeted fact: Cherry groves appear in mountainous regions with pink petals and cherry wood. They are useful for beauty, bees, and bases that refuse to look grim.
- [triggers: find pale garden|pale garden biome|creaking biome|pale oak] [topic: vanilla_pale_garden] [tags: vanilla, biome, pale garden] Targeted fact: Pale gardens are eerie forest biomes tied to pale oak and Creaking danger in current Minecraft. Treat quiet woods as if they are listening.
- [triggers: find deep dark|deep dark biome|sculk biome|warden biome] [topic: vanilla_deep_dark] [tags: vanilla, biome, deep dark] Targeted fact: Deep dark biomes generate far underground with sculk sensors, shriekers, and Warden danger. Wool and silence are survival tools there.

## Weather And World Rules
- [triggers: rain mechanics|what rain does|rain minecraft|does rain matter] [topic: vanilla_rain] [tags: vanilla, weather] Targeted fact: Rain darkens mood and affects some mobs, fires, farmland feel, and surface visibility. It is ordinary weather unless magic or monsters make it otherwise.
- [triggers: thunderstorm mechanics|lightning minecraft|storm danger|charged creeper] [topic: vanilla_thunderstorm] [tags: vanilla, weather] Targeted fact: Thunderstorms bring lightning, darker skies, and rare transformations such as charged creepers or struck villagers. Sleep can skip storms when allowed.
- [triggers: lightning rod|protect house lightning|make lightning rod|copper rod] [topic: vanilla_lightning_rod] [tags: vanilla, weather, copper] Targeted fact: Lightning rods attract lightning strikes nearby and help protect flammable builds. Copper earns its keep when the sky gets ideas.
- [triggers: snow biome|powder snow|freeze damage|leather boots snow] [topic: vanilla_powder_snow] [tags: vanilla, weather, snow] Targeted fact: Powder snow can trap and freeze players; leather boots let you walk over it. Carry caution in snowy mountains.
- [triggers: world spawn|spawn chunks|where world starts|spawn point] [topic: vanilla_world_spawn] [tags: vanilla, worldgen, spawn] Targeted fact: World spawn is the world's default return point and compass target. Beds and respawn anchors set personal spawn under the right conditions.
- [triggers: world border|far lands|how far world|edge of world] [topic: vanilla_world_border] [tags: vanilla, worldgen, boundary] Targeted fact: Modern Minecraft worlds have vast limits and, on some servers, configured world borders. For normal play, distance becomes a logistics problem long before it becomes an edge.
