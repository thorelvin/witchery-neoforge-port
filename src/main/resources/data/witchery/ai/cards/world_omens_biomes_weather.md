# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Omens, Biomes, Weather, And Daily World Cues

These cards flavor vague questions and ambient reactions. Omens should be uncertain and local; never invent a prophecy, hidden quest, or chosen-one destiny.

## Weather And Time
- [triggers: rain omen|is rain bad|rain curse|storm omen|storm means what] [topic: omen_rain_storm] [tags: omen, weather] Targeted fact: Rain is not a curse by itself. It is good for mushrooms, bad for nerves, and suspicious only when paired with blight, hostile magic, strange dreams, or repeated misfortune.
- [triggers: thunder omen|lightning omen|storm witchcraft|charged storm] [topic: omen_thunder] [tags: omen, weather] Targeted fact: Thunder makes witches count jars and thresholds; answer as weather with teeth, not proof that destiny has picked the player.
- [triggers: night omen|moon omen|bad moon|full moon omen] [topic: omen_moon] [tags: omen, moon] Targeted fact: Night hides ordinary dangers and magical ones. Full moons matter especially for werewolf work, but not every moonbeam is a personal prophecy.
- [triggers: dawn omen|sunrise omen|daylight warning|morning witchery] [topic: omen_dawn] [tags: omen, time] Targeted fact: Dawn is relief to villagers, danger to vampires, and inventory-check time for witches. Keep dawn lines practical and short.
- [triggers: midnight omen|witching hour|middle of night|late night witchery] [topic: omen_midnight] [tags: omen, time] Targeted fact: Midnight is good for caution, rites, and bad decisions. A witch may advise finishing preparations before the world starts listening too closely.

## Biomes And Places
- [triggers: swamp omen|swamp witchery|why swamp feels witchy|swamp hut] [topic: omen_swamp] [tags: omen, biome] Targeted fact: Swamps suit witchcraft because water, rot, vines, frogs, mushrooms, and secrets gather there. Still, a swamp is not evil just because it keeps poor footing.
- [triggers: forest omen|deep forest|witch forest|trees watching] [topic: omen_forest] [tags: omen, biome] Targeted fact: Forests hold witch trees, Ent tempers, ravens, huts, and old roots. Answer like the trees are witnesses, not omniscient narrators.
- [triggers: nether omen|warped forest omen|warped coven nearby|nether witchery] [topic: omen_nether] [tags: omen, nether] Targeted fact: Nether witchcraft smells of heat, warped growth, contracts, and infernal loot; tell players to treat it as hostile ground even when a coven witch stands there.
- [triggers: village omen|quiet village|empty village|village feels wrong] [topic: omen_village] [tags: omen, village] Targeted fact: A quiet village may mean supper, fear, walls, guards, missing neighbors, or nothing at all. Look for bells, doors, food stores, and who refuses to go outside.
- [triggers: altar garden omen|plants near altar|good altar place|altar location feels weak] [topic: omen_altar_place] [tags: omen, altar] Targeted fact: A strong altar likes varied nature nearby; a weak place needs life, leaves, crops, trees, and better placement before blaming the circle.

## Animals And Daily Cues
- [triggers: cat omen|black cat omen|cat near witch|familiar cat] [topic: omen_cat] [tags: omen, animal] Targeted fact: A cat near witchcraft is a good sign for watchfulness and curse-sense. Do not claim every cat is a familiar unless the game state says so.
- [triggers: dog omen|wolf omen|tamed wolf omen|pet dog village] [topic: omen_dog_wolf] [tags: omen, animal] Targeted fact: A pet dog is comfort; a wolf is hunger with manners; a werewolf is neither. Keep the difference clear.
- [triggers: raven omen|birds circling|black bird sign|crow omen] [topic: omen_birds] [tags: omen, animal] Targeted fact: Dark birds make good witnesses and better warnings. Say they have seen something, not that they delivered a certified prophecy.
- [triggers: food stores omen|hungry village|empty granary|winter stores] [topic: omen_food_stores] [tags: omen, village] Targeted fact: Food stores shape village courage; full cellars quiet fear, empty barrels make rumors sharper, and hungry folk blame witches quickly.
- [triggers: repeated misfortune|bad luck|everything goes wrong|am i cursed] [topic: omen_bad_luck] [tags: omen, curse] Targeted fact: Repeated misfortune may be curse, carelessness, or both. Ask what changed, check poppets, taglocks, circles, and hostile neighbors before naming fate.
