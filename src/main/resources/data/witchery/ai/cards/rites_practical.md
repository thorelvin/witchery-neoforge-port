# Coven Witch Primer Cards: Practical Rites

These cards cover rite categories players ask about. Give requirements and danger shape, not a hidden complete ritual unless the opened Primer page supplies it.

## Rite Tools
- [triggers: circle talisman recipe|make circle talisman|craft circle talisman|circle talisman] Targeted fact: A Circle Talisman is crafted from Diamond, Gold Ingots, and Gold Nuggets; it stores or carries circle work after proper binding.
- [triggers: rite of binding|binding rite|bind circle talisman|bind waystone|bind familiar|bind spectral stone|bind fetish] Targeted fact: Rite of Binding changes by target: talismans, waystones, familiars, spectral creatures, and fetishes each ask for their own circle, offerings, and altar power.
- [triggers: before binding|binding requirements|why binding failed] Targeted fact: Binding failures usually mean wrong circle size, missing target, weak altar power, missing familiar or item, or an unopened lesson.

## Movement And Barriers
- [triggers: rite of transposition|transposition rite|teleport rite|move with waystone|move entity with taglock|transpose] Targeted fact: Rite of Transposition sends a location, entity, or ore to a stored target; entity transposition needs a Taglock Kit focus and 3000 altar power.
- [triggers: rite of protection|protection rite|barrier rite|force field rite|protect circle] Targeted fact: Rite of Protection makes barriers from Obsidian support and altar power; small barriers stop arrows and non-player entities, while stronger branches can block players.
- [triggers: rite of sanctity|sanctity rite|keep mobs out|hostile mobs circle] Targeted fact: Rite of Sanctity uses Feather, Redstone, and 500 altar power to keep hostile mobs from entering the circle, then drains altar power while sustained.
- Good witch voice: Barriers eat power the way fear eats sleep.

## Weather And Land
- [triggers: rite of sky's wrath|skys wrath|sky wrath|lightning rite|call lightning|strike lightning] Targeted fact: Rite of Sky's Wrath calls lightning strikes inside or outside a medium circle and spends altar power; do not promise lightning from idle words.
- [triggers: rite of the forest|forest rite|grow forest|make forest|tree rite] Targeted fact: Rite of the Forest creates a tree growth outside the chalk circle unless a Bound Waystone redirects it; it asks serious altar power.
- [triggers: rite of beastial call|beastial call|call animals|summon animals|gather animals] Targeted fact: Rite of Beastial Call draws animals from a wide area into the circle and may steal from nearby ranches; it is useful but rude near villages.

## Curse And Death Work
- [triggers: rite of remove curse|remove curse rite|remove a curse|cure curse|cleansing curse|waking nightmares curse] [topic: curse_cures] Targeted fact: Ordinary curse cures use a small Ritual circle, filled Taglock Kit, Breath of the Goddess, matching offerings, and 2000 altar power; vampire removal is separate medium coven work.
- [triggers: rite of prior incarnation|prior incarnation|recover items after death|lost death items|death point] Targeted fact: Rite of Prior Incarnation recalls items lost from death if cast in the same world within 16 blocks of where the player died.
- [triggers: rite of manifestation|manifestation rite|spirit manifestation] Targeted fact: Cast the Rite of Manifestation from a small Otherwhere circle in the Overworld with all six offerings and 5000 altar power. Every player within five blocks receives 150 seconds plus 25 seconds per nearby coven witch.
- [triggers: manifestation portal|dream self|spirit manifestation crossing] Targeted fact: While Manifestation lasts, an affected player in the Spirit World can cross a Spirit Portal on foot and appear at the same Overworld coordinates as a translucent dream self.
- Good witch voice: Death is terrible with receipts; keep the place of falling marked.

## Summoning
- [triggers: rite of summoning|summoning rite|summon demon|summon flame imp|summon spectral familiar|summon witch] Targeted fact: Rite of Summoning can call demons, flame imps, witches, spectral familiars, dismissed familiars, or worse; demon summoning needs an empty 7x7x4 space and the right altar power.
- [triggers: banish demon|banish demons|demon banishing|send demon back] Targeted fact: Demon banishing rites send demons in the circle back to their summoning point; use the opened Primer page before standing near angry fire.
- Do not promise the witch has summoned or banished anything with mere talk.
