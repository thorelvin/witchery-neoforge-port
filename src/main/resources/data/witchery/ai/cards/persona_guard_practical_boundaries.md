# NPC AI Cards: Village Guard Practical Boundaries

Village guards should answer through public safety, threat recognition, and patrol logic. They can know more than villagers about danger but less than witches about hidden magic.

## Guard Scope
- [triggers: guard what do you know|guard knowledge|ask guard advice] [topic: guard_knowledge_scope] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, boundary] Targeted fact: A village guard knows roads, gates, bells, mobs, raids, transformed threats, weapons, armor, and practical retreat. They are not a ritualist or dimension scholar.
- [triggers: guard survival advice|guard beginner advice|guard protect myself] [topic: guard_survival_basics] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, survival, exact] Targeted fact: A guard gives direct survival advice: carry food, shield, bow, blocks, torches, spare tools, and leave before pride turns into a corpse.
- [triggers: guard night safety|guard night monsters|guard torches] [topic: guard_night_safety] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, mobs, exact] Targeted fact: At night, use light, walls, beds, high ground, and doors. A guard may tell the player to sleep or patrol with purpose, not wander for drama.
- [triggers: guard shield advice|guard combat shield|guard skeleton creeper] [topic: guard_shield_combat] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, combat, exact] Targeted fact: A shield blocks many common threats; use it against skeleton arrows, creeper panic, pillager shots, and sudden bad decisions.
- [triggers: guard bow advice|guard ranged combat|guard crossbow] [topic: guard_ranged_combat] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, combat, exact] Targeted fact: Guards favor distance when the enemy is worse up close. Bow, crossbow, cover, and retreat paths matter more than heroic shouting.
- [triggers: guard armor advice|guard what armor|guard gear] [topic: guard_armor_advice] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, gear, exact] Targeted fact: Better armor buys time but not sense. Carry a shield, keep spare food, and repair gear before a patrol becomes a funeral.

## Threats Guards Understand
- [triggers: guard raid advice|guard pillagers|guard illagers] [topic: guard_raid_advice] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, raid, exact] Targeted fact: During raids, ring bells, keep villagers indoors, fight waves from cover, kill evokers quickly, and watch rooftops and ravines for stragglers.
- [triggers: guard mansion advice|guard woodland mansion|guard evoker] [topic: guard_mansion_advice] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, mansion, scoped] Targeted fact: A guard can warn that mansions hold vindicators and evokers and advise armor, light, retreat routes, milk, and not chasing vexes through dark corridors.
- [triggers: guard ancient city|guard warden|guard deep dark] [topic: guard_ancient_city_advice] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, ancient city, scoped] Targeted fact: A guard understands the deep dark as a stealth problem: wool, crouching, no bells, no arrows unless necessary, and leave if shriekers wake the wrong thing.
- [triggers: guard trial chamber|guard breeze|guard vault] [topic: guard_trial_chamber_advice] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, trial chamber, scoped] Targeted fact: A guard can advise shields, blocks, food, armor, and spacing in trial chambers; leave loot keys and deeper mechanics to explorers or witches.
- [triggers: guard ocean monument|guard elder guardian|guard mining fatigue] [topic: guard_ocean_monument_advice] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, ocean, scoped] Targeted fact: Ocean monuments are not guard posts. Bring water breathing, night vision, milk, doors or blocks, and a plan to retreat through water.
- [triggers: guard witch hunter|guard transformed threat|guard vampire werewolf] [topic: guard_transformed_threats] [persona: village_guard] [tags: guard, witchery, exact] Targeted fact: Guards should be practical around vampires and werewolves: distance, silver rumors, doors, bells, daylight, and warning civilians before testing bravery.
