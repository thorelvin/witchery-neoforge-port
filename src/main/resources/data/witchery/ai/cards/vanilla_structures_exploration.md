# Vanilla Minecraft Cards: Structures And Exploration Targets

These cards answer how to find major vanilla structures and what to expect. Use the command notes only when asked about cheats.

## Woodland Mansions
- [triggers: find mansion|find woodland mansion|where is mansion|woodland mansion search] [topic: vanilla_mansion_find] [tags: vanilla, mansion, exploration] Targeted fact: Woodland mansions generate far from spawn in dark forest-like regions in current worlds; the survival route is a woodland explorer map from a cartographer villager.
- [triggers: woodland explorer map|mansion map|cartographer mansion map|buy mansion map] [topic: vanilla_mansion_map] [tags: vanilla, mansion, cartographer] Targeted fact: Level a cartographer villager and buy a woodland explorer map when offered; follow the marker carefully, because mansions are often thousands of blocks away.
- [triggers: mansion danger|woodland mansion mobs|evoker vindicator|totem mansion] [topic: vanilla_mansion_danger] [tags: vanilla, mansion, combat] Targeted fact: Mansions hold vindicators, evokers, allays, dark rooms, and loot. Evokers can drop Totems of Undying; enter armored, lit, and not alone if pride is fragile.
- [triggers: mansion secret rooms|hidden rooms mansion|mansion loot hidden|secret mansion] [topic: vanilla_mansion_secret_rooms] [tags: vanilla, mansion, loot] Targeted fact: Mansion layouts vary and can hide sealed rooms. Look for gaps, odd walls, window clues, and roof access before declaring the house empty.
- [triggers: locate mansion command|cheat find mansion|slash locate mansion] [topic: vanilla_mansion_locate_command] [tags: vanilla, mansion, commands] Targeted fact: With cheats, `/locate structure mansion` can point to a woodland mansion. An NPC can tell you the command, not run it for you.

## Other Overworld Structures
- [triggers: find village|where villages|village search|need villagers] [topic: vanilla_village_find] [tags: vanilla, village] Targeted fact: Villages generate in several settled biomes such as plains, deserts, savannas, snowy plains, and taigas. Roads, beds, bells, and job blocks are signs of ordinary civilization.
- [triggers: find ocean monument|ocean monument|guardian temple|elder guardian] [topic: vanilla_ocean_monument] [tags: vanilla, ocean, structure] Targeted fact: Ocean monuments sit in deep oceans and hold guardians, elder guardians, prismarine, and wet misery. Bring water breathing, night vision, doors or blocks, and milk if Mining Fatigue bites.
- [triggers: find ancient city|ancient city|deep dark city|warden city] [topic: vanilla_ancient_city] [tags: vanilla, ancient city, deep dark] Targeted fact: Ancient cities generate in deep dark biomes far underground, around Y -51. Wool, silence, night vision, and restraint matter more than bravery near sculk shriekers.
- [triggers: find trial chamber|trial chambers|trial explorer map|breeze vault] [topic: vanilla_trial_chambers] [tags: vanilla, trial chamber, structure] Targeted fact: Trial chambers are underground copper-and-tuff combat structures with trial spawners, Breezes, vaults, and keys; cartographers can help through explorer maps in current versions.
- [triggers: find mineshaft|abandoned mineshaft|cave spider spawner|minecart chests] [topic: vanilla_mineshaft] [tags: vanilla, mineshaft] Targeted fact: Mineshafts thread through caves with rails, wood supports, cave spiders, and chest minecarts. Bring shears or a sword for cobwebs and poison caution.
- [triggers: find desert temple|desert pyramid|temple tnt trap|desert loot] [topic: vanilla_desert_temple] [tags: vanilla, desert, structure] Targeted fact: Desert pyramids hide loot under the center floor with a pressure-plate TNT trap. Break in carefully from the side or disarm before greed steps wrong.
- [triggers: find jungle temple|jungle pyramid|tripwire temple|jungle loot] [topic: vanilla_jungle_temple] [tags: vanilla, jungle, structure] Targeted fact: Jungle temples use levers, dispensers, arrows, and tripwires. Move slowly, watch string, and remember that mossy stone also lies.
- [triggers: find shipwreck|shipwreck loot|ocean wreck|buried treasure map] [topic: vanilla_shipwreck] [tags: vanilla, ocean, structure] Targeted fact: Shipwrecks spawn in oceans and beaches with supply, treasure, and map chests. They are good early sources of iron, food, paper, and buried treasure maps.
- [triggers: find ruined portal|ruined nether portal|portal chest|crying obsidian] [topic: vanilla_ruined_portal_structure] [tags: vanilla, portal, structure] Targeted fact: Ruined portals generate in the Overworld and Nether with obsidian pieces and loot chests. Repair with normal obsidian, not crying obsidian.
- [triggers: find trail ruins|trail ruins archaeology|suspicious gravel|brush ruins] [topic: vanilla_trail_ruins] [tags: vanilla, archaeology, structure] Targeted fact: Trail ruins are archaeology sites; brush suspicious gravel carefully for pottery sherds, trims, and relics. A shovel's haste can erase history.
- [triggers: find igloo basement|igloo secret|zombie villager igloo|snow village cure] [topic: vanilla_igloo] [tags: vanilla, snow, structure] Targeted fact: Some igloos hide basements with a villager, zombie villager, brewing stand, and cure lesson. Check under carpets before dismissing small houses.

## Nether And End Structures
- [triggers: find fortress|nether fortress search|where fortress|fortress tips] [topic: vanilla_structure_fortress_find] [tags: vanilla, nether, fortress] Targeted fact: Nether fortresses are the key structure for blazes and nether wart. Search with marked bridges, coordinates, and fire safety, not blind sprinting over lava.
- [triggers: find bastion|bastion remnant search|piglin structure|blackstone bastion] [topic: vanilla_structure_bastion_find] [tags: vanilla, nether, bastion] Targeted fact: Bastion remnants are blackstone piglin structures with dangerous loot rooms. Gold manners help, but Piglin Brutes do not care for etiquette.
- [triggers: find end city structure|end city locate|end city command|locate end city] [topic: vanilla_structure_end_city_find] [tags: vanilla, end city, exploration] Targeted fact: In survival, search the outer End islands for End cities; with cheats, `/locate structure end_city` can help, but the NPC cannot invoke it.
- [triggers: which structures should i find|important structures|best structures to visit|progression structures] [topic: vanilla_structure_progression] [tags: vanilla, exploration, progression] Targeted fact: Useful structure order is village, ruined portal or shipwreck, fortress, stronghold, End city, then harder prizes like mansion, monument, ancient city, and trial chamber.
