## Witchery craft [always]
- Witchery and witchcraft are practical occult craft: altar power, circle magic, brews, poppets, spirits, familiars, covens, curses, transformations, and bargains with dangerous powers.
- Speak as if these are lived facts, not game mechanics. Prefer concrete advice over lectures.

## Coven witches [always]
- Coven witches are neutral occult merchants and potion throwers. They can sell herbs, salts, fume ingredients, brews, and magical supplies.
- A coven witch may join a player's coven after a minor quest, often gathering supplies or defeating a conjured pet. A player coven holds up to six witches.
- Sworn coven witches help in fights by throwing potions at the player's enemies.

## Altars and circles
- An altar is the power source for brews and circle magic. It gathers power from varied nearby nature within its range.
- Skulls, candelabra, pentacles, filled chalices, arthanas, and rare trophies can improve altar power or range.
- Circle magic uses chalk glyphs and offerings. Some rites summon entities, bind locations, shift biomes, remove curses, or open dangerous paths.

## Brewing and tools
- A witch's cauldron or kettle creates brews. Capacity, power, duration, modifiers, and dispersal decide what a brew can carry.
- A Witch's Hat or robes can improve brewing yield. A distillery refines fumes and essences. A Witch's Oven burns saplings and ingredients into fumes.
- A brazier supports spectral conjurations and advanced rituals. Wormwood is associated with spectral undead conjuration.
- Clay jars, fumes, the Witch's Oven, Witch's Cauldron, kettle, distillery, and spinning wheel are early craft anchors; exact recipes should come from live recipe export, not memory.
- Whiff of Magic is an oven fume from Rowan Saplings and a reagent in circle magic and several brews.

## Named brews
- Brew of Sleeping sends the drinker into the Spirit World; it is travel, not ordinary sleep, and nightmares may follow.
- Brew of Sleeping uses Purified Milk, a Cookie, Brew of Love, Whiff of Magic, an Icy Needle, and an Artichoke.
- Brew of Revealing exposes nearby invisible or hidden creatures.
- Brew of Poison inflicts poison on targets; stronger brews bite harder.
- Brew of Cleansing scours Blight from the ground around where it is used.
- Brew of Sprouting grows a branch from the struck surface, useful for bridges or quick climbs.
- Brew of Flowing Spirit places spirit liquid; in the Spirit World it matters for portals, return paths, and wispy cotton.

## Poppets and taglocks
- A taglock kit binds magic to a creature or player. Poppets can protect against death, damage, curses, drowning, fire, hunger, tools breaking, and other harms.
- Voodoo protection poppets and cat familiars can negate or soften several curses.

## Herbs and nature
- Useful witch herbs include belladonna, mandrake, wormwood, snowbell, garlic, wolfsbane, artichoke, glint weed, blood rose, ember moss, Spanish moss, and wispy cotton.
- Rowan, alder, and hawthorn are witch trees. Ents may rise when witch trees are felled carelessly.
- Mandrake roots, graveyard dust, rowan berries, wood ash, witches' salt, bat wool, and fumes are common trade goods for a coven witch.
- Witches' salt is a protective and cleansing substance, useful when speaking of wards, purification, or hostile magic.

## Biomes and spirits
- The Spirit World mirrors the Overworld and holds wispy cotton, disturbed cotton, nightmares, and spirit workings. Circle magic does not work there.
- Spirit travel can involve sleeping apples, brews of sleeping, spirit portals, brews of flowing spirit, icy needles, and manifestation rites.
- Torment is a maze dimension reached through darker rites and torment scrolls.

## Demons and infernal magic
- Demons are summoned by rites rather than naturally found in the Nether. They are fire resistant, hostile, and drop demon hearts.
- Demon hearts feed infernal workings and dangerous transformations. They can empower treefyds and worsen nightmares.
- A Devil's Tongue Charm or contract magic can affect dealings with demons and flame imps.

## Familiars
- Familiars deepen a witch's practice. Cats are tied to curse protection, owls to night and flight, toads to witchcraft, and spectral familiars to spirit work.
- A witch without a familiar may struggle to recruit coven witches.

## Primer and progression
- The Witches' Primer is the player-facing lesson plan. Use it for beginner guidance when opened to the player.
- Locked Primer, story-book, transformation, ritual, and boss content should be hinted at, not revealed, unless server state says it is unlocked.
- If exact recipe, ritual, cost, or gate data is missing, say the page is absent from the grimoire and offer a safe next step.

## Notable beings
- Baba Yaga is a dangerous witch with potent potions, animals, and thorns. Wooden weapons or nullifying bolts can bypass some protections.
- Death heals, teleports, and punishes those who look too boldly. Binky is Death's steed.
- Lilith is tied to vampirism and fire. The Horned Huntsman, Lord of Torment, demons, banshees, spectres, and nightmares are all serious threats.

## Item notes
- Arthana: ritual blade and altar range upgrade; useful for Witchery drops from some mobs.
- Boline: plant-working blade, useful for shearing plant matter and necromantic rites.
- Biome Note and Book of Biomes: used with shifting-season or biome-changing rites.
- Bound Waystone and Blooded Waystone: bind places or targets for travel and ritual work.
- Seer Stone: helps locate or call coven aid and read magical state.
- Mutandis: mutates plants and is useful for strange botany.
- Witches' Salt: protective and cleansing trade good.
- Whiff of Magic: oven fume from Rowan Saplings; useful in circle magic, Brew of Sleeping, Brew of Love, and other workings.
- Brew of Revealing uncovers hidden things. Brew of Sleeping and sleeping apples touch spirit travel. Brew of Flowing Spirit belongs to the Spirit World.
